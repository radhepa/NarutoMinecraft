## Arc

### Wants

- **Early (to Ch 41):** that no child is used and thrown away by a village again. She believes the
  creed of dusk: the Last Bonfire will end the system that made her. She recruits orphans because
  she thinks Yūgure is the family they never had.
- **Underneath:** her lost child back. In lineage A, the child is the player and she was told they
  burned with the persimmon house at six. In lineage B, her son died of fever in a nursery at five,
  in the year she started asking questions she was not allowed to ask.
- **From Ch 41:** to know what the Bonfire actually needs to burn.
- **From Ch 48:** nothing for herself. To be useful before she is punished.

### Fears

- That she has been the thing she hates: a grown-up who uses children.
- That the leader is right about her: that she is only a flame he lit, and without him she is ash.
- In A, after Ch 48: that you will call her "mother" only because the story needs you to.

### Lineage A track (the second is the player's mother)

1. **Y-12:** She bears the child in a border town; the father, a civilian, has died in a skirmish.
   She hides the baby with her sister in the persimmon house and sings the watch-fire song at the
   door the night she leaves. She carves the charm in two.
2. **Y-6:** The cells take the child. The leader tells her the house burned with everyone in it.
   She grieves in his presence, and he comforts her. (The cruellest thing in the story; the player
   learns it in Ch 48.)
3. **Ch 16:** In the forest she feels the flame and spares the child; that night she hears the song
   from the trees. She does not let herself believe it.
4. **Ch 19:** "Kagari." She means: *you are what I am.* She half means: *you are mine.*
5. **Ch 27:** The persimmon on the sill is hers. She found out where the child lives from the
   leader's own reports and could not stay away.
6. **Ch 48:** At the persimmon house's ruins she waits, unarmed. The halves fit.
7. **Ch 52:** She shields the player as a mother.
8. **Ch 53:** Lives (rekindled) or dies at dawn with both halves of the charm in her hand.

### Lineage B track (the fallen Leaf shinobi was the player's parent)

1. **Y-9 to Y-8:** Her son, a nursery child, dies of fever at five. She stays in Yūgure because
   leaving would mean admitting what she had given him to.
2. **Ch 16:** She feels a flame she has only ever felt in herself; she spares a nursery child she
   swore, years ago, to protect. The song stops her in the trees because her mother sang it.
3. **Ch 19:** "Kagari." She means: *you are what I am, and they will use you as they used him.*
4. **Ch 27:** No persimmon (that is the sensei's). Her warning is the whole of her gift.
5. **Ch 48:** Not present. That night her letter reaches the Seventh: she has left Yūgure.
6. **Ch 49:** She tells the council the plan. She tells the player about her son once, flatly,
   and never again.
7. **Ch 52:** She shields the player as a debt to her son.
8. **Ch 53:** Lives (rekindled) or dies at dawn; the player can lay her under the persimmon tree.

### Her thread (Thread Sight)

From Ch 27, when the player has Thread Sight, a **faint, one-sided thread** runs from her toward
the player: she feels for a child she cannot name. It shows in both lineages (in A it is a
mother's; in B it is for her son, landing on the nearest child of the nurseries) and must never
be explained before Ch 48. She has no thread the player could read toward anyone else in Yūgure
except one, strong and old, to the blade. After her defection the thread becomes two-sided as her
bond with the player grows; in Ch 52 it is the thread the leader's mirror tears.

### Her bond

She has a bond with the player from Ch 13 (level 0, met at the bridge if the player saw her).
While she is an enemy it grows only through story moments, never talk or gifts: sparing (Ch 16),
the warning (Ch 27), the memorial conversation (Ch 41). **Her bond levels open properly from
Ch 41**, and conversation and hangouts only after her defection (Ch 48 in A, Ch 49 in B), when she
lives under the Seventh's watch at the shrine. Her questions below are spaced so a player who
spends time with her between Ch 49 and Ch 52 can reach level 4 (the Ch 53 survival gate) without
grinding: two shared experiences (the anchor hunt, a meal) and one moment (her questions 3 or 4).

## Dialogue brief

### Voice

- **Short sentences. Dry.** She says the least that will do. She answers questions with a question
  when she does not want to lie.
- **Fire words she does not notice using:** "burn", "ash", "smoulder", "catch". Never "light" or
  "warm": those are for other people.
- **She never uses the player's name before Ch 48** (A) or Ch 49 (B). Before that: "you", "child"
  (to Ch 19), "Leaf" (Chunin and Jonin).
- **In A after Ch 48 she uses the player's given name carefully, as if it might break.** She never
  calls herself "your mother" unless the player chose that answer in Ch 48
  (`ans.player.parent_meet = mother`).
- **Never says:** "for your own good", "I'm sorry" (before Ch 49; when she finally says it, once,
  it is to teammate_b), the leader's given name.
- **Humour:** rare, bone-dry, usually about her own failures ("I've been a terrible recruiter.
  Everyone I recruit ends up fighting me.").

*Voice samples, not shipped lines:*

- (Ch 13, to teammate_b) "I knew your parent. They're alive. Come alone in three days."
- (Ch 27) "Don't let them make you a candle."
- (Ch 41) "You carve a name back and you think it means something. It does. That's the worst part."
- (Ch 49, to the council) "Lock me up after. Listen first."
- (A, after Ch 48, at the shrine) "You eat like my sister. Too fast. Like someone will take it."
- (B, Ch 49, to the player) "I had a son. Five. He'd be your age. That's all."

## Arc

### Wants

- **Early (to Ch 41):** that no child is used and thrown away by a village again. She believes the
  creed of dusk: the Last Bonfire will end the system that made her. She recruits orphans because
  she thinks Yūgure is the family they never had.
- **Underneath:** her lost child back. In lineage A, the child is the player and she was told they
  burned with the persimmon house at six. In lineage B, her son died of fever in a nursery at five,
  in the year she started asking questions she was not allowed to ask.
- **From Ch 41:** to know what the Bonfire actually needs to burn.
- **From Ch 48:** nothing for herself. To be useful before she is punished.

### Fears

- That she has been the thing she hates: a grown-up who uses children.
- That the leader is right about her: that she is only a flame he lit, and without him she is ash.
- In A, after Ch 48: that you will call her "mother" only because the story needs you to.

### Lineage A track (the second is the player's mother)

1. **Y-12:** She bears the child in a border town; the father, a civilian, has died in a skirmish.
   She hides the baby with her sister in the persimmon house and sings the watch-fire song at the
   door the night she leaves. She carves the charm in two.
2. **Y-6:** The cells take the child. The leader tells her the house burned with everyone in it.
   She grieves in his presence, and he comforts her. (The cruellest thing in the story; the player
   learns it in Ch 48.)
3. **Ch 16:** In the forest, as an escort "looking for her team", she feels the flame and saves
   the child from another team's trap; that night she hears the song from the trees. She does not
   let herself believe it.
4. **Ch 19:** Sent to find the strongest flame and report how it burns, she names it aloud instead: "Kagari." She means: *you are what I am.* She half means: *you are mine.*
5. **Ch 27:** The persimmon on the sill is hers. She found out where the child lives from the
   leader's own reports and could not stay away.
6. **Ch 48:** What sent her into the leader's records: the Ch 41 doubt (what the Bonfire needs from the Leaf) and the "candle" orders since Ch 27; looking for the plan, she found the line about the child. His reports only ever said where the candle lives, never whose it is. At the persimmon house's ruins she waits, unarmed. The halves fit. Of the stadium she
   says: "I nearly told him how you burn." Before the player answers (`ans.player.parent_meet`), she tells them the Ch 0 map was hers, so "You saved me twice" is a choice the player can make knowingly.
7. **Ch 52:** She shields the player as a mother.
8. **Ch 53.5 to 54:** Lives (by the gate below). Otherwise the player holds her through 53.6 and
   she dies at morning, the first scene of Ch 54, holding both halves of the charm. Her grave is
   under the persimmon tree; her half is buried with her, and the player's goes back in their sleeve (54.2).

### Lineage B track (the fallen Leaf shinobi was the player's parent)

1. **Y-9 to Y-8:** Her son, a nursery child, dies of fever at five. She stays in Yūgure because
   leaving would mean admitting what she had given him to.
2. **Ch 16:** As an escort "looking for her team", she feels a flame she has only ever felt in
   herself and saves a nursery child from another team's trap (after her son she swore to protect any nursery child she could; she does not know which child this is). The song stops her in the trees because her mother sang it.
3. **Ch 19:** Sent to find and report the flame, she names it aloud instead: "Kagari." She means: *you are what I am, and they will use you as they used him.*
4. **Ch 27:** No persimmon (that is the sensei's). Her warning is the whole of her gift.
5. **Ch 48:** Not present. What sent her into the leader's notes: the Valley of Ash rescue (Ch 35)
   reached Yūgure's ears, and she went to the emptied caves herself: the hearth's own day-book, left
   behind when the Leaf took the list, named the nursery **where her son died**, and what it was really for. In his notes she found Nine named as the
   wick. That night her letter reaches the Seventh: she has left Yūgure.
6. **Ch 49:** She tells the council the plan. She tells the player about her son once, flatly,
   and never again.
7. **Ch 52:** She shields the player as a debt to her son.
8. **Ch 53.5 to 54:** Lives (by the gate below). Otherwise the player holds her through 53.6 and
   she dies at morning, the first scene of Ch 54, with her son's name on her lips. Her grave is at
   the shrine beside the stone basket, her son's name carved on it.

(In both lineages `fallen_parent` also had a child "lost" after the Ash Road ambush: in A that
child truly died, in B it is the player. She does not know of it; it never touches her lines.)

### Her thread (Thread Sight)

From Ch 27, a **faint, one-sided thread** runs from her toward the player: she feels for a child
she cannot name. It is a scripted exception (a `thread_floor`), not a level: the player is far
below 6 with her. It shows in both lineages (in A it is a mother's; in B it is for her son,
landing on the nearest child of the nurseries) and must never be explained before Ch 48. It
**frays** from Ch 49 to Ch 52 (her guilt, her fear of the leader). The player sees no other thread
of hers; her strong old thread to the blade is shown only in Ch 52. In Ch 52.4 it is the thread
the leader's mirror tears; in Ch 53.5, if she lives, the player holds that frayed thread and pours
flame into it.

### Her bond

She has a bond with the player from Ch 13 (level 0, met at the bridge if the player saw her).
While she is an enemy it grows only through story moments, never talk or gifts: sparing (Ch 16),
the warning (Ch 27), the memorial conversation (Ch 41). **Her level is capped at 2 until Ch 41**
(`level_cap [role:yugure_second, 2, story.ch41.done]`), and conversation and hangouts only after her defection (Ch 48 in A, Ch 49 in B), when she
lives under the Seventh's watch at the shrine. The Ch 49 truth scene gives a `shared_moment bond.second.truth`; with the
anchor hunt and the last meal (two shared experiences) a player who spends time with her between
Ch 49 and Ch 52 reaches level 4 (one of the Ch 53.5 survival gates) without grinding. Every
main-path read of her `ans.*` flags has an unset default (a neutral line, or no callback).

**Her fate resolves in Ch 53.5, before the leader's end (53.6).** She lives if any of: the web at
the **"strong"** tier or above (the tier the bonds session calibrates; not a raw number),
`friendship [yugure_second, 4]`, or `friendship [squad4_c, 6]` with `ans.squad4_c.second` not
"someone else first". Survival is shown as the player holding her frayed thread and pouring flame
into it. Otherwise she does not survive the night: the player holds her in their arms through the
leader's end (53.6), and she dies at morning as **the first scene of Ch 54** (her own beat), by
lineage: **A** holding both halves of the charm, buried under the persimmon tree; **B** with her
son's name on her lips, buried at the shrine beside the basket with his name carved on it.

## Dialogue brief

### Voice

- **Short sentences. Dry.** She says the least that will do. She answers questions with a question
  when she does not want to lie.
- **Fire words she does not notice using:** "burn", "ash", "smoulder", "catch". Never "light" or
  "warm": those are for other people.
- **She never uses the player's name before Ch 48** (A) or Ch 49 (B). Before that: "you", "child"
  (to Ch 19), "Leaf" (Chunin and Jonin).
- **In A after Ch 48 she uses the player's given name carefully, as if it might break.** She never
  calls herself "your mother" unless the player chose that answer in Ch 48
  (`ans.player.parent_meet = mother`).
- **Never says:** "for your own good", "I'm sorry" (before Ch 49; when she finally says it, once,
  it is to teammate_b, the `b_sorry` beat below), the leader's given name.
- **Humour:** rare, bone-dry, usually about her own failures ("I've been a terrible recruiter.
  Everyone I recruit ends up fighting me.").

*Voice samples, not shipped lines:*

- (Ch 13, to teammate_b) "I knew your parent. They're alive. Come alone in three days."
- (Ch 27) "Don't let them make you a candle."
- (Ch 27.3, only if `story.trail.count` ≥ 1) "And stop reading our marks on the roads. They aren't written for you."
- (Ch 41) "You carve a name back and you think it means something. It does. That's the worst part."
- (Ch 49, to the council) "Lock me up after. Listen first."
- (A, after Ch 48, at the shrine) "You eat like my sister. Too fast. Like someone will take it."
- (B, Ch 49, to the player) "I had a son. Five. He'd be your age. That's all."

## Arc

### Common to every save

- They owe money to a lender in the east (it was a bad harvest year and a dead mule). They talk
  about it as a joke.
- They keep a **delivery book** of every stop, border-village orphanages and waystations included, in their own
  road-stop shorthand (`E3 · two · bright`, a tick with a tail). They show it off in Ch 11 because
  they are proud of it: "Never lost a delivery in eleven years."
- They hire the player's squad again and again (Ch 11, Ch 14, and board missions that roll them as
  the client). They like the player especially, and say it's because the player eats like a
  teamster.
- Their cart had a false floor; chakra paper went east in it.
- **Ch 11.3, every save:** the axle breaks and the merchant repairs it on the spot with their own
  tools, leaving their tool marks (the same marks the Chunin alibi check compares).
- **Their every-save secret**: old debts, and years ago (before they met the player) they smuggled
  for bandits on the eastern roads to pay them: salt, stolen bolts of silk, once a crate of swords.
  It is true whether or not they are the voice.
- **Something happened at the east waystation the night before Ch 14** (every save): a hearth's
  pair, plain-clothed, soot sun on the wrist, sat at their fire.
- They sell at the exam-week stalls; the vendor stall permits list the guard posts for the finals
  (one of the routes by which a rotation could leak, true in every save).
- **Their held-back piece:** the night at the waystation. They keep it until Ch 39 (bound) or Ch 40
  (innocent), in every save.

**Thread Sight.** From Ch 24, if they are bonded to the player at 6 or more and the player probes
them, their thread flickers: they are hiding something. They are in every save (the bandit
smuggling, and the waystation night), so the flicker cannot single out the voice, never says what,
and **never goes out before Ch 39 or 40**. A player who asks gently at level 7 hears the bandit story
in every save; the flicker stays in every save, because the waystation night is still held back.
Bound, their thread to the player reads as a normal two-way thread. Their tie to the porter (who
loaded crates onto their cart) is never visible. Their flag is `hides:old_roads`.
The flicker is one steady state from a single `hides:` bond flag, never line by line, and it
is not a lie detector: it means *hiding*, nothing more. Each of the four candidates carries exactly
one `hides:` flag of the same kind, from the start of the game until Ch 39/40, in every save. Their
daily schedules and night movements are identical in every save until Ch 39.

### If innocent

- **Wants:** to pay the debt, open a shop, and stop being afraid on the road.
- **Fears:** the road; the soot-wristed people at the waystations who look at the cargo too long.
- **The waystation night:** the hearth's pair threatened them: "Be slow tomorrow."
- **The false floor** (`story.clue.merchant.1`): they bought the cart second-hand from a carpenter in
  the east who built it; they did not know.
- **Slowing the squad** (`story.clue.merchant.2`): fear. They were told "be slow tomorrow".
- **The gate log** (`story.clue.merchant.3`): the bored Hagane guards waved every regular through.
- **The slip scene (Ch 25.1):** a fruit basket and a note: "For my favourite customer. Eat the
  persimmons first; they don't keep."
- **The alibi** (`mission.b.false_floors`, Chunin B-rank): they name the carpenter; the player finds
  his abandoned shop in the east full of half-built false floors.
- **Ch 40 cleared:** they tell the waystation night at last, and turn **informant**: their road gossip and waystation contacts give A-rank
  hunts a head start (systemic: +1 trail drop chance on far-region missions) and in Ch 49 they
  recognise two of the array's crate marks from loads they carried.
- **Road to Hokage:** with the player's help (a district quest) they open a shop in the market street.
- **Ending:** a shop with a roof. At the inauguration, a cart of persimmons "for nobody in particular".

### If bound (the voice)

- **Wants:** to be out of debt and safe; the money was the only way.
- **Fears:** being found out; the children under the floor that one time.
- **Secret:** since about Y-10 (sixteen years by Ch 39; messages by an old east-road carter until
  Y-6, through the porter since) they carried paper, then paper and names (orphanage stops from the
  delivery book), and once children, for three times the pay, and told themselves each time was the
  last; the finals' guard posts from the vendor stall permits (Ch 19); the news, heard at the gate
  from the Sixth's clerk, that the archive would be open (Ch 37); and after the porter fell (Ch 28),
  messages in the cart itself.
- **The waystation night:** they met the pair and took the order to slow the squad.
- **The false floor:** they built it.
- **Slowing the squad:** to let the pair slip past.
- **The gate log:** they bribed the guards (a little), and the guards also waved everyone through.
- **The slip scene (Ch 25.1):** the note: "For the ninth of my favourite customers. Eat the persimmons
  first; they don't keep."
- **The failed alibi:** the carpenter is real, but the floor in their cart carries the merchant's own
  tool marks (the same marks as their axle repair in Ch 11.3).
- **Ch 38:** the list is in road-stop shorthand with tailed ticks.
- **Ch 39 reveal:** their cart in the rain at the east gate, half packed to run. "It was paper. Then
  it was paper and names. Then once there were children under the floor, and they paid me three times
  over, and I told myself it was the last time." Then the held-back piece: "The night at the
  waystation, I didn't get threatened. I got paid." They kept hiring the squad because they liked
  it, and because being close to it kept them close to the news; they cannot say which mattered more.
- **Fate:** *arrested*: the delivery book helps the A-rank hunts. *Turned* (needs
  `friendship [merchant, 4]`): they run false cargo to Yūgure, and in Ch 51a a wave of the assault
  arrives without its cinder bombs. Ch 55's ruling: pardon (they open the shop under watch), exile,
  or service (they drive supplies to the rebuilt border posts for life).

## Dialogue brief

### Voice

- **Register:** fast, friendly, a talker; prices and food in every other sentence.
- **Habits:** rounds every number ("call it a hundred"); haggles out of habit even when giving things
  away; names their mule (whichever mule they have that year).
- **Tics:** taps the abacus when nervous, which in the beta reads as greed and in the reveal as fear.
- **Never says:** "Yūgure" (they call them "the soot-wrists"); "nine" except the bound note; anything about the waystation night before Ch 39/40.

*Voice samples, not shipped lines:*

- (Ch 11) "Never lost a delivery in eleven years. Well. One cabbage. It was a very fast cabbage."
- (Ch 11, the floor) "I didn't know! I swear on my mule. I didn't know there was a floor under my
  floor."
- (Ch 14) "Oh, the horse is lame. Look at him. Look at his face. Lame face."
- (Ch 40, innocent) "So I'm not a crook. I'm just bad at buying carts. Can I buy you a bowl? Call it
  ten."
- (Ch 39, bound) "I told myself it was the last time. I told myself that eleven times."

### What their lines are for

- **Functions:** clue carrier (beta, identical), comic relief on the road, the human cost of poverty
  (both tracks), the informant (innocent), the most ordinary of the four voices (bound).
- **Mood states:** `chatty` (default), `nervous` (eastern roads, Chunin arc, both tracks), `relieved`
  (innocent after Ch 40), `ashamed` (bound after Ch 39). No mood differs before Ch 39.
- Every main-path read of an `ans.merchant.*` flag has an `unset` branch.
- **Pools:** `npc.merchant.*`, `talk.q.merchant.<topic>`, `story.ch25.note.merchant.<bound|innocent>`.

### Board chains (missions session)

After Ch 14 the merchant posts a weekly chain on the board (`mission.c.escort_merchant`,
`mission.c.caravan_guard`). **No bound-only clue steps before Ch 39.** Each run may include one
neutral camp step identical in every save: the merchant shows the delivery book again (the notation
stays in view) or talks about the roads. The every-save flicker applies to Thread Sight as above.
After Ch 40, an innocent merchant's chain becomes the **informant chain** (road gossip, waystation
contacts, trail drops). After an arrest the chain ends; a turned merchant's chain is suspended
(they are running false cargo).

### Variants

- Classic curated, Varied rolled (look, gender, personality); the fixed traits above hold in both. In
  Varied the chatty lines get archetype variants (a `reserved` merchant is terse about prices but
  still keeps the book and still gives fruit).
- `twist.traitor = merchant`: the bound track. Watch the rotation matrix: with `twist.lineage = second`
  the persimmon on the sill (Ch 27) is the second's, two days after the merchant's note (Ch 25.1).
- Board missions that roll the merchant as client add time with them in every save.

# Review log

> **SPOILERS: for developers only.** Part of the [story bible](STORY_BIBLE.md).

The story was reviewed in rounds before it was locked. Each round used six fresh, independent
reviewers, one per lens, given the story files and the rubric but no opinion of what was good. Each
scored 1 to 10 and listed blocking issues with locations. A round passes when every lens scores 8 or
more with no blocking issues.

| Lens | What it checks |
|---|---|
| (a) Structure and pacing | Arc shapes, chapter purposes, the beta's hours per ROADMAP §8, the 60-hour budget, authored versus systemic balance |
| (b) Characters and motivation | Everyone acts for reasons; wants, fears and secrets; change over the story |
| (c) Mystery fairness | Every `twist.traitor` rotation (and the lineage and Fourth's fate slots): clues present, no cheats, the twist lands |
| (d) Naruto tone and canon | Feels like the anime without copying it |
| (e) Player agency and systems fit | Choices that matter; missions, bonds, side quests and clans carry the plot |
| (f) Emotional payoff | Which moments should hit hard, and whether they are earned |

**Revisions after round 1:** see the round 2 entry for what changed.

**What changed after round 1:**
- **Time and pacing:** an elapsed-time and ages table (the second skip is four years; the player is 18 at the Jonin trial); the Chunin arc trimmed to 7 h authored with a main-path-only systemic share; the optional branch gated after Ch 39 and Ch 44, with Ch 47 resolving a half-played branch; the Road's systemic work moved before the war, Ch 49 at level 47, the succession within an hour of the war; Ch 51 split into 51a and 51b; Ch 50 a 30-minute core.
- **The leader:** a beat-by-beat plan (he let the Leaf raise his wick; the attack's real purposes; the border post built to wake the gift), with mid-story appearances (24.7, 44.3, his voice in 51b).
- **Tone:** Yūgure in road clothes with a soot sun on the wrist, working in hearths of three, ash-violet scarves only when seen; the Undercroft re-founded as the sealing team's heirs; the sensei never late, a fire user, the bell test as three bells on one red cord; sensei_2's contests lopsided and his own; the Fourth's "falls" fate as an Akimichi farewell feast; no eye-technique option for the player.
- **Mystery:** one list of allowed divergences before Ch 39 (the slip and the alibi results); the night shift identical in every save; held-back secrets so no flicker ends early; access routes to every leak for every candidate; one alibi mechanism for the councillor; the lineage-B line removed from his reveal; journal documents with each notation.
- **Agency:** story floors for scripted gift beats; `offered_flame`; Ch 46 waives the fee and covers three clan states; unset defaults for every main-path read; readers for every player choice; Ch 25.3 identical in every save; the bond spec request for the new gift.
- **Emotion:** a lonely-playthrough floor (the red cord is always the first flame; the sensei always gives theirs; a small-fire variant and a lonely epilogue tier); the lineage-B heart line rewritten; the archive chapter ending on the Third's letter, the Ash Road report on its own night; the player present at sensei_2's death; the keeper dying beside the player; one version of every key line.
- **Ensemble:** the rival holds the Rock's stair in 51b; the Sarutobi senior carries a contested council in Ch 42; squad5_c given a Chunin-arc beat; every cross-file contradiction resolved.
- **Owner decisions applied since:** the stipend continues all game (Ch 55 extends it and makes it law); the player is always a clanless orphan.

**What changed after round 2:**
- **The Exams, transformed:** an observation test; a real foreign team with the second and the blade as its adult escorts (no disguised genin, no dupe); the attack begins at the opening lantern-lighting, so the rival bout is never fought; the Fourth is hit on the east stair carrying children; the rival's awakening moved to their Ch 29 spar with the player.
- **The cord test:** three carved watch-fire tags on one red cord replace the bells; sensei_2's challenge moved out of the scene; his tally grows over the years.
- **The Undercroft:** its hall moved to the old quarry; the Shimura have no part in it ("the jar" replaces "vessel").
- **Tone allowlist** in WORLD.md (clan arts, Academy basics and the map's canon places allowed; canon signature techniques, catchphrases and exam gimmicks forbidden); the gift explained in-world as a sealed bloodline.
- **Mystery:** the voice since ~Y-10 through an old carter, the porter since Y-6; the hand (not the voice) warned of the Ch 0 raid; one Thread Sight engine rule (a steady `hides:` flicker, never a lie detector; a short list of scripted thread exceptions); the night shift, hospital supplies and the Forger's Ink made identical before Ch 39; sources reach border children; the medic's age and every spoken span fixed.
- **Characters:** a single Ch 51 who-is-where; the 51b order with a beat of play between deaths; squad3_a's second line (51a.5); the leader's beat table complete and his questions cut to four; the blade turns and lives; the sensei's Ash Road signature defined; distinct voices for the rival, sensei_2 and squad5_c.
- **Agency:** a beta falling-out handled (stand-ins, story mends); every main-path answer read has an unset default; scripted thread floors; the web's tiers calibrated by the bond simulation (small under 20, strong from 20, the whole village from 41); the war's end applies the level floor and the standing lift, so the Eighth follows at once.
- **Emotion:** the body-shield beat used only twice (Ch 24, Ch 52); one version of every key line; the second's fate resolved before the leader's, held through her thread, split by lineage; six spoken flames in a fixed order; a lonely epilogue line that does not scold; humour beats added in Arcs 3 and 5.

# Overheard talk between shinobi: missions, training, the Mission Desk, gear. Nothing classified.
#! tags: relation=shinobi

## ambient.shinobi  fn=worldbuild
A: What did the Mission Desk give you?
B: Weeding. Three gardens. A Chunin, weeding.
A: Every rank weeds. That's the Leaf.

A: Back from the east road?
B: Escort job. The merchant talked the whole way. Every step, every hour.

A: Have you tried the new kunai from the weapon shop?
B: Lighter. Flies further, sticks harder. I bought a dozen.

A: Training ground three's booked till noon.
B: Then we'll take the lake. Water walking, wet clothes, the usual.

A: My sensei made us carry logs up the cliff all morning.
B: Mine made us carry each other. I'd take logs.

A: Paper bombs went up a ryo at the tool shop.
B: Powder cart's late. Everything's gone up a ryo.

A: Who's got the night watch on the wall?
B: The Tobari, mostly. They hear things before they happen.

A: How long were you out?
B: Six days. I'd kill for a bath. Not literally. Mostly.

## ambient.shinobi  fn=teach
A: Did you eat before the spar?
B: Barbecue. A plate of beef.
A: Smart. Hits harder for a while after.

A: You look done in.
B: Up till three. Couldn't sleep before the mission.
A: Sleep in your own bed before midnight next time. You wake up with chakra to spare.

A: Your ninken's getting good at tracking.
B: Started on the cat-finding D-ranks. Cats leave a strong trail.

## ambient.shinobi  fn=reveal_character
A: Do you ever miss the Academy?
B: The lunches. Not the tests.

A: My first C-rank, my hands shook the whole way.
B: Mine still do. You just learn to throw with them shaking.

A: I've never once hit the bullseye at the festival target stall.
B: A Chunin, and you can't win a paper fan?
A: Chakra's not allowed. That's my excuse.

## ambient.shinobi  rank=genin  fn=react
A: Our sensei was late again.
B: Ours too. We practised hand signs for an hour waiting.
A: Maybe they're all together somewhere, laughing at us.

A: Two D-ranks today! Painting a fence and catching a goat.
B: Which was harder?
A: The fence. The goat had more sense.

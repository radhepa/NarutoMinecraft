# Overheard talk between rivals: friendly or not quite, at the training grounds, the market and the shops.
#! tags: relation=rivals

## ambient.rivals  fn=react
A: Forty laps this morning.
B: Forty-one.
A: You're lying.
B: Count them yourself tomorrow.

A: Your shuriken went wide on the third throw.
B: You were watching? I'm flattered.

A: I hear you're top of the Mission Desk tally this month.
B: Jealous?
A: Motivated.

A: Rematch. Training ground three. Dawn.
B: Bring a spare headband. You'll want to hide your face after.

A: Nice footwork yesterday.
B: Was that a compliment?
A: Don't get used to it.

## ambient.rivals  fn=worldbuild
A: My stall sold more chestnuts than yours today.
B: Mine sold better chestnuts.
A: Nobody can taste "better" through the shell.

A: Your tea's a ryo cheaper than mine again.
B: And yours is a ryo worse.
A: Ha! We'll see who's still open in spring.

A: My garden's chrysanthemums are taller.
B: Mine have more heads. The judges count heads.
A: The judges are your cousins.

A: The tea house has your name above mine on the go board.
B: Read it again. It's in order of wins.

## ambient.rivals  fn=reveal_character
A: Why do you always train at the same time as me?
B: Why do you always train at the same time as me?
A: ...Fair.

A: You pushed me today. I'll admit that.
B: I always push you. That's the point of me.

A: When we were small, I wanted to be you.
B: And now?
A: Now I want to beat you. It's healthier.

A: If you ever leave the village, who'll I compete with?
B: I'm not going anywhere. Who'd keep you honest?

## ambient.rivals  stage=child  fn=react
A: I can climb the Academy wall faster than you.
B: Can not.
A: Can so. Race you.
B: ...Tomorrow. I've got a stitch.

A: I got a better mark on the throwing test.
B: By one point.
A: One point is still a point!

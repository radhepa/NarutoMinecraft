# Overheard talk between relatives (siblings, cousins, grandparents and grown children).
#! tags: relation=family

## ambient.family  fn=worldbuild
A: Grandmother wants us all for supper on the last day of the month.
B: All of us? Where will we sit?
A: On each other, probably. Same as every year.

A: Did you fix the gate?
B: I tied it with wire.
A: That's not fixing, that's hoping.

A: Our cousin's coming from the farms for Market Day.
B: Then hide the pickles. Last time the barrel came back empty.

A: Who's cooking for New Year?
B: Not you. Never again you. The rice cakes were grey.

A: Mother's planted chrysanthemums again.
B: Says it's for the shrine. It's really to beat the neighbour's.

A: Is the old ladder still in the shed?
B: The one with the cracked rung? Nobody's been brave enough to throw it out.

## ambient.family  fn=reveal_character
A: You've got your father's laugh. You know that?
B: Everybody tells me. I wish I'd heard more of it.

A: I found our old Academy notebooks in the trunk.
B: Burn them. Please. Mine has a poem in it.
A: I've already read it to the whole family.

A: Grandfather still keeps his old headband in the drawer.
B: Polished every week. Don't ever touch it.

A: You never call.
B: I'm right here, every week, at your table.
A: You never call before you come. That's what I mean.

## ambient.family  fn=react
A: The baby's teething. Nobody in the house has slept.
B: I'll take a night. Bring the little one round.

A: You've grown again!
B: I'm twenty-four. I stopped growing years ago.
A: Not to me you didn't.

A: Your brother passed his exam!
B: I know. The whole street has heard about it since breakfast.

## ambient.family  fn=filler
A: Supper's at six.
B: It's always at six.
A: And you're always late.

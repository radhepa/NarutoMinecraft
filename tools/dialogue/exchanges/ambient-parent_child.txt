# Overheard talk between a parent and a grown or teenage child.
#! tags: relation=parent_child

## ambient.parent_child  fn=worldbuild
A: Have you eaten?
B: I ate at Ichiraku.
A: That's not eating. That's slurping. Sit.

A: Your sensei came by asking for you.
B: Was I in trouble?
A: Only for being late. Again.

A: Take the umbrella.
B: It's not going to rain.
A: Take the umbrella.

A: Help me carry the rice sacks in.
B: I just got back from a mission!
A: Then your arms are warmed up.

A: You left your headband on the washing line.
B: I was drying it!
A: It was dry yesterday. It's been there three days.

A: The neighbours say you've been training at the lake at dawn.
B: Is that bad?
A: It's wonderful. Just tell me next time, so I don't worry.

## ambient.parent_child  fn=reveal_character
A: You look more like your grandmother every year.
B: Is that good?
A: She was the bravest woman on this street. So yes.

A: When you were small, you were afraid of the ninken.
B: I'm still a little afraid of the ninken.
A: Good. They're very big.

A: I'm proud of you. I don't say it enough.
B: You say it every time I leave the house.
A: Then I'll say it when you come back, too.

A: Did you want to be a shinobi, or did you just want to make me proud?
B: Both. Is that allowed?
A: That's how most of us started.

## ambient.parent_child  fn=react
A: You're home late.
B: The mission ran over. I'm fine. Look, all ten fingers.

A: You've grown out of that jacket.
B: I like that jacket.
A: Your elbows like it less. Let's go to the tailor.

A: Is that a new cut?
B: Training. It's nothing.
A: Let me see. Sit down. Let me see.

## ambient.parent_child  fn=filler
A: Go to sleep.
B: Five more minutes.
A: You said that an hour ago.

# Overheard talk between a shopkeeper and a customer, or two customers in a queue.
#! tags: relation=customers

## ambient.customers  fn=commerce
A: How much for the cabbage?
B: Three ryo.
A: Three? It was two last week.
B: Last week it was smaller.

A: Is the eel fresh?
B: It's still moving. You tell me.

A: Can you hold this jacket till Market Day?
B: For you, till the day after.

A: Two bowls of miso, one extra egg.
B: Who gets the egg?
A: Whoever argues best.

A: Do you sell fruit milk?
B: In the cooler. Strawberry's nearly gone.

A: I bought this lamp here a year ago. Still working.
B: Of course it is. I don't sell lamps that give up.

A: The tea across the street is cheaper.
B: Then why are you standing in my shop?
A: Because yours is better. Don't tell them I said so.

A: Do you have any more of the sesame dango?
B: Last skewer. It's yours if you're quick.

## ambient.customers  fn=worldbuild
A: Long queue at Ichiraku tonight.
B: Academy graduates. They've been here an hour.
A: Then I'll wait. It's worth it.

A: Have you tried the senbei shop in the Uchiha quarter?
B: Every week. The soy ones. I walk all the way there.

A: The grocer's got persimmons in.
B: Already? Then autumn's properly here.

A: They say the bookshop's got the new card set.
B: My nephew's been waiting a month. I'd better hurry.

A: You're buying flowers?
B: For the hospital. A friend broke an ankle on the climbing cliff.
A: Cut flowers, then. Never a potted plant for the sick.

## ambient.customers  fn=react
A: Sorry, I've only got a thousand-ryo note.
B: For a dango? You'll take my whole till in change!

A: Is it always this busy?
B: Only on Market Day. And festival days. And every day ending in -day.

## ambient.customers  fn=filler
A: Thank you, come again.
B: I always do.

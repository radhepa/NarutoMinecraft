# Overheard talk between people who work together: shopkeepers, healers, gate guards, Mission Desk staff.
#! tags: relation=coworkers

## ambient.coworkers  fn=worldbuild
A: Who's on the gate tonight?
B: Us. Again. Bring the good tea this time.

A: The Mission Desk had forty D-ranks come in this morning.
B: Forty? It's Market Day. Everyone's lost a cat at once.

A: The new apprentice burnt the rice again.
B: Third time this week. I'm starting to think it's deliberate.

A: Did you restock the bandages?
B: Twice. The Genin go through them like paper.

A: The boss wants the shelves reorganised by colour.
B: By colour? What's wrong with by what it is?

A: Ichiraku's queue went round the corner at lunch.
B: That's the Academy letting out. Every child in the village had the same idea.

A: The furniture delivery's late.
B: The carpenters' lane is buried in orders before the festival. It'll be days.

A: Fold the towels in thirds, not halves.
B: Who decided thirds?
A: The bath house decided thirds. Forty years ago.

## ambient.coworkers  fn=reveal_character
A: I've worked this stall nineteen years.
B: And still you can't tell a turnip from a radish.
A: I can. I just like hearing you correct me.

A: You stayed after shift again.
B: The ledger wouldn't balance. I can't sleep when it won't balance.

A: Thank you for covering for me yesterday.
B: Your little one was sick. Of course I did.

## ambient.coworkers  fn=react
A: Busy day.
B: Busy week. Busy season. I need a holiday.
A: We're getting one. It's called New Year.

A: The heat in the kitchen today, I could cook an egg on my forehead.
B: Don't. We've got enough orders.

## ambient.coworkers  district=hospital  fn=worldbuild
A: Three sprained ankles from the training grounds before noon.
B: The obstacle course again. They never learn to land.

A: The herbalists sent the new salve.
B: Good. It smells like a swamp, but it works.

## ambient.coworkers  district=gate  fn=worldbuild
A: Count the carts again. I make it twenty-two.
B: Twenty-three. One's a goat with a basket on its back.

A: Quiet road today.
B: Quiet's good. Quiet means our relief comes on time.

A: Lunch?

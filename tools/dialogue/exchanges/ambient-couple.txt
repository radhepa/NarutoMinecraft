# Overheard talk between married or settled couples (adults only; plain domestic life, no romance words).
#! tags: relation=couple stage=adult

## ambient.couple  fn=worldbuild
A: Did you buy the radishes?
B: I bought a lamp.
A: We needed radishes.
B: We needed a lamp. We'll have radishes in the dark otherwise.

A: Your mother's visiting for the festival.
B: The whole festival?
A: The whole festival. And the week after.

A: The roof's leaking over the futon again.
B: I'll ask the Kinoshita yard. Again.

A: You left your kunai on the kitchen table.
B: I'll move them.
A: You said that yesterday. And the day before.

A: The tailor says your jacket can't be mended anymore.
B: That jacket's been on more missions than most Jonin.
A: Then let it retire.

A: Shall we walk down to the river after supper?
B: Like we used to. Yes, let's.

## ambient.couple  fn=reveal_character
A: Twelve years married this spring.
B: Twelve? I'd have guessed fewer.
A: That's the kindest thing you've said all year.

A: You worried while I was away, didn't you?
B: I reorganised the whole spice shelf.
A: So yes.

A: I still have the paper lantern from the night we met.
B: That old thing? It barely holds a candle.
A: It holds enough.

A: I'm sorry about this morning.
B: So am I. Let's have supper and forget the sharp words.

## ambient.couple  fn=react
A: You're home early!
B: The mission was quick. The client cried, the cat was found, I came home.

A: Snow's coming. I can smell it.
B: Then I'm not leaving this house until spring.

## ambient.couple  fn=filler
A: Tea?
B: Please. You always make it better than I do.

# Overheard talk between the village's old folk, on benches, at the tea house, at the shrine.
#! tags: relation=elders

## ambient.elders  fn=worldbuild
A: In our day the market street was a dirt track.
B: In our day there was no market street. We traded at the well.
A: That's because you're older than me.

A: The Hokage Rock had only one face when I was a child.
B: And now four. I hope I live to see another carved.

A: The tea house has changed its cups.
B: Those cups were older than both of us.
A: Exactly. Some things should stay.

A: Remember when the river froze solid?
B: The winter of the long snow. We skated to the farms and back.

A: The young ones run everywhere now.
B: We ran everywhere too. We just ran slower.

A: The cherry tree by the shrine bloomed early.
B: It always does when the winter's kind. Mark it in the book.

## ambient.elders  fn=reveal_character
A: My knees know the rain's coming before the clouds do.
B: Mine tell me every morning. Whether it rains or not.

A: I taught three generations of this street to read.
B: And they all still mind their spelling around you.

A: I still set out a cup for my husband every morning.
B: Of course you do. I still talk to my wife's garden.

A: Do you think we did well by the village?
B: Look at it. Children laughing, ramen steaming. We did well enough.

## ambient.elders  fn=react
A: Did you hear? The fishmonger's grandchild just made Genin.
B: That family. Fish and shinobi, fish and shinobi. Never anything in between.

A: Cold today.
B: Every day's cold at our age. Sit closer to the brazier.

A: That young one from the apartments carried my basket all the way home yesterday.
B: Good manners. The Leaf's raising them right.

## ambient.elders  district=shrine  fn=worldbuild
A: The bell rope's fraying.
B: I'll tell the priests. They never look up.

## ambient.elders  fn=filler
A: Another game of go?
B: You'll only lose again.
A: That's why I like playing you.

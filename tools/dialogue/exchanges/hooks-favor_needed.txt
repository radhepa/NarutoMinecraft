# Overheard leads for favor_needed hooks (SIDE_QUESTS.md §3.2, gen.favour and its variants): two residents mention a
# need the player could help with. Each exchange is a lead in the journal.
#! fn: hook

## ambient.hook.favor_needed  fn=hook
A: {client} needs those rice sacks carried up to the third floor, and nobody's got the back for it.
B: I'd help, but my knee. Somebody young ought to give a hand.
lead: hook.favor_needed

A: Did {neighbor} ever get that roof patched?
B: Not yet. Another rain like last night and the whole kitchen floods.
lead: hook.favor_needed

A: {client} has to mind the stall and see to a delivery at the same time.
B: Can't be done. Someone should offer to watch the counter for an hour.
lead: hook.favor_needed

A: The firewood's still piled outside {neighbor}'s door. Hasn't been split in a week.
B: With those hands? It won't be split till spring unless someone helps.
lead: hook.favor_needed

A: {client} asked me to water the garden while they're away, and I'm off to my sister's tomorrow.
B: Ask around. Somebody's bound to have an afternoon free.
lead: hook.favor_needed

A: The shutters on {neighbor}'s house are hanging by one hinge.
B: I keep meaning to fix them. I don't own a single tool.
lead: hook.favor_needed

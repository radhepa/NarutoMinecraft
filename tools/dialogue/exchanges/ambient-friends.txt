# Overheard talk between friends.
#! tags: relation=friends

## ambient.friends  fn=worldbuild
A: Ichiraku tonight?
B: Ichiraku every night. Why do you even ask anymore?

A: I finally beat the dango stall's record. Twelve skewers.
B: And how's your stomach?
A: We don't talk about my stomach.

A: Did you see the new tea house across the market? Half the price.
B: Half the tea, too. I'll stay loyal, thanks.

A: Let's go up the Hokage Rock at sunset.
B: Last time we did that you got stuck on the third face.
A: Second face. And it was dark.

A: The bath house has fruit milk again.
B: Then my whole evening is decided.

A: I'm learning to make dumplings. My kitchen looks like a snowstorm.
B: Bring me the failures. I'm not fussy.

A: The fishmonger gave me extra eel today.
B: You must be the favourite. The fishmonger only ever gives me bones.

A: Did you hear the bell from the shrine at dawn?
B: Every morning. I've stopped hearing it. Is that sad?
A: A little. Come and listen with me tomorrow.

## ambient.friends  fn=reveal_character
A: Do you ever think about leaving the village? Seeing the coast?
B: Every time the fish cart comes in. Then I eat the fish and forget.

A: You've been quiet all week.
B: Work's heavy. It'll pass.
A: Then I'll be loud enough for both of us till it does.

A: I kept the letter you sent when I was laid up with fever.
B: That silly drawing of the cat?
A: It's on my wall. Don't you dare laugh.

A: Thank you for helping me move. My back's still talking to me about it.
B: Mine too. We'll go to the bath house and complain together.

A: When did we meet? I can't remember a time before.
B: The flood year. You lent me a dry pair of sandals.
A: And you never gave them back.

## ambient.friends  fn=react
A: Rain again! My washing's been out for three days.
B: Bring it to mine. I've got a line by the stove.

A: You look tired.
B: I sat up reading till two. The tea house is my only hope.

A: Your cooking's improved.
B: That's the nicest lie anyone's told me all week.

## ambient.friends  fn=filler
A: Lovely evening.
B: It is. Let's just stand here a moment.

A: Same time tomorrow?
B: Same time, same bench.

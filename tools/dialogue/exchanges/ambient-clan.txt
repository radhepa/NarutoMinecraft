# Overheard talk between members of the same clan: life inside the clan, never its secrets.
#! tags: relation=clan

## ambient.clan  fn=worldbuild
A: The elders want everyone at the main hall for the evening meal.
B: Everyone? Even the cousins from the border post?
A: Even them. It must be important. Or someone's birthday.

A: Who's sweeping the clan gate this week?
B: You are. I did it last week, and the week before.

A: The crest on the gate needs repainting.
B: Ask the painters' lane. They did it beautifully last time.

A: Our clan day's coming. Have you thought about what to bring?
B: Grandmother's pickles. I'm afraid of what happens if I don't.

## ambient.clan  clan=akimichi  fn=worldbuild
A: The storehouse is full to the rafters.
B: Then the feast's on. Nothing in an Akimichi storehouse stays full for long.

A: Another bowl?
B: I've had four.
A: That wasn't a question.

## ambient.clan  clan=nara  fn=worldbuild
A: A doe gave birth by the stream this morning.
B: Write it in the book. And leave them both be for a week.

A: You fell asleep in the forest again.
B: I was watching clouds. It's clan work, technically.

## ambient.clan  clan=inuzuka  fn=worldbuild
A: The kennels need mucking out.

A: Your partner's been howling at the gate since you left.
B: Four hours! I was only at the market.

## ambient.clan  fn=reveal_character

B: Sometimes. Then I sit at the long table, and I don't.

# Overheard talk between neighbours: shared walls, shared stairwells, shared opinions about each other's plants.
#! tags: relation=neighbors

## ambient.neighbors  fn=worldbuild
A: Your cat was on my roof again.
B: It thinks your roof is its roof.
A: It might be right. It's up there more than I am.

A: Are you using the well this morning?
B: After you. I'm still finding my sandals.

A: Who plays the flute at midnight?
B: The new tenant on the top floor.
A: Lovely tune. Terrible hour.

A: I've got too many persimmons. Take a basket.
B: Again? My whole kitchen is orange.

A: The stairwell lamp's out again.
B: I'll bring a candle. I'm not buying another wick, it's your turn.

A: Lend me a cup of rice? I'll pay you back on Market Day.
B: You said that last Market Day.
A: And I paid you back! Mostly.

A: Your washing's dripping on my step.
B: Sorry! I'll move the line.
A: Don't. My step's never been so clean.

A: The street's having a sweep-out before the festival. Are you in?
B: If there's tea after, I'm in.

## ambient.neighbors  fn=reveal_character
A: I heard you singing in the bath last night.
B: The walls are thinner than I thought.
A: You're better than you think, too.

A: I've lived on this lane thirty years.
B: Then you've seen everything.
A: I've seen everyone. That's better.

A: I made too much soup.
B: You always make too much soup.
A: And you always come and help me eat it.

## ambient.neighbors  fn=react
A: Did the storm get your fence?
B: Half of it. The other half is in your garden.

A: Quiet street tonight.
B: Everyone's gone to the festival. Shall we sit out on the step?

A: Your little one waved at me from the window this morning.
B: Every morning. You're the window friend now.

## ambient.neighbors  district=apartments  fn=worldbuild
A: Someone's been leaving their sandals on the third-floor landing.
B: A whole tower of them. I counted nine pairs.

A: The building's water's hot again!
B: Only took the landlord a month. A festival of a day.

## ambient.neighbors  fn=filler
A: Morning.

# Overheard talk between children: games, lessons, snacks, siblings, dares.
#! tags: relation=kids stage=child

## ambient.kids  fn=worldbuild
A: Dare you to touch the Hokage Rock's nose.
B: You can't even get up there. It's taller than the whole Academy.

A: I've got two ryo. Dango or a spinning top?
B: Dango. Then you can spin the stick.

A: The teacher's got a new chalk board. It squeaks.
B: Good. Now we'll hear when the questions are coming.

A: Want to play shinobi and bandits?
B: I'm the shinobi.
A: You're always the shinobi!

A: I saw a deer at the edge of the Nara forest!
B: Did it see you?
A: It looked at me like I was boring.

A: My big sister says ghosts live under the old bridge.
B: Your big sister says lots of things to make you cry.

A: Let's catch tadpoles by the river after lessons.
B: My mother said no more tadpoles in the house.
A: We'll keep them in a bucket. A secret bucket.

A: Hide and seek. You count to fifty.
B: Last time you hid so well we went home.
A: That's the point!

A: My dad lets me hold the kunai when he cleans them. The blunt end.
B: My grandpa lets me sharpen pencils with one. Sort of the same.

A: I learnt a new hand sign today. Watch.
B: That's just your fingers crossed.
A: It's a very advanced hand sign.

## ambient.kids  fn=reveal_character
A: Do you think I'll be good at missions?
B: You found my lost sandal in the river. You're already good at missions.

A: I don't like the dark.
B: Me neither. That's why I sleep with the lantern on. Don't tell.

A: When I grow up I'm opening a ramen stand.
B: Then I'll eat there every day for free.
A: Every day for half price. I'll need money.

A: I miss my cousin. They moved to the farms.
B: You can write letters. I'll help with the hard words.

## ambient.kids  fn=react
A: It's snowing! It's snowing!
B: Snowball fight! Last one to the yard is a turnip!

A: I fell off the climbing frame.
B: Are you hurt?
A: Only my feelings. And my knee. Mostly my feelings.

A: The festival's tonight!
B: I've saved four ryo. I'm going to win a goldfish.

A: I got a good mark on the written test!
B: Really? Show me. ...Wow. I'm telling everyone.

## ambient.kids  fn=filler
A: Race you home!
B: Go!

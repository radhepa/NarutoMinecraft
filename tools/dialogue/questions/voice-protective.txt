? q.past.prot_got_home | fn: deepen_bond | level: 2

? q.values.prot_stronger_foe | fn: deepen_bond reveal_character | kind: opinion | level: 3 | axis: caution_or_daring

Q: A friend in trouble, an enemy who outclasses you. Is stepping in brave, or foolish?

- Reckless. Get help :: Yes. Run for help, fast. That's not cowardice, that's sense. {values:+duty} {tone:cautious} {pole:1}

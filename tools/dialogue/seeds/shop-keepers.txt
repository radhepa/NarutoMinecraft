# Shopkeepers' greetings and haggling in their own voices: one piece of content per kernel, said in the
# shopkeeper's register. Hearing one voice of a kernel spends the others.

## shop.ichiraku.greet  shop=ichiraku  fn=commerce,reveal_character
* ichiraku_steam
warm:   You look half frozen. Sit by the pot, the steam will put colour back in your cheeks.
bold:   Stool's open, broth's boiling, and I'm ready to cook. Let's go!
proud:  Sit. Nine years and this broth has never once been served lukewarm.
quiet:  Sit. Noodles?
any:    Steam's up, stools are free. Sit wherever.

## shop.pawn_shop.haggle  shop=pawn_shop  fn=commerce,reveal_character

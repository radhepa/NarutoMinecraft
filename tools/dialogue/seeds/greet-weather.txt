# Greetings by weather, one idea in several voices (compose.py writes generated/gen-greet-weather.txt).

## greet.weather  weather=rain  fn=react
* rain_umbrella
warm:    You're soaked! Take my umbrella, I live just there. Bring it back whenever.
bright:  Rain! Did you know the water tanks on the roofs overflow if it keeps up past noon? I'm watching to see!
bold:    Rain's no reason to stay in! I ran laps round the market in it this morning!
calm:    Rain. The village needs it, even if our sandals don't.
precise: It's been raining since four. The gutters on this street are blocked at the corner again; I've reported it twice.
quiet:   Rain. ...I like the sound on the roof.
proud:   A little rain doesn't keep a proper shinobi indoors. Or a proper shopkeeper.
guard:   Wet streets, slippery bridges. Walk, don't run, {player}.
dreamy:  The whole village has gone soft at the edges, like a painting someone left out in the rain.

## greet.weather  weather=rain  fn=worldbuild
* rain_training
bold:    The training grounds are pure mud! Best footwork practice there is! I fell over eleven times!
proud:   The training grounds are mud today. The lazy stay home. I'm on my way there.
calm:    The training grounds will be empty in this. A good day to practise alone, if you don't mind the mud.
bright:  Somebody tried water-walking on the lake in the rain and couldn't tell where the lake stopped!
any:     The training grounds are all mud in this rain. Only the keenest are out there.

## greet.weather  weather=thunder  fn=react
* thunder_count
bright:  Count with me! One, two, three... BOOM! Three seconds! It's getting closer!
warm:    That thunder! Come in, sit by the stove. I'll put the kettle on to drown it out.
bold:    Did you feel that one? It shook my teeth! What a storm!
calm:    Count between the flash and the thunder, and you'll know how far the storm is. Don't be afraid of it.
quiet:   ...Loud. Stay inside for a bit.
guard:   Storm's right overhead. Get under a roof and stay away from the trees.
proud:   Thunder doesn't frighten me. I simply prefer to wait it out indoors.
any:     What a storm! Get inside till it passes, {player}.

## greet.weather  weather=snow  fn=react
* snow_play
bright:  Snow! Snowball fight at the Academy yard! Everyone's going! Come on!
warm:    Snow! Have you got warm enough clothes? Come here, your hands are like ice.
bold:    Snow's perfect for training! You can see exactly where you slipped!
calm:    Snow makes the whole village slow down. I think it's good for us.
quiet:   Snow. ...Everything's so quiet.
proud:   I've already cleared my whole stretch of the street. The neighbours are still on their first shovel.
dreamy:  It looks like the sky's been sifting flour all night.
guard:   Ice on the bridges. Watch your footing, and hold the rail.
any:     Snow today! Wrap up warm, {player}.

## greet.weather  weather=snow  fn=guide
* snow_food
warm:    In this cold you need hot food. Ichiraku's broth, or the sweet potato cart by the main street.
bright:  The sweet potato cart is out! It's the best thing about snow. Second best is snowballs.
precise: When it snows, the sweet potato cart sets up at the main street corner at ten. Six ryo a potato.
any:     The sweet potato cart is out by the main street. Nothing better in the snow.

# Greetings by district, and on Market Day.

## greet.place  district=academy  fn=guide
Visiting the Academy? The library's through the main doors and left. The workshop's out the back by the yard.
The target posts in the yard are free after lessons. Just don't use the swing tree as a target. It's been tried.
If you're looking for a teacher, try the staff room upstairs. Knock loudly; they're all marking.

## greet.place  district=academy  fn=worldbuild
Hear that bell? Every child in the Leaf learns to run at that sound.
The swing tree out front has carvings in its bark going back generations. Half the Jonin in the village left one.
The big fire sign on the Academy roof gets repainted every spring. The students do it. Badly. Then the teachers fix it.
Mind the yard, the kids drew a throwing line in chalk and they'll be very cross if you scuff it.
The Academy and the Hokage Residence are two buildings, you know. Visitors mix them up every single day.
Every graduation year, somebody climbs the swing tree to tie their old practice headband on a branch.

The Academy grounds are quiet between lessons. Lovely, really. Like the eye of a storm.

## greet.place  district=market  fn=guide

Need a tool? The weapon shop's past the tea house; follow the sound of the forge.
The flower stalls are under the striped awnings. You'll smell them before you see them.
The pawn shop's down the side alley by the dumpling seller. They'll buy bandit gear, nothing else.

## greet.place  district=market  fn=worldbuild

Every stallholder on this street knows every other stallholder's prices. Nobody admits it.
Mind the carts. Market traffic waits for no one, and the radish cart least of all.
The market street smells of grilled squid at one end and cut flowers at the other.
Busy today. Somebody always gets their sandal trodden off by noon.

## greet.place  district=main_street  fn=guide
Ichiraku's just along the main street. Look for the red lanterns and the queue.
Follow the main street all the way and you'll reach the Hokage Residence, right under the Rock.
The tea house is two doors past the furniture shop. Benches out front, shogi inside.
The Mission Desk is on the Residence's ground floor. Open from eight till six.

## greet.place  district=main_street  fn=worldbuild
The main street runs straight from the gate to the Rock. You can see the faces from every step of it.
Every shop on the main street hangs its own lantern. Count them some evening; it's never the same number.
The main street was paved stone by stone. Every family on it laid a few. Some of the stones have initials.
Parades come down the main street at festivals. The rest of the year it's carts, kids and arguments.
The utility poles along here hum on wet days. The old folk say it's the village talking to itself.

## greet.place  district=residential  fn=worldbuild
Quiet street, this. The loudest thing here is the neighbour's rooster.
The houses on this street all have round water tanks on the roof. On hot days you can hear them ticking.
Every house here has its own plaque by the door. Ours is a bit crooked. My father hung it.
Laundry lines from one balcony to the next. If you see a sock fall, nobody's going to claim it.
The residential streets wake up slowly. First the kettles, then the shutters, then the arguing.
This lane's had the same families in it for generations. Everyone knows whose cat is whose.

## greet.place  district=residential  fn=guide

The bathhouse is at the end of this street, past the big persimmon tree. You can't miss the chimney.
The furniture shop delivers to anyone on these streets. Ask nicely and they'll carry it up the stairs too.

## greet.place  district=gate  fn=guide
That's the main gate. If you're heading out on a mission, sign the guard's book first.
The guard booth's on the left. They'll answer any question you've got, and several you haven't.
Waiting for a squad? Missions usually leave from right here, under the big characters.

## greet.place  district=gate  fn=worldbuild
Those two huge characters on the gate doors? One on each. Every traveller stops to stare at them.
The gate's the best place to hear news. Every cart, every courier, every rumour comes through here first.
The wall runs right round the village from here. The guards walk the whole of it twice a day.
The Inuzuka kennels are just inside the gate. If you hear barking, that's the welcome committee.
Caravans line up out there at dawn. The merchants have to show their papers, every one of them.
Coming home through this gate feels different from leaving. Everyone says so.

## greet.place  district=hokage_rock  fn=worldbuild
Look up. Four faces, and room left for more. Everyone in the village wonders whose will be next.
The faces on the Rock were carved by hand, with ropes and chisels. It took years for each.
The stairs up the side of the Rock have more steps than anyone agrees on. Count them yourself.
From up there you can see the whole fan of the village, every street running out from here.
The Rock catches the sun first in the morning and last at night. The faces never sleep.
Children dare each other to touch the nose of the first face. Few get anywhere near.

## greet.place  district=hokage_rock  fn=guide
The path up the Rock starts behind the Residence. Take water; it's a climb.
The best view of the faces is from the bench at the foot of the main street. Sit, look up, breathe.
The Hokage Residence is right below the faces. Mission Desk on the ground floor, offices upstairs.

## greet.place  district=training  fn=teach
Training? Warm up first, then the posts. Cold muscles pull, and pulled muscles take a week.
The lake on this ground is for water-walking. Keep your chakra steady in your feet, not too much, not too little.
The cliff's for wall-running. Start low. Everyone who starts at the top starts again at the bottom.
A spar here ends when someone yields or can barely stand. Nobody dies on the training ground. That's the rule.

## greet.place  district=training  fn=worldbuild
Hear that? Kunai into wood, all day long. The posts get replaced every season.
Three stumps in a row at Training Ground Three. Every Genin squad in the village has stood at them.
Somebody's been at the logs since dawn. Look at the dents.
The grass on the training grounds never grows back in the middle. Too many feet.

## greet.place  district=shrine  fn=worldbuild
Hear the bell? Everyone gets one ring. Make it count.
The shrine's where the village comes for New Year and for weddings. Most days it's just the crows and me.
The fortune papers tied to the rope there? Bad luck, left behind so it doesn't follow people home.
The steps up to the shrine are worn smooth in the middle. Hundreds of years of feet.
The shrine's foxes are carved stone, but people still leave them fried tofu. Someone always eats it.
Quiet up here, isn't it? Even the wind seems to whisper.

## greet.place  district=shrine  fn=guide

Fortunes are drawn from the box by the bell. A few ryo, and you'll know your luck for the season.
The bench behind the main hall faces the Rock. Best place in the village to sit and think.

## greet.place  district=river  fn=worldbuild
The river splits the village in two, and the bridges sew it back together.
The fishing boats tie up under the second bridge. The fishermen argue there every evening.
The water's clear enough to see the stones on the bottom, right up until the rain.
The ferry's run from the same landing for a hundred years. The ferryman's rope is new every spring.
Herons fish along this bank at dawn. Stand still and they'll come within arm's reach.
The kids skip stones off the third bridge. The record's nine. Nobody's beaten it in years.

## greet.place  district=river  fn=teach

The stepping stones by the old bridge are good balance practice. They're also good for falling in.

## greet.market  market=yes  fn=worldbuild
Market Day! Every farmer from outside the wall is here, and every one of them is shouting.
It's Market Day, {player}! The main street's been stalls end to end since dawn.
Mind your purse on Market Day. Not because of thieves. Because of the dango.
Market Day means the travelling merchants are in. Strange spices, strange cloth, strange hats.
Smell that? Every food stall in the village is grilling at once. Market Day's the best day of the week.
The fishmongers bring their best catch on Market Day. The cats know. Look at them all lined up.
Market Day! I've been saving all week. Today I'm buying the good pickles.
Every Market Day the same two farmers set up side by side and argue about turnips. It's tradition now.
The flower stalls spill right across the street on Market Day. You can't walk without stepping in petals.
Market Day crowds! I nearly lost my shoe by the tea house. Somebody handed it back on a stick.
The Akimichi buy half the meat on Market Day. The other half's for the rest of us, if we're quick.
Market Day brings the whole village out. Even the Aburame come, quiet as always, buying honey.

For lunch on Market Day, skip Ichiraku's queue and try the dumpling cart by the second bridge.
Prices drop at the end of Market Day. Wait until the stalls start packing up, and haggle.

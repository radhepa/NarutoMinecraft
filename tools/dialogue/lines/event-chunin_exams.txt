# betting on teams, full inns. Nothing about how the exams go.
#! tags: event=chunin_exams

## chat.event  fn=worldbuild
The visiting teams stay in the guest lodging by the stadium. Lights on all night over there.
Every tea house has a betting board up. Half the village has a few ryo on some team or other.
I put five ryo on a team from a village I'd never heard of. Don't ask me why, I liked their hats.
The stadium seats sell out for the finals the same day they go on sale.
The bakers make special exam buns. Sweet bean inside, a little headband drawn in sesame on top.
Merchants follow the visiting teams. There are stalls in the market street I've never seen before.
The guards at the gate are checking travel papers twice over this week.
The noise from the stadium on finals day carries all the way to the river.
The visitors stare at the Hokage Rock like they've never seen a carved mountain before. They haven't.
Exam weeks are good for business and bad for sleep. My inn hasn't had a quiet night since the fourteenth.
Some of the visiting shinobi bought out the whole dango stall. Twice.
The written test is on the first day, they say. After that, nobody outside knows much until the finals.

## chat.event  fn=teach
The betting boards are for fun. Lose a few ryo, not your rent.
The finals are open to anyone with a seat. The stalls outside sell cushions, bring one.

## chat.event  personality=pragmatic|meticulous  fn=reveal_character

## chat.event  personality=curious|thoughtful  fn=reveal_character

I wonder what the visitors think of us. Our food, our streets, our stone faces.

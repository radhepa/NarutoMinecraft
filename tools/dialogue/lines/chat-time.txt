# Small talk, part A: what this hour is like in the Leaf, and what's open.

## chat.time  time=dawn  fn=react
Dawn's my favourite hour. The Rock goes pink, the river steams, and nobody's asked me for anything yet.
Bakers have been up for an hour already. You can smell the first loaves from here.
Out on the river, the fishermen are just pushing off. If you want the best catch, you'll be at the river bank in an hour.
About now the night guards are handing over at the gate. Tired faces going home, fresh ones coming out.
Up this early? Me too. The birds are louder than the whole market at this hour.
One by one, the lamp keepers are putting out the street lanterns. You can watch the dark fold up behind them.
I like walking the main street at dawn. Every shutter's closed, and the whole village feels like it's holding its breath.
Hear the shrine bell? That's dawn. Some people say a prayer. Most just roll over.
At this hour the training grounds belong to the early risers. Hear the thuds? Someone's been at it since dark.
Mist off the river at this hour makes the bridges look like they're floating.

## chat.time  time=dawn  fn=teach
Shops won't open until about seven. If you're hungry now, the bakery sells from its back door.
Most folk wake around six. If you slept before midnight, you'll feel it. Chakra comes easier all morning.
The Mission Desk opens at eight. Squads start queuing well before. Bring breakfast.

## chat.time  time=dawn  fn=filler
Morning already. The sun never asks if I'm ready.
Early, isn't it? Still, a good early.

## chat.time  time=morning  fn=react
Shutters are going up all down the market street. The grocer's already arguing with a customer about leeks.
Any moment now the Academy bell rings. Look at the children running. The late ones run the fastest.
Look at that line at the Mission Desk, out the door already. Every Genin squad in the Leaf wants the easy D-ranks.
Morning's when the market's freshest. The vegetables still have dew on them.
Squads are heading out through the gate. You can tell the C-rank ones by how heavy their packs are.
Every morning the tea house fills up with old folk. Same seats, same tea, same arguments as yesterday.
Washing's out on every balcony. On a sunny morning, the whole village looks like it's waving.
Forge's morning shift has started. Listen. That's three hammers, and one of them's out of time.
Mornings, the flower shop gets its deliveries, and the whole main street smells of lilies until ten.
Every morning the herbalists put their racks out by the hospital. By noon the air there smells like medicine and mint.

## chat.time  time=morning  fn=teach
Shops open around seven and stay open until evening. Morning's the best time if you want first pick.
The Mission Desk is open from eight to six. The best D-ranks go early, so don't dawdle.
Academy lessons run from nine till noon on weekdays. After that, the yard's a madhouse.

## chat.time  time=morning  fn=filler
A fresh morning. Anything could happen.
Good morning, properly. The day's still clean.

## chat.time  time=noon  fn=react
Noon already? The Academy's just let out. Listen to that racket. Like a flock of sparrows let out of a cage.
Ichiraku's busiest at noon. You'll wait for a stool, but the line moves quick.
Everyone eats at noon here. Look, even the guards on the wall have their lunch boxes open.
Sun's right over the Rock. No shade anywhere. The cats have all gone under the porches.
Noon is when the market gets loud. Every stall's shouting its best price before the afternoon slump.
Quietest hour at the shrine is noon. Everyone's eating. Good time to sit up there and think.
Hear the forge stop? That's the smiths at lunch. The silence is almost odd.
At noon the tea house does a cheap lunch set. Rice, soup, pickles and a fish. Best bargain in the Leaf.
Squads that left at dawn on short D-ranks start coming back about now, muddy and starving.
At noon the river's full of children cooling off. The water's cold enough to shout about.

## chat.time  time=noon  fn=teach
If you slept well last night, that extra spring in your chakra's about to wear off. It lasts until noon.
Academy lessons end at noon. If you want the library quiet, go before then. After lunch it's full.

## chat.time  time=noon  fn=filler
Lunch time. That's the best time.
Half the day gone. Not bad, so far.

## chat.time  time=afternoon  fn=react
Afternoons are for training. Hear the training grounds? Kunai thunking into posts from here to the river.
Market's slowed right down. The stallholders are fanning themselves and playing cards.
Afternoon sun makes the Rock look carved out of honey. Painters love this hour.
After lunch, everyone gets a little slow. Even the dogs.
Nobody's at the bathhouse in the afternoon. Best time to go, if you want the hot water to yourself.
Squads with long D-ranks are still out in the farms. You'll see them stagger home near dusk.
In the afternoon the kites come out. Children fly them off the bridges. Some of them even come back down.
Every afternoon the old shogi players set up their boards on the benches by the river. Nobody's allowed to watch silently.
The tea house is full of people pretending they're not napping. The owner pretends not to notice.
Afternoon is when the part-time shifts start. Ichiraku needs onion choppers, the forge needs coal haulers.

## chat.time  time=afternoon  fn=teach
The Mission Desk is open until six. If you want a mission today, now's the time.
Shops close in the evening, so if you need supplies, don't leave it much later.
Plenty of folk take part-time jobs in the afternoon. Ask at Ichiraku, the grocer, the flower shop or the forge.

## chat.time  time=afternoon  fn=filler
A slow afternoon. I don't mind them.
Long day yet. Plenty of time.

## chat.time  time=evening  fn=react
Lanterns are going up along the main street. Evening in the Leaf always smells of grilling fish.
Evening's when Ichiraku really comes alive. Shinobi coming off missions, families out for dinner.
One by one, the shops are shuttering. That clatter's the sound of the Leaf saying "enough work for today".
Look at the sky behind the Rock. Orange, then purple. The faces look like they're glowing.
Squads are back from the day's missions. Listen to them bragging on the bridge.
At dusk the river fills with lantern reflections. Some people sit on the banks and do nothing else.
Evening's when the barbecue house fills up. The smoke over the Akimichi roofs is a kind of dinner bell.
Mothers are calling children home all over the residential streets. Some of the names get shouted three times.
There goes the dusk bell from the shrine. Half the village stops for a breath. The other half doesn't hear it over dinner.
Cicadas start up at dusk. In summer, they're louder than the market at noon.

## chat.time  time=evening  fn=teach
The Mission Desk closes at six. If you've a report to hand in, you'll have to wait for morning now.
Most shops close in the evening. Ichiraku's still open. So are the bathhouses, for an hour or two.
Festivals and events go on in the evening, but they're done by midnight. The Leaf goes to bed on time.

## chat.time  time=evening  fn=filler
Evening already. Where did the day go?
Nice evening. The kind you want to keep.

## chat.time  time=night  fn=react
How fast the village goes quiet at night. One minute the main street's full, the next it's just lanterns and cats.
At night you can see the lanterns on the wall from anywhere in the Leaf. A ring of little fires.
Every night the watch walks past my window at the same time. I've started waving. They wave back.
At night the Rock's just a shadow. You can only see the faces when the moon's out.
Bathhouse steam rises white in the dark. It's nice to walk past on a cold night.
I like the sound of the river at night. You can't hear it during the day over all the noise.
Police are out on the main street at this hour. Strict, but they'll walk you home if you ask.
So many stars over the Leaf tonight. Less light from the shops, more from the sky.
Owls are out. One's been sitting on the Academy swing all night. Thinks it's a student.
Last of the ramen customers are leaving Ichiraku now. Sleepy faces, full bellies.

## chat.time  time=night  fn=teach
Sleep before midnight and you'll wake Well Rested. Your chakra comes back quicker all morning.
If you can't sleep, a cup of green tea will keep the tiredness off for an hour. Don't make a habit of it.
Your own bed's the best place to sleep. You'll wake at six, fresh, and ready.

## chat.time  time=night  fn=filler
Getting late. Time to think about bed.
Quiet night. I like them.

## chat.time  time=late  fn=react
Still up? The whole village is asleep. Even the dogs.
This late, it's just the night watch and the owls. And us, apparently.
I can't sleep. I've been sitting on my step listening to the wind in the training ground trees.
Main street lanterns have burned low. Some have gone out altogether.
In a few hours the bakers'll be up. That's how late it is. Nearly early.
Past midnight. The Leaf belongs to the cats now.
At this hour, even the river sounds sleepy.
I heard the night watch change on the wall a while ago. That's how I know it's very late.

## chat.time  time=late  fn=teach
Past one, and you'll start to feel it. Heavy legs, slow chakra. Bed's the only cure.
Stay up past three and you'll be stumbling. Chakra jumps go short, kunai fly wide. Get some sleep.
If you're still awake at six, you'll drop where you stand and wake at noon. Ask me how I know.
Any bed will clear your tiredness, but only your own bed before midnight leaves you Well Rested. Too late for that now.
A hot meal helps when you've been up too long. Takes the weight off your legs for an hour.

## chat.time  time=late  fn=filler
It's very late. Go to bed. I'm going to.
Late, late, late. What are we doing up?

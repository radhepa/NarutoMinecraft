# Greetings by season: the Leaf's year as residents feel it.

## greet.season  season=spring  fn=worldbuild
Spring at last! The cherry trees along the river are about to burst.
The Uzumaki repaint the seals on their memorial every spring. You can smell the fresh ink from the street.
The rice paddies outside the wall are flooded for planting. From the Rock they look like mirrors.
Every kite in the village is out this week. Three of them are stuck in the Academy swing tree.
The frogs are back in the irrigation ditches. Loud little things. I missed them.
The Senju children plant their saplings in spring. You'll see them out with tiny watering cans.
The grocer's got the first bamboo shoots in. Spring's officially here when those come.
Spring cleaning season. Every balcony in the apartments has a futon hanging over it.
The Yuhi grove is in flower. You can smell it from two streets away.
Spring rain and spring mud. The training grounds look like a pig pen.

## greet.season  season=summer  fn=worldbuild
The cicadas are deafening this year. You can't hear yourself chew.
Summer means festival yukata hung out to air on every line in the residential streets.
The kids have discovered the deep pool under the old bridge again. Their mothers have too.
The shaved ice stall by the market is open. The queue's longer than Ichiraku's.
Fireflies along the river after dark. Summer's best gift, if you ask me.
Mosquitoes are vicious by the water this year. Burn some incense on your sill.
Even the faces on the Rock look like they want to go for a swim.
The Akimichi are buying watermelons by the cartload. I've never seen one go to waste.
Every training ground is half empty after noon. Too hot to throw anything but water.
The bathhouse switched to cool baths for summer. Not that anyone's complaining.

## greet.season  season=summer  fn=teach

In summer the Academy shortens afternoon drills. Even the teachers give up in this heat.

## greet.season  season=autumn  fn=worldbuild
The maples on the Rock have turned. The faces look like they're wearing red scarves.
Chestnut season! The roasting cart by the tea house is out from noon.
Persimmons drying on every eave in the residential streets. Orange lanterns that you can eat.
The Akimichi harvest feast is coming. The smell from their kitchen hall reaches the market.
The Nara count their deer in autumn. Half the clan stalks through the forest with tally sticks.
Moon-viewing season. The Gekko platform will be full every clear night now.
Every gutter in the village is full of leaves. The sweepers can't keep up.
Rice harvest's in, outside the wall. The farmers are celebrating with too much sake.
The grocer's piled sweet potatoes to the ceiling. Autumn's the only season the rice gets competition.
The Academy holds its sports day when the leaves turn. Parents shout louder than the kids.

## greet.season  season=winter  fn=worldbuild
The Sarutobi are preparing lanterns for the New Year walk. Their whole yard glows at night.
Winter means the bathhouse drops yuzu in the big tub. Smells like a whole grove in there.
The oden carts are out. Nothing warms the hands like a cup of broth on a cold street.
The days are so short in winter. It's dark before the Mission Desk even closes.
Every house on the street has a kotatsu going. You can tell by who doesn't come out.
The New Year's coming. The shrine is already stacking up sake barrels for the visits.
Mochi pounding at the end of the year is the loudest thing in the residential streets.
Frost on the training posts in the morning. Hit one and your hand rings for an hour.
The river's slow and grey in winter. The ferry runs half as often.
Winter's quiet. Even the market slows down.

# Small talk from inside the fifteen noble clans: customs, the quarter, clan day, neighbours. Never a secret.

# -- Senju ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=senju  fn=worldbuild
The tree in our hall was a sapling when the village was founded. We water it before we feed ourselves, every morning.
Every Senju child plants a sapling on their first clan day. Mine is by the east wall, crooked and stubborn, a lot like me.
Guests at the hall are given tea under the tree. Nobody refuses, and nobody has ever been allowed to sit with their back to it.
The founders' shrine is swept twice a day. Most mornings the cousins argue over whose turn it is, which is half the point.
Our quarter sits right under the Rock, beside the Residence. Living that close to the faces, you learn to stand up straight.
The hall roof leaks wherever the branches grew through it. We put bowls under the drips and call it the tree's music.
The Uzumaki memorial stands just over our wall. Our elders leave a cup of tea there on the first morning of every month.
We were a great clan once and we're a small one now. The tree doesn't seem to mind, so we try not to either.

## chat.clan  clan=senju  fn=reveal_character
I like pruning best. You have to know what to cut and what to leave, and the tree tells you if you got it wrong.
My grandmother can name every Senju who ever sat under that tree. It takes her most of an afternoon.
I'm not as strong as the stories about my family. I've made my peace with being good at gardening instead.
Living in a founding family means everyone expects you to be wise. Mostly I just remember to water things.

## chat.clan  clan=senju  fn=hook
A dry week is coming, the elders say, and the tree drinks more than the well gives. We'll need extra hands with buckets.

# -- Uzumaki -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=uzumaki  fn=worldbuild
Our hall is mostly a memorial now. The spiral is on every beam, and every name on the wall is read aloud once a year.
The seals on the memorial are repainted each spring. One wrong stroke and you start the whole panel again.
There are so few of us that clan day is one long table. We set out bowls for the ones who aren't here and fill them anyway.
The sealing-mask hall is the loudest quiet room in the village. All those faces on the wall, and you can feel them watching.
The Senju have looked after us since before I was born. We repay them with noise and seals, which seems to suit them.
We live long, my family. My great-aunt still climbs the memorial ladder at ninety, and complains when anyone holds it.
Brushes for sealing are made from the tips of a weasel's tail. I've got one that belonged to someone I never met.

## chat.clan  clan=uzumaki  fn=reveal_character
I practise seal strokes on the back of rice paper every night. My hand is steady now; it used to look like a spider walked through ink.
I'm loud, I know. When there's only a handful of you, you have to be loud enough for everyone who's missing.
I learned all the names on the wall before I learned my letters. My grandfather made a song out of them.
Some days the memorial feels heavy. Then I repaint a seal and it feels like keeping a promise instead.

## chat.clan  clan=uzumaki  fn=hook
The ladder by the memorial has a cracked rung, and nobody here is a carpenter. I'd rather ask before somebody falls.

# -- Uchiha --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=uchiha  fn=worldbuild
The fan is on every wall in our quarter. My grandmother stitches it into the hem of every cloth she owns, dishcloths too.
Most of the Military Police are Uchiha. The station is in the far corner of the quarter, and the lanterns there never go out.
At sixteen we take the police oath in front of the whole clan. I've seen grown Jonin cry at it and swear it was the smoke.
Our quarter is on the edge of the village. People say it's so we keep watch on the wall. People say a lot of things.
Guests in an Uchiha house don't ask about eyes. That's the first rule, and nearly the only one we explain.
The police festival parade goes through the main street every summer. The children wear paper fans and march out of step.
The Morino think they should hold every prisoner. We think prisoners should be held by people who caught them. Old argument.
Our grilled rice crackers are the best in the Leaf. That's not pride; it's just true, and I'll fight anyone who says otherwise.

## chat.clan  clan=uchiha  fn=reveal_character
I joined the police because my family expected it. I stayed because I'm good at it, and that surprised me.
We have a reputation for taking offence. I take offence at that, so I suppose it's earned.
I'd like to see the main street without someone stepping aside for me. Just once. It's the uniform, mostly.
I practise fire jutsu at the lake at dawn. My little cousin counts how long the fireball lasts and never gets the number right.

## chat.clan  clan=uchiha  fn=hook
A market stall keeps reporting thefts to the station and then refusing to say what was taken. I'd like to know what we're guarding.

# -- Hyuga ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=hyuga  fn=worldbuild
A bow in the Hyuga estate has a depth for every person: elder, guest, cousin, main house, branch. You learn them before you can read.
Tea in the main hall is taken in silence. The first person to speak sets the topic, so nobody speaks lightly.
Sparring is how we talk. Two cousins who quarrel will be on the dojo floor before dinner, and friends by dessert.
The koi in our pond are older than some of the servants. The eldest is white with one gold spot and it sulks if you're late with the food.
The estate is two houses under one roof line, main and branch. Visitors notice; we pretend they don't.
We're a short walk from the Residence. Hyuga have carried messages along that road for longer than anyone remembers.
The gardeners rake the gravel in straight lines every morning. A crooked line means someone ran across it, and everyone knows who.

## chat.clan  clan=hyuga  fn=reveal_character
I've held a bow at exactly the right depth for an hour while an elder spoke. My back remembers it better than my head.
In the branch house we're taught that duty is a kind of freedom. Some days I'm sure of it. Some days I spar very hard.
My favourite place in the estate is the edge of the pond at dawn, before anyone asks me to be correct.
I can tell how angry my father is by how quietly he sets down his cup. Today it barely made a sound.

## chat.clan  clan=hyuga  fn=hook
One of the young branch-house cousins has been training alone at night, far past the curfew. I'd like someone to check on them without making it a matter.

# -- Nara ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=nara  fn=worldbuild
Every porch in our quarter has a shogi board. Nobody finishes a game in one sitting; we leave them set up for weeks.
The deer forest is behind the house, and the deer are counted every year by hand. You'd be amazed how badly deer stand in line.
People call us lazy. We call it thinking lying down. The clouds help.
The memorial for the three clans stands near the forest's edge. The Yamanaka bring flowers, the Akimichi bring snacks, we bring the shade.
Our alliance with the Akimichi and the Yamanaka is older than the village. We still split the bill three ways at the barbecue house.
Antlers the deer drop in spring are gathered by the children. What happens to them after that is family business.
The forest paths look the same to visitors, but every Nara knows them by the shadows at noon.

## chat.clan  clan=nara  fn=reveal_character
I once spent a whole summer afternoon watching one cloud. My mother asked what I'd done all day. I said, a lot.
I lose at shogi to my grandfather every time. I've started to suspect it's on purpose, so I'll keep coming back.
Strategy is just being too lazy to make the same mistake twice.
The deer trust me more than people do. I don't take it personally; deer are better judges of character.

## chat.clan  clan=nara  fn=hook
A few of the deer have been limping near the forest's north edge. It's probably a fallen fence. Probably.

# -- Akimichi ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=akimichi  fn=worldbuild
Our kitchen hall feeds forty on an ordinary night. On clan day, nobody's counting.
We always set one more place than we need. Someone always turns up to fill it.
Never refuse the third helping in an Akimichi house. The first two are hospitality; the third is a promise.
The storehouse behind the hall has rice from three harvests. A clan that eats this well plans for the lean years.
The barbecue house down the street is ours. Every Akimichi child learns to turn meat before they learn to throw a kunai.
Strength is like food: it's meant to be shared. That's the first thing my grandfather taught me, at a table, naturally.
We live right by the market street. The fishmongers know us by name and the grocer saves us the big sacks.
The three-clan alliance meets for barbecue once a month. The Nara never pay on time and the Yamanaka always bring flowers we can't eat.

## chat.clan  clan=akimichi  fn=reveal_character
I can cook for fifty, but I've never cooked for one. I wouldn't know how to make a pot that small.
Some of my family want to be shinobi, some want to be chefs. I want both, and I'm told that's greedy. Fine.
Comment on an Akimichi's size and you'll learn a lot about yourself very quickly. I'm just warning you.
My favourite sound is a full table going quiet because the food's arrived.

## chat.clan  clan=akimichi  fn=hook
The barbecue house has had a quiet season. Some of the elders want to change the menu, and some would rather close it than change a thing.

# -- Yamanaka ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=yamanaka  fn=worldbuild
The flower shop is the front of our compound, right on the main street. Most of the village thinks that's all we are.
A bouquet always says something. Get the flowers wrong and you've said something you didn't mean.
The greenhouse behind the shop grows what the market can't: orchids, winter roses and a moonflower that blooms once a year.
The quiet hall at the back is where we train. It's called quiet for a reason; even the birds seem to land softly there.
Many of the village's sensors are Yamanaka. We hear things in a crowd that others miss, and we learn early not to repeat them.
The Suzuran sell herbs on the same street. We send each other customers at the hospital and steal them at the market.
At clan day we make a garland of one flower from every member's garden. It's longer every year, and that's the point.

## chat.clan  clan=yamanaka  fn=reveal_character
I can read a room the way other people read a menu. It's not a technique, just years behind a shop counter.
My favourite flower is the one nobody buys. The plain ones are honest, and I like honest.
I tried to give up the shop for the field once. I lasted a month before I missed the smell of the cold room.
Some days I'd like to be a little less perceptive. You notice too much about people at the counter.

## chat.clan  clan=yamanaka  fn=hook
Someone keeps ordering a bouquet with a message no flower should send, and asking us to leave it unsigned. I'd like to know who it's for.

# -- Inuzuka -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=inuzuka  fn=worldbuild
Every Inuzuka has a ninken. You don't pick the dog; the dog picks you, and a dog's choice is final.
Our compound is right by the main gate, which suits us. The dogs need the outer fields and so do we.
On clan day the pups are brought out and the children sit in a circle. The pups wander until one sits down in a lap. That's that.
The clinic sees half the dogs in the village, not just ours. There's always one barking, and it's usually the healthy one.
Dinner in our house is loud. Dinner in the kennels is louder. Nobody minds either.
The Aburame think we're too noisy. We think they're too quiet. Every year we race them in the outer fields to settle it.
The run behind the kennels is the size of a training ground, and the fence still has holes the dogs pretend aren't theirs.

## chat.clan  clan=inuzuka  fn=reveal_character
My partner's been with me since I was six. I can't remember a morning I woke up without a cold nose in my ear.
I'd go hungry before my dog did. Anybody in the clan would. That's the whole of the rule.
People say I smell like a kennel. I say they smell like soap and shame, and the dogs agree with me.
I've never been able to whisper. Grew up shouting over forty dogs, so the dogs got the volume and so did I.

## chat.clan  clan=inuzuka  fn=hook
A young ninken has gone missing near the wall. Its partner's frantic, and the scent trail stops dead at a stream.

# -- Sarutobi ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=sarutobi  fn=worldbuild
Every guest in a Sarutobi house is offered a seat by the hearth. Even in summer, even if the guest is only delivering a letter.
The monkey on our gate has been repainted so many times its face has softened. My uncle swears it used to look fierce.
The fire-training yard behind the house is black to the ankle. Nothing grows there, and we like it like that.
We have a long staff tradition. The old staves hang in the hall by height, and the tallest is taller than the doorway.
At New Year our family leads the lantern walk through the village. A thousand lanterns and every one lit from the same flame.
The Shimura disagree with us about everything at council, and I mean everything. Once it was the colour of the new curtains.
Our house is near the Residence. When I was small, I thought the Hokage was just a neighbour who worked very late.

## chat.clan  clan=sarutobi  fn=reveal_character
I've given more of my life to this village than to my own garden, and my garden is a mess. I'd choose the same again.
My grandfather taught me to light a pipe, a lantern and a fireball in the same week. My mother has never forgiven him.
I talk too much about the village. Tell me if I start a speech; my cousins time them.
I'm tired a lot. A Sarutobi is supposed to burn bright, and nobody mentions how much fuel that takes.

## chat.clan  clan=sarutobi  fn=hook
We need a hundred more lanterns for the walk this year, and the paper merchant's sold out. Somebody has to find another supplier.

# -- Aburame -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=aburame  fn=worldbuild
Visitors to the insect gardens stand still and let the kikaichu taste their chakra. It takes about a minute. Nobody has ever enjoyed that minute.
The first rule of the Aburame house: never swat. The second is to remember the first.
Our hive walls hum all night. Guests can't sleep; family can't sleep without it.
Insect night is in early summer. We open the garden gates and the fireflies come up off the grass all at once. The whole street goes quiet.
The house is dark timber at the edge of the woods. The insects prefer shade, and so, frankly, do we.
The Inuzuka's dogs dig in our garden beds. We document every incident, with drawings. The file is very thick.
Every Aburame wears lenses outdoors. People think it's to hide. It's because pollen is a nightmare.

## chat.clan  clan=aburame  fn=reveal_character
People find me cold. I'm not. I simply see no reason to say a thing twice when once was accurate.
I keep a notebook of every beetle in the village. There are one hundred and twelve kinds. I've named the rarest ones after the weather.
My favourite hour is dusk in the garden. The day insects go to bed and the night ones wake, and for a moment nobody is working.
I laughed once, at the Academy. It surprised everyone so much I've been careful ever since.

## chat.clan  clan=aburame  fn=hook
A rare beetle is hatching in the east garden this week, and the dogs next door won't stay out. I'd value a polite fence.

# -- Hatake --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=hatake  fn=worldbuild
The house is mostly empty. The ninken come and go as they please; they've lived here longer than I have.
Everyone in the village knows this lane. Very few knock on the door. I've stopped minding.
The yard has kennels for six. Two of them are in use, and the other four still get fresh straw every week.
There's a memorial stone I visit quietly. That's a custom in this family, and there's nothing more to it than that.
The roof leaks over the old study. I've put a bucket there so long the bucket has a name.

## chat.clan  clan=hatake  fn=reveal_character
I'm the only one in the family who still lives here. Some evenings I set two cups out of habit.
I read too much. When the house is this quiet, a book is the only voice that doesn't echo.
The dogs decide who's worth greeting. They've been right about everyone so far, including me, which was humbling.
I'm not good at visits. If I come to your door and stand there for a while, it means I'm trying.
I keep the house the way it was. Not because I'm sad, mostly. It's easier than deciding what to change.
The old ninken sleeps on the porch every afternoon in the same patch of sun. I think it's waiting for something. I don't ask.

## chat.clan  clan=hatake  fn=hook
The lane's landlord keeps asking whether the house is for sale. It isn't. I could use someone to say so more loudly than I do.

# -- Kurama --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=kurama  fn=worldbuild
Our house is on the old artists' lane. Every room is half studio, and the screens are painted on both sides.
A portrait is a promise in this family. You don't paint someone unless you mean to keep them in your life.
A painted eye is never finished in public. We cover the easel when the last stroke goes on.
The light in the north studio is the best in the village. We fight over it, politely, in turns.
Our genjutsu and our painting grew from the same root. Nobody outside the family quite believes that until they see a screen move.
The Yuhi have their grove and we have our studio. Two schools of illusion, and we compliment each other's work in the most cutting way possible.
We hold an exhibition every autumn. Half the village comes for the art and half comes to see whether the paintings blink.

## chat.clan  clan=kurama  fn=reveal_character
I paint the same pear every morning before I'm allowed to paint anything else. Thirty years, and the pear is still the hardest subject.
Ink gets under my nails and never comes out. I've accepted being permanently grey at the fingertips.
I can't look at a wall without wanting to paint it. My landlord has asked me to stop asking.
Some days I'm not sure whether I'm seeing what's there or what I'd rather paint. That's the family gift, and the family worry.

## chat.clan  clan=kurama  fn=hook
One of the smaller screens from the hall has gone missing. The house won't say much about it, but everyone is rather quiet today.

# -- Shimura -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=shimura  fn=worldbuild
A sealed letter is never opened by its carrier. In this house that rule is older than the house itself.
Silence is a courtesy here. If I don't speak, I'm not ignoring you. I'm being polite.
The house is close to the Rock. The shadow of the faces reaches our roof by mid-afternoon.
Shimura sit on the council and always have. We disagree with the Sarutobi on every point, and we keep the minutes of each disagreement.
We eat at the same time every evening, in the same order, at the same table. Visitors find it severe. We find it restful.
Our garden is gravel and three stones. Anything more would be decoration.

## chat.clan  clan=shimura  fn=reveal_character
I was taught to say exactly what I mean, once. Most people use three times the words for half the meaning.
I have one indulgence: a sweet bean cake after a long council day. Don't tell anyone.
People think our family is cold. We are not cold. We are simply not wasteful with warmth.
I run the same route along the wall every morning before light. I've done it for twenty years, and I intend to do it for twenty more.
I respect the Sarutobi, though I would never say so at council. Their hearth is warm. Their arguments are not good.

## chat.clan  clan=shimura  fn=hook
A council letter went astray between the Residence and our gate last week. It was found, still sealed. Someone wanted it found.

# -- Yuhi ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=yuhi  fn=worldbuild
Our house sits in a grove of old trees. We train among them, and the trees have long since learned not to believe their eyes.
Grove viewing is in spring, when the roses climb the oldest trunks. Guests come for the flowers and stay for the tea.
Tea in our grove may not be tea. Guests are told so before they drink. It's only polite.
Roses, thorns and crimson. Every Yuhi house has the same three things on its gate.
The Kurama paint their illusions; we grow ours. They say ours are untidy. We say theirs are flat.
The oldest tree in the grove has a split down the middle. Children of the family sit in the split on their first clan day.
The thorns on our hedge are left long on purpose. The gardeners wear thick gloves and grumble beautifully.

## chat.clan  clan=yuhi  fn=reveal_character
I prune roses at dawn because nobody can see me get pricked. I always get pricked.
My grandmother could make a guest believe the teacup was empty for a whole afternoon. I'm still working on a minute.
I like the grove best in the rain, when the illusions and the mist get mixed up and nobody can tell which is which.
I've been told I have a cold stare. I've practised it in the mirror, so I'm glad it works.

## chat.clan  clan=yuhi  fn=hook
One of the old trees in the grove is losing its leaves out of season. The elders blame the Kurama's studio water. The Kurama blame the weather.

# -- Gekko ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=gekko  fn=worldbuild
Our dojo is by the training grounds, and the moon-viewing platform is on its roof. On a clear night you can see half the village from there.
Kenjutsu duels are held under the full moon. The moon is the judge; the clan is only the audience.
A blade in this family is named and never lent. Lend your sword and you might as well lend your hand.
We're a quiet family. Most people only notice us when we come off the night shift, pale and polite.
The Namiashi think they move like water. We move like moonlight. We've argued about which is faster for a hundred years.
The dojo floor has a crescent worn into it where the first form ends. Every Gekko child has stood in that crescent.
Moon viewing in autumn is our clan day. Rice dumplings on the platform, and the duels after.

## chat.clan  clan=gekko  fn=reveal_character
I sleep poorly. Most of my family do. We tell ourselves it's the moon, and that's a fine story.
I practise the first kata three hundred times before breakfast. It's dull, which is the point.
My blade is called Still Water. I named it at twelve, and I've regretted how serious that sounds ever since.
I cough in the cold. I always have. A swordsman learns to make even a cough sound polite.

## chat.clan  clan=gekko  fn=hook
A named blade in the family was lent, against custom, and hasn't come back. Nobody will say who lent it. Everybody knows.

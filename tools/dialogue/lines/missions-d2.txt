# -- orchard: persimmons from the tallest trees. Slots: client (farmer), orchard ---------------------

## mission.d.orchard.title
Persimmon Picking, Tall Trees

## mission.d.orchard.brief  job=mission_desk  fn=quest,guide
{client}'s persimmons at {orchard} are ripe at the very tops. Tree walking welcome, ladders provided. {pay}.

## mission.d.orchard.brief  fn=quest,guide
The low fruit's picked. The best ones are up top in {orchard}, where my ladder won't reach.
Twist, don't pull, and lay them in the basket stem up. A bruised persimmon is a sad persimmon.

## mission.d.orchard.accept
Wonderful. Mind the thin branches. Persimmon wood snaps without warning.
Good. You'll take a basket home after. Hang them to dry and they'll be sweet by winter.

## mission.d.orchard.decline
Then the birds get the top ones again. They always do.
No? I'll ask my nephew. He climbs like a monkey and complains like one too.

## mission.d.orchard.progress
Look at the colour on that one! Careful, careful. Into the basket.
Your feet are stuck to the trunk like it's the ground. Show-off.

## mission.d.orchard.success
Baskets full to the brim and not one bruise. You've a light touch.
The best crop in years, and it all came down in one piece. Take some home.

## mission.d.orchard.fail
Half the fruit on the grass. Well, the crows will be happy.
Not many baskets today. Never mind. The tree will try again next autumn.

## mission.d.orchard.report  job=mission_desk
Orchard job, done. There's a persimmon on my desk now. I'm choosing to think it's for me.
{client} says the top branches are bare for the first time in years. Logged.

## mission.d.orchard.twist.wasps  fn=quest,react
A wasp nest hangs in the best tree, right where the ripest fruit is. Of course it does.

## mission.d.orchard.twist.stuck_child  fn=quest,react
A child climbed up after you and now won't move. Clinging to a branch, very high, very quiet.

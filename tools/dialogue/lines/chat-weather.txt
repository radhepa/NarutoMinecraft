# Small talk about the weather: rain, thunder and snow, and only the clear days worth mentioning.

# -- rain ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.weather  weather=rain  fn=react,worldbuild
Rain like this and the Ichiraku queue doubles. Everybody wants something hot, and nobody wants to cook it.
The gutters on the market street can't take more than an hour of this. By noon the fish stall will be selling from a puddle.
The training grounds turn to porridge in the rain. Good for practising your footing, terrible for your sandals.
The stone steps up to the shrine get slick as river rocks. Take the handrail, whatever your pride says.
The frogs in the Academy pond are singing like they've been paid. You can hear them from the main street.
Rain on the Hokage Rock runs down the faces in streaks. The children say the Hokage are crying, and the grown-ups say they're washing.
The bath house will be full by four today. Wet people want warm water; it's simple arithmetic.
Every paper umbrella in the Leaf is open at once. From the Rock, the main street must look like a row of mushrooms.
The river's up to the second step at the ferry landing. Another day of this and the ferryman will be poling over the washing stones.
Laundry day ruined again. Every balcony on this street has the same sad row of damp shirts.
The drying racks behind the fish stall are all under tarps. Rain is bad news for anyone selling dried mackerel.
A good soaking rain is what the farmers outside the wall have been praying for. The cabbages will be the size of heads.
Rain makes the dogs at the kennels sulk. Forty damp ninken, all piled together and smelling exactly like you'd expect.
The roofs are too slippery for jumping today. You'll see a lot of shinobi walking the streets like ordinary people.
Mission scrolls go out in oiled pouches on days like this. A wet briefing is no briefing at all.
The tea houses close their garden doors in the rain and light the braziers. The best seats are by the window, watching it come down.
The water tanks on the roofs are filling nicely. That's a warm bath for the whole block for a week.
My sandals squeak in this weather. I can't sneak up on anyone, which my family is very grateful for.
The Academy moved yard practice into the hall today. Thirty children throwing practice shuriken indoors: the janitor is not happy.
Rain stops the market haggling dead. Nobody wants to stand in it arguing over two ryo; the prices fall all morning.

## chat.weather  weather=rain  fn=react,reveal_character
I like the rain, honestly. The village goes quiet and soft, like somebody turned the noise down.
I left my umbrella at the tea house again. That's three this month. The owner's started a collection.
Rain makes my old knee ache. That's how I know it's coming before the clouds do.
I always mean to stay home on rainy days, and I always end up out in it. Something about puddles.

## chat.weather  weather=rain  personality=playful|fiery  fn=react
Race you to the end of the street without an umbrella! Loser buys the hot tea. [EXCLAIM]
Puddles this deep are basically a training exercise. Jump them all and you've done your footwork for the day!

## chat.weather  weather=rain  personality=gentle|warmhearted  fn=react
Are you dry enough? Stand under the awning a minute. A cold now and you'll be sniffling all week.
The stray cats have all found doorways to sit in. I put out a dish under the eaves for them.

## chat.weather  weather=rain  personality=pragmatic|meticulous  fn=react
Rain means indoor work: mending, inventory, sharpening. I've had my whole list ready since yesterday's clouds.
Rain or no rain, the Mission Desk opens at eight. A D-rank doesn't care that you're wet.

## chat.weather  weather=rain  job=farmer|grocer|fishmonger|ferryman  fn=react,worldbuild
This rain is worth a hundred ryo a day to the fields. I'd dance in it, if my knees would let me.
Wet days, the river carries mud down from the hills. The fish go deep and sulk, and so does my trade.

## chat.weather  weather=rain  job=ramen_cook|cook|tea_master|bath_keeper  fn=react,commerce
Rain is good for business. A bowl of something hot never sells better than when the street's a river.
I put an extra pot on this morning. The clouds were too grey to waste.

## chat.weather  weather=rain  rank=genin|chunin|jonin  fn=react,worldbuild
Tracking's useless in this. Every scent washed off and every footprint a puddle. Ask any Inuzuka how they feel about it.
Rain hides sound well. Good for scouting, terrible for hearing who's scouting you.
Paper bombs and wet days don't mix. Keep your tags wrapped, or you'll be throwing very expensive confetti.

## chat.weather  weather=rain  season=spring  fn=react,worldbuild
Spring rain knocks the cherry blossoms off in a single night. Tomorrow every street will be pink and soggy.

## chat.weather  weather=rain  season=summer  fn=react,worldbuild
Summer rain is warm enough to stand in. The children are out in it already, catching it in their mouths.

## chat.weather  weather=rain  season=autumn  fn=react,worldbuild
Autumn rain strips the maples. By tomorrow the training grounds will be a red carpet with mud underneath.

# -- thunder -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.weather  weather=thunder  fn=react,worldbuild
Every dog in the Inuzuka kennels is under a blanket right now. Even the war-trained ones hate thunder.
The Mission Desk sometimes holds outgoing squads in weather like this. Lightning picks the tallest thing on a ridge, and that's usually a Genin.
The tea houses shutter their windows at the first crack. Nobody wants to drop a cup when the sky bangs.
That one rattled the bowls on the shelf. My grandmother says it's the sky clapping for somebody.
The shrine keeps a lightning rod on the cedar. The tree has been hit three times in a hundred years, and it's still standing.
Thunder echoes off the Hokage Rock like a drum. It sounds twice as loud by the Residence.
Storms like this blow the lanterns off the market street. The stallholders will be picking paper out of the gutters tomorrow.
The bath house never closes for a storm. The keeper says no lightning has ever got through all that steam.
Thunder at night and the whole apartment block wakes. You can hear every flat's kettle go on at once.
The river turns brown and fast in a storm. The ferry's tied up and the ferryman is sitting under the landing roof, glaring at the sky.
Children at the Academy count between the flash and the bang. The instructors pretend it's an arithmetic lesson.
The farmers will be out with tarps over the seed beds the moment this passes. One big storm can flatten a month of growing.
Lightning lit the whole village white for a moment just then. I could see every roof tile down to the wall.
The gate guards bring the booth's kettle inside in a storm. Someone learned that the hard way.
The roof-runners are all on the ground today. Running across tiles in a thunderstorm is how you meet the hospital staff.
A storm this size usually clears overnight. Tomorrow will be rinsed clean and smell like cut grass.
The Tobari night watch calls a storm a blind night. You can't hear anything on the wall, so you have to watch twice as hard.
That was close. The air smells like a forge right after a strike, burnt and sharp.
Thunderstorms send the herons up off the river in a cloud. They'll be back on their stones by morning, as if nothing happened.
My cat is under the bed and won't come out until the sky apologises.

## chat.weather  weather=thunder  fn=react,reveal_character
I love a good storm. I sit on the porch with the shutters half open and count the strikes. Twenty-three last time.
I'm not scared of thunder. I just happen to be standing very close to this doorway, that's all.
When I was small, my father told me thunder was the Hokage moving furniture upstairs. I believed him for years.
Storms make me restless. I'd rather be out in it doing something than inside listening to it.

## chat.weather  weather=thunder  personality=shy|gentle  fn=react
Is it... is it getting closer? I'll just stay under this roof a bit longer, if that's all right.
The baby birds under the eaves are all huddled up. I hope the nest holds.

## chat.weather  weather=thunder  personality=fiery|adventurous  fn=react
Listen to that sky! That's what a lightning jutsu ought to sound like. [EXCLAIM]
Storms are the best time to train your nerve. Anyone can concentrate when it's quiet!

## chat.weather  weather=thunder  personality=stern|protective  fn=react
Stay off the roofs and away from the training ground trees. I won't say it twice.
Go home if you've nowhere you need to be. There's nothing out here worth a lightning strike.

## chat.weather  weather=thunder  rank=genin|chunin|jonin  fn=react,worldbuild
A lightning-natured shinobi feels a storm in their teeth. I know three who can't sleep through one.
Thunder covers footsteps. On a mission, a storm is a friend for about an hour, and then it's an enemy.
Missions don't stop for weather, but smart squads do. Shelter, wait it out and move when it passes.

## chat.weather  weather=thunder  job=smith|carpenter|painter|tailor  fn=react,worldbuild
Damp in the air and my glue won't set. Today's a day for sharpening, not building.
A storm's no time for the forge. Lightning likes metal, and I like having eyebrows.

## chat.weather  weather=thunder  job=vet|kennel_keeper  fn=react,worldbuild
Every pup in the clinic is pressed against my legs. I've been standing in the same spot for an hour.

## chat.weather  weather=thunder  season=summer  fn=react,worldbuild
Summer storms come up over the hills in the afternoon and blow out by supper. Like a bad temper.

## chat.weather  weather=thunder  season=autumn|spring  fn=react,worldbuild
Storms between seasons are the worst. Warm wind, cold rain and thunder that can't make up its mind.

# -- snow ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.weather  weather=snow  fn=react,worldbuild
The Hokage faces have snowy eyebrows this morning. The second one looks like a very stern grandparent.
Snow on the training grounds means footprints for everyone to read. A sneaking exercise in this is a lesson in humility.
The bath house will be packed by sunset. In this weather, a hot soak is worth more than ryo.
The market street shovels a path down the middle at dawn. By noon it's packed ice, and somebody's always sliding.
Every roof in the Leaf has a white cap. The round water tanks look like giant rice cakes.
The Akimichi kitchen hall has a pot of hot soup going all day when it snows. Their doors are open to anyone cold.
Snow muffles the whole village. Even the kennels are quiet, and that never happens.
The children have built a snow Hokage outside the Academy. It looks nothing like any of them and it's perfect.
The river's freezing at the edges. The ferryman says another cold week and you'll be able to walk across without chakra.
The shrine priest knits caps for the fox statues when it snows. They've all got new ones on today.
The gate guards drink their tea standing in a snowfall. They say sitting makes your bottom freeze to the bench.
Fresh snow at dawn is the best time to see who's been out at night. Every alley has its own story in footprints.
The tea house has put out its winter cushions, thick and heavy. You sink into them like a bowl of rice.
Snow makes the main street look twice as wide. All those dark roofs turned to white, and the lanterns glowing through it.
The deer in the Nara forest come right up to the house when it snows. The clan feeds them on the porch.
Grocers sell more sweet potatoes on a snowy day than in a month of sun. There's a man with a roasting cart on every corner.
The ice on the Academy pond is thick enough to skate on. The instructors have forbidden it, which means everyone is skating on it.
Wet snow sticks to the training dummies. They look like snowmen in sparring stances.
The Uchiha police walk their rounds in black coats in the snow. You can see them from the Rock, dark spots moving along every street.
A snowy village sounds like a held breath. Even the shinobi landing on rooftops make a soft little thump.

## chat.weather  weather=snow  fn=react,reveal_character
I've never been able to resist the first footprint in fresh snow. It's like signing your name on the day.
Snow makes me want to stay home under the kotatsu with a book and a bowl of oranges. I'm fighting it.
I grew up in a village where it never snowed. My first winter here, I thought the sky was falling.
I'm terrible on ice. Two winters ago I slid the length of the market street on my back. Nobody let me forget it.

## chat.weather  weather=snow  personality=playful|fiery  fn=react

## chat.weather  weather=snow  personality=warmhearted|protective  fn=react

Make sure you've got something hot for dinner tonight. Cold days creep into your bones if you skip a meal.

## chat.weather  weather=snow  personality=imaginative|thoughtful  fn=react

Every snowflake is different, they say. I wonder who checked.

## chat.weather  weather=snow  rank=genin|chunin|jonin  fn=react,worldbuild

Water-walking on a half-frozen lake is a lesson you only need once. Ask me how I know.

## chat.weather  weather=snow  job=farmer|merchant|courier  fn=react,worldbuild
Carts can't get through the east road in this. Everything in the market today is whatever was already in the village.
Deliveries take twice as long in the snow. I've had to swap my sandals for straw boots, and they're terrible for running.

## chat.weather  weather=snow  job=ramen_cook|dango_maker|tea_master|barbecue_cook  fn=react,commerce
Snow days are my best days. The steam off my counter brings people in from all the way down the street.

## chat.weather  weather=snow  season=winter  fn=react,worldbuild
Deep winter now. The shops open late and close early, and everyone carries a little charcoal warmer in their sleeve.

## chat.weather  weather=snow  season=autumn|spring  fn=react,worldbuild
Snow this early? The farmers will be rushing to get the last of the radishes in before the ground freezes.

# -- clear (only the days worth mentioning) ----------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.weather  weather=clear  season=summer  fn=react,worldbuild
This heat would melt a kunai. The children have taken over the river shallows and the dogs have taken over every shadow.
Ichiraku sells cold noodles on days like this, and the queue still goes round the corner.
The cicadas are so loud by the training grounds you can't hear yourself throw. Everyone's chakra feels like it's boiling.
The bath house keeper says nobody comes on a day this hot. Then the cold box of milk sells out by noon.
The stone of the Hokage Rock holds the heat until midnight. Sit against it after dark and it's like a warm stove.
The tea houses are serving barley tea over ice. It's the only thing anyone wants in this weather.
Shinobi in full gear on a day like this are made of sweat and determination. Mostly sweat.

## chat.weather  weather=clear  fn=react,worldbuild
Not a cloud from the Rock to the wall. You can see all four faces sharp as if they were carved this morning.
That's a perfect sky. The kind of blue you only get after a week of rain.
Wind's up from the east today. All the laundry on the residential streets is trying to fly away.
The wind's strong enough today that the Academy cancelled shuriken practice. Half of yesterday's throws ended up on the main street.
Sky this clear and the hawks from the Residence loft are flying high. You can see them circling over the Rock.
On a day this clear you can see the far hills past the wall, blue in the distance, like a painting of somewhere else.
Clear night ahead. If you stay up, the stars over the training grounds are better than any festival.

## chat.weather  weather=clear  season=winter  fn=react,worldbuild
Bright sun on yesterday's snow. Everyone's squinting and the icicles are dripping from every roof.
A clear winter morning is the coldest kind. Frost on every railing and your breath hanging in front of you like a cloud.

## chat.weather  weather=clear  season=autumn  fn=react,worldbuild

## chat.weather  weather=clear  season=spring  fn=react,worldbuild

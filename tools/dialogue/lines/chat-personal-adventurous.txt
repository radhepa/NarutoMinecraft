# Small talk, part A: the answer to "How's your day?" for adventurous residents. Their own life, in their voice.
#! tags: personality=adventurous

## chat.personal  fn=reveal_character
Restless! I climbed the watchtower just to look at the hills past the wall. One day I'll walk to the far ridge.
I traded a merchant from the east road a jar of pickles for a map. Half the map is wrong. I love it.
I've been sleeping on the roof this week. The sky's bigger up there. My back disagrees.
A trader told me about a village where the houses float on a lake. I've been dreaming of boats all morning.
I keep a pack by my door, ready to go. Spare socks, rope, dried fish. I've never needed it. One day.
I walked the whole length of the wall this morning. It's further than you'd think, and the view never gets old.
My grandfather crossed three countries with a cart and a goat. I've only crossed the river. I've some catching up to do.
I've been learning to read the clouds. The fisherfolk say a mackerel sky means rain by tomorrow. We'll see.
I ate a fruit today I've never seen before. Spiky outside, sweet inside. The merchant said it comes from the south coast.
I found a path behind the shrine that goes up into the trees. I followed it for an hour and found... more trees. Wonderful.
My dream is to see the sea. Real salt water, waves bigger than the Hokage Rock. That's what they say, anyway.
I've been collecting stones from every place I've been. The jar's half full. Most are from the riverbank, if I'm honest.
A courier stopped at the tea house today, dusty to the knees. I bought the tea just to hear where the road had been.
I tried to cook a dish I heard about from a traveller. Fish wrapped in leaves, buried in coals. The fish didn't survive.
Every time the main gate opens I stop and watch. Carts, couriers, squads. I like guessing where they're headed.
I swam across the river and back before breakfast. The cold makes you feel like you could do anything.
I spent the morning drawing a map of the village from memory. Got the Rock right. Everything else wandered.
They say there's a hot spring three days north. I'm saving up the time off. I've already packed a towel.
I bought new sandals for walking. Broke them in by walking to the farms and back. Twice. Blisters, but happy.
My wife and I are planning a trip to the border villages. She wants the markets. I want the mountains. We'll do both.
I climbed the tallest tree in the training grounds. Saw smoke from the farms, a hawk below me, and my own house. Tiny.
I keep asking couriers to bring back a postcard of something. One brought me a drawing of a cow. Best gift I've had.
The fishermen took me out on their boat at dawn. I caught nothing and fell in. Best morning in months.
I've been learning words in the trade dialect from the merchants. So far I can say "too expensive" and "where is the toilet".
I keep the window open at night so I can hear the wind from the forest. It always sounds like it's got somewhere to be.
There's a cave above the quarry I've been meaning to explore. Bats, probably. Maybe something better.

## chat.personal  rank=civilian  fn=reveal_character
I'm a courier's apprentice now. Only inside the walls so far, but I run every route like it's a mountain pass.
I sell maps at the market. Most people buy them to frame. I buy them back when they move. Maps should travel.

## chat.personal  rank=!civilian  fn=reveal_character
My last mission took me past a waterfall taller than the Rock. I'd do a hundred D-ranks to see that again.
I always volunteer for the escort missions on the east road. Every inn has its own pickles. I'm keeping score.
Coming home over the hills after a mission, that first sight of the faces on the Rock. Nothing beats it.
I slept under the stars for five nights on my last mission. Back in a bed, I can't sleep. Too soft, too quiet.

## chat.personal  fn=worldbuild
Travellers come in through the main gate and sign the guard's book. I've read it. People come from very far away.
The east road has a waystation with a stone lantern. Squads leave a pebble on it when they come home safe.
Merchants from the south bring salt and dried squid in autumn. The whole market street smells of the sea for a week.
The Leaf sits in a bowl of hills, and every road in starts with a climb. That's why couriers have such good calves.

## chat.personal  fn=hook
My compass rolled off the bridge and into the reeds. It was my grandfather's. Points north and slightly to the left.
A traveller left a sealed letter at the tea house for "whoever is going east". Nobody's going east. It's just sitting there.
I've been trying to find the old trail to the lookout rock. The map says it's behind the Nara forest. I can't find the start.
I lost my walking stick at the river crossing. It had a carved hawk on top. I'd hate for it to wash away.

## chat.personal  fn=filler
Good! Better if I were somewhere new, but good.
Every day's an adventure if you walk a different street.

## chat.personal  fn=reveal_character
I learned to tie six knots from a ferryman today. A traveller should know at least six. I know four, properly.
My boots are drying by the stove after a walk through the marsh by the farms. Worth every soggy step.
I asked the gate guard what's the furthest anyone's come from. A dancer from beyond the desert. I've been dreaming of sand.

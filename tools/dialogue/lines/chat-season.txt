# Small talk, part A: the season in the Leaf. What's ahead, what's cooking, what needs doing.

## chat.season  season=spring  fn=worldbuild
Spring means graduation at the Academy. The yard fills up with families, and the new headbands catch the sun.
Spring brings the Chunin Exams. Teams from other villages start arriving, and the inns fill up overnight.
When the cherry trees bloom along the river, half the Leaf takes its lunch outside. Nobody gets any work done.
Spring is planting season at the farms. The Mission Desk fills up with D-ranks for digging and sowing.
Fawns are born in the Nara forest in spring. They wobble about the forest edge on legs like chopsticks.
In spring the market's full of bamboo shoots and wild greens. Everyone's soup tastes of the forest for a month.
Spring cleaning's serious business in the Leaf. Every futon in the village gets beaten on the balconies the same week.
High water in spring, with snowmelt from the hills. Kids are told to stay off the low bridge.
Spring rain comes soft and often. The moss on the shrine steps turns bright green overnight.
Come spring, the Yamanaka flower shop's window is all blossoms and tulips. People queue just to look.
Swallows come back to the eaves in spring. Families mark the nests and argue about whose are the same birds.
In spring the roofers are everywhere, fixing what the winter snow cracked. The village sounds like a drum.
Spring's Chunin Exam finals fill the stadium. Even the shopkeepers close up to go and watch.

## chat.season  season=spring  fn=reveal_character
I planted my beans this week. Every spring I promise myself a neat garden, and every summer it's a jungle.
Spring makes me restless. I find myself staring at the gate, wondering what the roads are like this year.
My nose hasn't stopped running since the blossoms came out. Lovely to look at. Terrible to breathe.
I always make sakura mochi in spring. Pink rice, salted leaf, sweet bean inside. My children eat them faster than I make them.
I put away the winter quilts today. Every year I'm sure I'm doing it too early, and every year I'm right.

## chat.season  season=spring  fn=react
Blossoms are out! I can't walk past the river without stopping. Even the stern folk smile under those trees.
Spring rain again. The gardens are happy. My laundry isn't.
Warm enough to leave the shutters open. Spring in the Leaf smells of wet earth and blossom.

## chat.season  season=spring  fn=hook
Late snow broke a branch on the cherry tree by the bridge. If it isn't tied up, the blossoms will go to waste.
Farms are behind on planting. They're asking for anyone with a strong back and a free morning.
A swallow's nest fell off the eaves at the tea house. The chicks are in a basket on the counter. They need a new home.

## chat.season  season=spring  fn=teach
Spring rain makes the training-ground logs slick. Shinobi say you learn more balance in one wet spring than in three dry summers.

## chat.season  season=spring  fn=filler
Spring at last. Everything feels new.
I love this time of year. Everyone's in a good mood.

## chat.season  season=summer  fn=worldbuild
Summer's the Leaf Festival. Lanterns on every street, food stalls from the gate to the river, fireworks over the Rock.
Summer means cicadas. They start before dawn and go until dusk. You stop hearing them after a week. Mostly.
In summer the river's full of children from noon till dusk. The deep pool under the low bridge is the favourite.
Summer storms come in fast over the hills. One minute blue sky, the next the whole village is running for cover.
Ichiraku does cold noodles in summer. Same broth, chilled. People who swear by hot ramen sneak a bowl anyway.
Every rooftop water tank in the Leaf is warm by afternoon in summer. Free hot baths, if you're not fussy.
Summer at the market is watermelons stacked like cannonballs. The grocer lets the children pick the biggest.
Festival fireworks go up from the top of the Rock. You can see them from every street in the village.
Festival yukata come out in summer. The tailors are busy for weeks beforehand, and nobody gets theirs early.
Wind chimes go up on every porch in summer. On a breezy evening the whole street sings.
Summer's the time for night markets. Grilled corn, shaved ice and goldfish scooping by lantern light.
In summer the training grounds are at their hardest. Sensei love making squads run when it's hot.
Fireflies come out by the river in early summer. People walk down after dinner just to see them.

## chat.season  season=summer  fn=reveal_character
I've been sleeping with the shutters open. The cicadas wake me before dawn, but the breeze is worth it.
Shaved ice with sweet syrup is the only thing that keeps me alive in summer. Strawberry, if anyone asks.
I always buy a new fan before the Leaf Festival. I've got forty fans. I'll buy another this year.
Summer's for staying in the shade and doing as little as possible. I take that very seriously.
I won a goldfish at the festival scooping game last year. It's still alive. It's huge. I'm a little afraid of it.

## chat.season  season=summer  fn=react
Hot! Even the dogs are lying flat in the shade. The Leaf melts a little more every afternoon.
Leaf Festival's coming. I can feel it. The lantern-makers are working through the night.
Another summer storm last night. The streets are steaming already.

## chat.season  season=summer  fn=hook
Someone's goldfish from the festival jumped out of its bowl into the river. The owner's been crying on the bridge all morning.
Wind chimes went missing from the tea house in the storm. The owner says the summer won't feel right without them.
Lantern-makers are short of paper for the festival. If someone could fetch a stack from the scroll shop, they'd be grateful.

## chat.season  season=summer  fn=teach
Drink water in summer, especially on missions. Heat's beaten more Genin than bandits have.

## chat.season  season=summer  fn=filler
Hot day, isn't it? Stay in the shade.
Summer. Long days, short nights, and too many bugs.

## chat.season  season=autumn  fn=worldbuild
Autumn brings the Chunin Exams again. Foreign teams in the market, everyone guessing who'll make the finals.
Graduation comes round again in autumn. New Genin, nervous parents, lots of photos by the Academy swing.
Harvest comes in autumn. Carts of rice roll in through the gate every morning, and the farmers stay for a drink.
Maples by the training grounds go red in autumn. People walk out there just to see them.
Moon-viewing night is in autumn. People sit on their roofs with rice dumplings and tea and look up.
In autumn the market fills with chestnuts, sweet potatoes and persimmons. The whole street smells roasted.
Autumn's when the Leaf stocks up for winter. Firewood stacked by every door, pickles in every pantry.
Persimmons hang drying from every balcony in autumn. The crows think it's a feast laid out for them.
Every autumn the Akimichi hold a harvest feast. If you help carry the rice in, you're invited to eat it.
Autumn winds blow down from the hills. The kites come out again, the last good kite days of the year.
In autumn the Nara deer grow thick coats and start to look very pleased with themselves.
Leaves pile up so fast in autumn that the shrine priest gives up sweeping after noon.
Harvest jobs flood the Mission Desk in autumn. Every Genin in the Leaf learns to carry a rice sack.

## chat.season  season=autumn  fn=reveal_character
I love roasting sweet potatoes in the fire pit in autumn. The smell brings the whole street out.
I make persimmon jam every autumn. My mother's recipe. I've never got it right. I'm going to try again.
Autumn makes me a bit wistful. Everything's beautiful and leaving at the same time.
I always pick the reddest maple leaf I can find in autumn and press it in a book. I've got thirty years of them.
I've been stacking firewood all week. My back aches. The pile looks magnificent.

## chat.season  season=autumn  fn=react
Maples are turning. The whole training ground looks like it's on fire.
Chilly mornings, warm afternoons. Autumn can't make up its mind.
Harvest's coming in. You can hear the carts rumbling through the gate at dawn.

## chat.season  season=autumn  fn=hook
More rice than hands at the farms this year. They're paying anyone who'll help with the harvest.
Someone's been stealing persimmons off the drying lines on the residential street. Probably crows. Possibly not crows.
Gekko dojo's moon-viewing platform needs a new rail before moon-viewing night. They're short of carpenters.

## chat.season  season=autumn  fn=teach
Eat hot food when the cold comes. A good meal takes the weight off tired legs for a while, and autumn nights get long.

## chat.season  season=autumn  fn=filler
Autumn's my favourite. Everything smells like smoke and apples.
Getting cooler. Time to find the warm coat.

## chat.season  season=winter  fn=worldbuild
New Year comes at the end of winter. The shrine bell rings at midnight, and the line up the steps goes down the hill.
First snow is a little holiday here. Lessons go quiet, and every child in the Leaf is outside throwing snowballs.
In winter the Hokage faces wear snow caps. The children call them the Hokage's hats.
Winter means hot pots. Every home in the Leaf has one bubbling in the evening, and the windows all fog up.
Deep winter freezes the river at the edges. People skate the shallows by the reeds. Never the middle.
In winter the bathhouses are packed. The steam from them rises over the rooftops like the village is breathing.
Winter's when the Leaf mends things. Nets, sandals, roofs, coats. Every porch has someone sewing or whittling.
Rice cakes are pounded in the shrine yard for New Year. Anyone can take a turn with the big mallet.
In winter the gate guards get a brazier by the booth. Half the village stops to warm their hands on the way past.
Snow piles high on the market awnings. The stallholders knock it off with brooms every hour.
Winter nights in the Leaf are long and quiet. The lamp keepers light the street lanterns early.
New Year's first sunrise is watched from the top of the Rock. People climb up in the dark to see it.
Winter is the slow season at the Mission Desk. Mostly snow clearing and firewood runs.

## chat.season  season=winter  fn=reveal_character
I keep a kettle on the stove all winter. Anyone who knocks gets tea. That's my rule.
I knit through the whole winter. By spring, everyone I know has a new scarf whether they want one or not.
I hate the cold. I sleep in three blankets and still wake up with a frozen nose.
Winter's my favourite. Snow makes everything quiet and clean. I like the village best like this.
I make sweet red bean soup every winter. With a toasted rice cake on top. My family queues for it.

## chat.season  season=winter  fn=react
Snow again! The whole street's white. The Rock looks like it's wearing a blanket.
Cold this morning. My breath froze on my scarf. Mind the ice on the bridges.
New Year's coming. The shrine's busy hanging the straw ropes, and everyone's cleaning their house top to bottom.

## chat.season  season=winter  fn=hook
The old woman on my lane can't clear her own path in the snow. Someone ought to shovel it before she slips.
More hands are wanted at the shrine for the New Year rice pounding. They're short of people who can swing a mallet.
Gate booth's brazier has gone cold. The guards' firewood store is empty, and the courier's late.

## chat.season  season=winter  fn=teach
Snow on the roofs makes for bad footing. Shinobi keep to the ridges in winter, where the tiles show through.

## chat.season  season=winter  fn=filler
Cold out, isn't it? Stay warm.
Winter. Good weather for tea and staying in.

## chat.season  season=spring  fn=worldbuild
In spring the Academy hangs paper carp from the roof for the new students. They flap like they're swimming upstream.
Spring is when the clans repaint their gate crests. You'll see ladders and paint pots outside every compound for a week.
First green tea of the year arrives at the tea house in spring. The old folk taste it and argue about whether it's better than last year's.

## chat.season  season=summer  fn=worldbuild
In the hottest weeks of summer the Mission Desk sends Genin to carry water to the farms. Every bucket counts.
Summer evenings in the Leaf are for sitting on the porch in a cotton robe with a fan and a cold barley tea.
Shops run the Leaf Festival games: ring toss, goldfish, target throwing. The shinobi are banned from that last one.

## chat.season  season=autumn  fn=worldbuild
In autumn the sweet-potato cart comes round the residential streets at dusk. You hear the seller's song before you see the steam.
Autumn's when the Leaf's roofs are checked before the snow. The Kinoshita carpenters are booked for weeks.
Rice straw from the harvest is woven into ropes and sandals in autumn. Half of it ends up as New Year decorations.

## chat.season  season=winter  fn=worldbuild
At the end of winter every house in the Leaf is scrubbed top to bottom for New Year. Dust is not allowed into the new year.
In winter the Inuzuka ninken grow so fluffy you can barely see their eyes. The children follow them about to pat them.
Winter market sells hot ginger tea, roasted chestnuts and padded jackets. It closes early, when the cold sets in.

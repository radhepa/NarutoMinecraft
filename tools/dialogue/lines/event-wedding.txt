# Residents' weddings at the shrine (adults), with family and friends, then a party. Plain talk, no romance words.
#! tags: event=wedding

A wedding! The whole street's turned out to see the procession.
Morning! Have you heard? There's a wedding today. The shrine's been decked in white since dawn.
Wedding day at the shrine. Everyone's in their best, even the dogs have ribbons.

## greet.event  personality=warmhearted|gentle  fn=react

## greet.event  personality=stern|reserved  fn=react
A wedding. Good. They'll need patience. Every married pair does.

## greet.event  stage=child  fn=react

## chat.event  fn=worldbuild
The couple walk to the shrine under a red umbrella. The families follow in a long line behind.
At the shrine the couple share three cups of sake, three sips each. It's very quiet, then everyone cheers.
After the shrine, there's a party in the family's house. It goes on until someone falls asleep in the rice.
Wedding guests bring ryo in a folded envelope. Odd numbers only, for luck.
The two families sit across from each other at the wedding meal. By the end, you can't tell them apart.
I was at a wedding where the dog walked the whole procession. Nobody minded. It looked very proud.
The shrine priests go through the wedding blessing slow. It's one of the longest prayers they know.
A wedding between two clans means two crests on the lanterns. People count how many of each.
The couple gives every guest a little box of sweets to take home. I keep the boxes.
My spouse and I married at that same shrine. The cedar by the steps was just a sapling then.

## chat.event  fn=teach
If you're invited to a wedding, wear your best and bring a gift. A small one is fine, a thoughtful one is better.

## chat.event  personality=playful|fiery  fn=reveal_character
I'm going to dance at the party until my feet fall off. Then I'll dance sitting down.

## chat.event  personality=pragmatic|meticulous  fn=reveal_character
I'm in charge of the seating plan. Two families, forty people, three old grudges. Pray for me.

## chat.event  personality=thoughtful|steadfast  fn=reveal_character
A wedding is two families agreeing to share their worries. That's a braver thing than people think.

## chat.event  clan=akimichi  fn=worldbuild
At an Akimichi wedding, the meal lasts longer than the ceremony. By about six hours.

## chat.event  clan=inuzuka  fn=worldbuild
In our clan the ninken walk in the wedding procession. A wedding without a howl isn't a proper one.

## chat.event  stage=child  fn=worldbuild

The grown-ups danced. It was very embarrassing. I loved it.

## mission.d.academy_assist.title

## mission.d.academy_assist.brief  job=mission_desk  fn=quest,guide

## mission.d.academy_assist.brief  fn=quest,guide

## mission.d.academy_assist.accept

Good. Bring patience. Somebody always throws backwards on the first day.

## mission.d.academy_assist.decline

## mission.d.academy_assist.progress

## mission.d.academy_assist.success

Did you see their faces? Half of them want to be you. Thank you, truly.

## mission.d.academy_assist.fail

Not today's lesson, then. Children learn from a bad day too. So do teachers.

## mission.d.academy_assist.report  job=mission_desk

Target practice signed off. Any first-years follow you here? Check your pack.

## mission.d.academy_assist.twist.prankster  fn=quest,react

## mission.d.academy_assist.twist.demonstration  fn=quest,react

## mission.d.academy_assist.twist.lost_kunai  fn=quest,react

# Quarrel arcs (BONDS.md §9): greetings while a quarrel lasts, the book's line for each state, a missed
# birthday and jealousy. Argument: raw and fresh. Cold shoulder: the silence. Making up: thawing, wanting to
# mend. Falling-out: a door closed for good (only the story mends it).

## bond.greet.argument  fn=react
I'm still angry, {player}. Give me a day before you try to talk me round.
Not now. What you did is still sitting in my chest like a stone.
You've got a nerve, turning up here today.

## bond.greet.cold_shoulder  fn=react
...
Hm.
I've nothing to say to you right now.

## bond.greet.making_up  fn=react
{player}. I've been thinking about what happened. Maybe we both said too much.
I don't want to stay angry. I'm just not sure how to stop.
It's been quiet without you. Too quiet. Can we talk?

## bond.greet.falling_out  fn=react
We're done, {player}. Some things don't mend.
I'll be civil. Don't ask for more than that.
You know why I'm not smiling. Leave it there.

# -- the bond book: the quarrel's state ---------------------------------------------------------------
## bond.conflict.argument  fn=react  kind=cycle
You argued. It's still raw.
A fresh quarrel stands between you.

## bond.conflict.cold_shoulder  fn=react  kind=cycle
They aren't speaking to you.
The cold shoulder: a silence you'll have to wait out, or apologise through.

## bond.conflict.making_up  fn=react  kind=cycle
They're ready to make up, if you are.
The quarrel is thawing. Go and mend it.

## bond.conflict.falling_out  fn=react  kind=cycle
A falling-out. The door between you is shut.
Something broke between you that time alone won't fix.

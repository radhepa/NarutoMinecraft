# == an evening walk by the river ==================================================================
## hangout.evening_walk.ask
Walk with me by the river tonight? Just the two of us.
The river's beautiful at dusk. Would you walk it with me, {player}?
I'd like an evening with you. A slow walk, nowhere to be. Come?
## hangout.evening_walk.invite
The river path at dusk. I'll wait by the willow.
A note, folded very carefully: "Walk with me tonight? The willow, after sunset."
## hangout.evening_walk.yes
A walk with you? I'd like nothing better.
Yes. I've been hoping you'd ask.
## hangout.evening_walk.no.busy
I can't tonight, I'm sorry. {when}? I'll be counting the hours.
Not tonight. Ask me {when}. Please do.
## hangout.evening_walk.no.not_today
I'm not quite myself tonight. Another evening, I promise.
Not tonight. I want to be good company when we go.
## hangout.evening_walk.no.not_their_thing
Long walks aren't really my sort of evening. Could we do something else together?
## hangout.evening_walk.no.not_close_enough
A walk like that, just us? I don't think we're there, {player}. I'm sorry.
## hangout.evening_walk.arrive
There's the willow. Shall we go downstream? The light's better that way.
Here we are. Listen to the water. Walk slowly, there's no rush tonight.
## hangout.evening_walk.rough
That was a bit awkward, wasn't it. We'll find our footing.
## hangout.evening_walk.fine
A nice walk. Thank you for tonight.
## hangout.evening_walk.good
I didn't want it to end. Thank you, {player}.
I'll sleep well tonight. Let's walk again soon.
## hangout.evening_walk.great
I'll remember every step of tonight. Every single one.
That was the loveliest evening of my life. I mean that.
## hangout.evening_walk.stood_up
I waited by the willow until the stars came out. You didn't come.
## hangout.evening_walk.ritual
Our walks by the river. I find myself walking toward the willow without thinking now.
## hangout.evening_walk.ritual_missed
I walk past our willow and keep going. It's not the same without you.
## hangout.evening_walk.highlight.1
Fireflies! All along the river! Look, there's one on your sleeve.
## hangout.evening_walk.highlight.2
You took my hand on the stepping stones and didn't let go after. I noticed. [BLUSH]
## hangout.evening_walk.highlight.3
A heron lifted off the water right in front of us, silver in the last light.
## hangout.evening_walk.highlight.4
The rain started and you held your jacket over us both. We didn't even hurry.
## hangout.evening_walk.highlight.5
We stopped on the bridge and the whole river turned gold under us.
## hangout.evening_walk.highlight.6
You picked me a flower from the bank. I'm going to press it.
## hangout.evening_walk.highlight.7
We walked so far we reached the old mill. I've never been out this far with anyone.
## hangout.evening_walk.highlight.8
We sat on the bank and talked until the moon came up. I lost track of everything else.
## hangout.evening_walk.highlight.9
A street musician on the bridge played just for us. Well, for anyone. But it felt like us.
## hangout.evening_walk.highlight.10
You told me something you've never told anyone. I'll keep it safe, I promise.
## hangout.evening_walk.highlight.11
We skipped stones and laughed every time they sank. Yours sank more.
## hangout.evening_walk.highlight.12
Walking back, the lanterns came on one by one, all the way home.

# == lantern night on the lake =====================================================================
## hangout.lantern_night.ask
Lantern night on the lake? We could float one together.
Come to the lake tonight, {player}. I've made a paper lantern for us.
There's a lantern night on the water. Would you come with me?
## hangout.lantern_night.invite
The lake shore, after dark. I'll bring the lantern and a candle.
A note with a tiny painted lantern: "Tonight, on the lake. With me?"
## hangout.lantern_night.yes
Lanterns on the lake with you? Yes. Of course yes.
I'd love that. I'll write something on ours.
## hangout.lantern_night.no.busy
I can't tonight. {when}? I'll save the lantern for us.
Not tonight, sorry. {when}, if you'll still have me.
## hangout.lantern_night.no.not_today
I'm too tired tonight. I want to be awake for it.
Not tonight. Another lantern night will come.
## hangout.lantern_night.no.not_their_thing
Crowds on the shore at night aren't really my thing. Could we do something quieter?
## hangout.lantern_night.no.not_close_enough
That's for people who are... together. I don't think we are, {player}.
## hangout.lantern_night.arrive
Here's a good spot. Hold the lantern while I light it. Careful, it's paper.
Look at them all already on the water. Let's add ours.
## hangout.lantern_night.rough
The wind kept blowing the candle out. Maybe next time.
## hangout.lantern_night.fine
A pretty night. Thank you for coming.
## hangout.lantern_night.good
Ours floated out so far! I'm glad we did this.
That was lovely. I keep looking back at the water.
## hangout.lantern_night.great
I'll remember tonight forever. Every light on the water, and you.
I've never felt so happy. Thank you for tonight, {player}.
## hangout.lantern_night.stood_up
I floated our lantern alone. I wrote both our names on it anyway.
## hangout.lantern_night.ritual
Our lantern nights. I keep a stack of paper ready now, for the next one.
## hangout.lantern_night.ritual_missed
I haven't made a lantern since we stopped going. The paper's still in the drawer.
## hangout.lantern_night.highlight.1
Our lantern's drifted out further than all the others! Look, it's leading them!
## hangout.lantern_night.highlight.2
We wrote our wishes on it and didn't show each other. I'll tell you mine one day.
## hangout.lantern_night.highlight.3
The lights are reflected in the water so it looks like two skies. One above, one below.
## hangout.lantern_night.highlight.4
A breeze pushed our lantern back to the shore, right to us. Like it didn't want to leave.
## hangout.lantern_night.highlight.5
Someone on a boat started singing and the whole shore joined in.
## hangout.lantern_night.highlight.6
You shielded the candle with your hands so it wouldn't go out. It didn't.
## hangout.lantern_night.highlight.7
We sat on the shore until the last lantern went out. Then we stayed a bit longer.
## hangout.lantern_night.highlight.8
A fish jumped right through the reflection of our lantern. It scattered into a hundred lights.
## hangout.lantern_night.highlight.9
The fireworks started over the far shore just as ours touched the water.
## hangout.lantern_night.highlight.10
You leaned on my shoulder and we watched the water without a word. [HEART]

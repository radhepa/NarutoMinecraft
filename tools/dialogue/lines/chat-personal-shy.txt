# Small talk, part A: the answer to "How's your day?" for shy residents. Short, hesitant, brave in small ways.
#! tags: personality=shy

## chat.personal  fn=reveal_character
It's... fine. I said good morning to the baker today. Out loud. First time.
I tried to order at the new tea house, but I pointed instead. They understood. I think. I got something.
I've been... drawing. Just small things. Teacups, mostly. You don't have to look. It's fine.
My sister made me go to the festival meeting. I sat in the corner. Someone asked my opinion. I said "yes". To what, I'm not sure.
One tomato. That's what I grew. It's very red. I haven't told anyone. Except you now. Um.
Someone smiled at me at the market and I walked into a pole. It's fine. The pole's fine.
I wrote a letter to the shrine priest asking to help with the festival. I haven't sent it. Maybe tomorrow.
The library's nice. It's quiet, and nobody expects you to talk. I read about birds today.
I baked cookies for my neighbour, but I left them on the step and ran. I hope they knew they were from me. Or didn't. Either.
I have a cat. Well... the cat has me, really. It's the only one I talk to without stammering.
I sang a song in the bath. The neighbour knocked on the wall. I don't think I'll sing again for a while.
My mother says I need to speak up. I'm trying. I'm speaking up now. This is me speaking up.
I fold paper animals. Little ones. I leave them in places for people to find. It's my secret. Was. Oops.
I raised my hand at the town meeting. Then put it down. Then raised it again. Then the meeting ended.
I keep a little notebook of things I want to say to people. Mostly "thank you". I'll say them. Someday.
Mirrors are very easy to talk to. I practise conversations in mine.
Someone complimented my scarf today. I said "you too". They weren't wearing a scarf.
I made a friend at the bathhouse. We sat in silence for an hour. It was the nicest hour.
Ducks are kind. I fed them by the river. They didn't mind that I didn't say anything.
I helped carry a box for an old woman. She asked my name and I said it too quietly. She called me something else. I didn't correct her.
I've been learning the koto. Only when nobody's home. The walls are thin, so... maybe people know.
Rainy days are my favourite. Nobody expects you to go out. I can read by the window and feel... ordinary.
There's a lantern-folding group I joined. There are six of us. Five talk. I fold. It works.
My brother says I'm braver than I think. I don't know. I did ask for extra pickles at Ichiraku today.
Earlier I wanted to say hello to you, but... you were busy. Or I was. Anyway. Hello.
Sorry, I'm not good at this. My day was... nice? I watered the plants. They're doing better than me.
I found a quiet spot by the old willow. I go there to think. Sometimes I just sit. Is that weird?

My squad's very loud. I like them, though. They ask what I think. Sometimes I even tell them.
I gave the mission report myself today. My voice shook. The desk clerk said "good work". I've been smiling all afternoon.
I'm good at hiding. On missions it's useful. At parties it's... also useful.

## chat.personal  fn=worldbuild
The library has little desks by the windows that face the Rock. People mostly leave you alone there. It's nice.
At the festival, the shrine hangs wish slips on a rope. You don't have to sign them. I write one every year.
The bathhouse has a quiet hour in the morning. You're not meant to talk. I... look forward to it.

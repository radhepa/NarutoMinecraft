# callback.contradict: a resident notices the player has just answered the opposite of something
# they said before (two answers on opposite poles of one axis). Teasing or thoughtful, never cruel.
#! fn: callback,reveal_character

## callback.contradict
Funny. Last time we talked, you said the exact opposite.
Hm. That's not what you told me before. Changed your mind?
Wait, didn't you say something quite different a while ago?
You've turned right around since we last spoke about this. What happened?
I remember you answering the other way. People change, I suppose.
Now that's interesting. A month ago you'd have argued against that.
You're full of surprises. You said the reverse to me not long ago.
Either you've grown, or you've forgotten what you told me. Which is it?
That's a change of heart. I won't hold you to the old answer. Much.
Both answers can't be true. Or maybe they can, on different days.

## callback.contradict  personality=stern
You said the opposite before. Choose one, and stand by it.

## callback.contradict  personality=playful
Ha! Caught you! You said the complete opposite last time! Which one's the real you?

## callback.contradict  personality=thoughtful
You answered differently before. I don't think that's inconsistency. I think you're still deciding.

## callback.contradict  personality=meticulous
That contradicts what you told me earlier. I noted it at the time. I note it again.

## callback.contradict  personality=gentle
Oh, you feel differently now? That's all right. Hearts are allowed to change.

## callback.contradict  personality=proud
That's not what you said before. I respect someone who admits a change of mind. Do you admit it?

## callback.contradict  personality=curious
Ooh, you've switched sides! What changed? Tell me everything!

## callback.contradict  personality=reserved
Hm. Different from last time. ...Fine.

## callback.contradict  personality=fiery
What?! You said the opposite before! Make up your mind! No, wait, tell me why!

## callback.contradict  personality=steadfast
You told me otherwise before. A person can change their mind. Just change it honestly.

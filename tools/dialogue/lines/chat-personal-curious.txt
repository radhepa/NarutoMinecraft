# Small talk, part A: the answer to "How's your day?" for curious residents. Questions, rumours, how things work.
#! tags: personality=curious

## chat.personal  fn=reveal_character
Fascinating! I took apart my clock to see how it works. Now I know. It doesn't, any more.
I spent the morning figuring out why the bathhouse water stays hot. There's a whole furnace under there! Did you know?
Why do cats always sit on the one paper you're reading? I watched mine for an hour. I have theories. No answers.
I asked the fishmonger how far fish swim in a day. The fishmonger asked me to leave. Still, I'd like to know.
I've been timing how long the bread takes to rise in different rooms. The kitchen wins. I'm testing the attic tomorrow.
Did you know ants walk in a line because they follow a smell? I put a lemon peel across one line. Total chaos.
A traveller at the gate had shoes with metal on the bottom. Metal! For ice, apparently. Where do they have that much ice?
I've been trying to work out how the Hokage faces were carved. Ladders? Ropes? Earth jutsu? Nobody gives a straight answer.
My daughter asked how rain gets into the clouds. I said "it climbs." I need a better answer by dinnertime.
I spent an hour watching the potter. Did you know the wheel has to spin a certain speed or the bowl flops? Wonderful.
I read a whole scroll about bees. They dance to show each other where flowers are! I tried it. My neighbour stared.
I can't stop wondering what the Aburame feed their insects. Is it rude to ask? I asked. It was a bit rude.
I've started a list of every bird I hear in the morning. Seven so far. One of them sounds like a squeaky door.
I peeked into the scroll shop's back room. Shelves to the ceiling! The owner shooed me out, but I saw a map.
Have you ever wondered why ramen broth goes cloudy? It's the bones! I asked at Ichiraku. Got a lecture and a free egg.
I'm learning how lanterns are folded. There's a trick at the corners. I've ruined twelve sheets. Thirteen's lucky.
I measured how deep the river is under the second bridge. Deeper than my rope. I need a longer rope.
Someone told me the tea house has a secret menu. I've been ordering random things to find it. I've found indigestion.
I've been asking old residents what the village looked like when they were young. Everyone remembers a different Leaf.
I heard snow is just tiny ice flowers. I caught some on a black cloth last winter. They really are! Every one different.
I took a compass to the training grounds and it pointed slightly off near the big rock. Iron in the stone? Has to be.
I spent the evening wondering why we say "good morning" but not "good noon". Language is so odd.
I asked a medic how bones heal. Very long answer. I understood a third. I want the rest, though.
I've been comparing the dango shops. Shape, sweetness, chew. My notes are very serious. My teeth are very sore.
My son found a beetle with a blue shell. We spent the whole afternoon looking it up. Still not sure what it is.
I tried to find where the river starts. Followed it upstream until the wall. What's beyond? I'm dying to know.
I've been listening to the bells at the shrine. Each one rings a slightly different note. Who tuned them?
I spent an hour asking the carpenter how joints fit without nails. I think I owe the carpenter a lunch now.

I keep asking my sensei why hand signs work. The answer is always "practice". That's not an answer. That's homework.

The Leaf's water comes down from the hills in clay pipes. Some of them are older than the Academy. Imagine that.
The bathhouse, the bakery and the forge all share a chimney flue under the market street. That's why it's so warm there.

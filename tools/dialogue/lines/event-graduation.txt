# Academy graduation day, spring 7 and autumn 7: a public ceremony in the Academy yard. Kept neutral: the player
# may or may not be graduating today.
#! tags: event=graduation

## greet.event  fn=react
Graduation day! The Academy yard is full of proud families.
Morning, {player}. Did you see the new headbands? The yard's sparkling with them.
It's graduation today. Half the village is at the Academy gate.
Good day to you! The Academy's sending another class of Genin out into the world.
The bell rang for the ceremony a moment ago. I can hear the cheering from here.

## greet.event  stage=child  fn=react
Graduation day! Look at the big kids with their headbands! Someday that's us!
I'm going to watch the ceremony. My cousin's graduating. If my cousin can, anyone can.

## chat.event  fn=worldbuild
The Hokage comes to the yard and every new Genin is called by name. It takes all morning.
The families sit on benches along the yard wall. The front row is fought over like a market stall.
After the ceremony, the new Genin all go for ramen. Ichiraku's queue goes round the corner.
The headbands are blessed at the shrine the night before. That's an old Leaf custom.
I still have my headband from my own graduation. The cloth's faded, but the plate still shines.
The teachers pretend not to cry. They do every time.
The Academy holds graduation twice a year, spring and autumn. Two chances, for those who need a second.
A photographer sets up by the gate. Every family wants a picture with a new headband in it.
The new Genin are put into squads right after. That's when the real nerves start.
There's always one who trips on the steps. Nobody forgets it, nobody minds.

## chat.event  personality=warmhearted|gentle  fn=reveal_character
I made a basket of sweet buns for the new Genin. They're so nervous, they forget to eat.
I always cry at graduation, even when I don't know anybody. All those proud faces.

## chat.event  personality=stern|proud  fn=reveal_character
A headband is a beginning. The hard part starts tomorrow.
In my day, the exam was harder. Still, they look a capable lot.

## chat.event  personality=playful|fiery  fn=reveal_character
I threw my headband in the air at my graduation and it landed on the roof. True story.
Listen to them cheer! I want to run down there and cheer too.

## chat.event  rank=genin|chunin|jonin  fn=reveal_character
My sensei found me at my graduation and said, "Well. Let's see." I've never forgotten it.
Seeing a new class graduate makes me feel old. And I'm not old.

## chat.event  group=civilian  fn=reveal_character

## chat.event  job=teacher  fn=reveal_character

# Small talk, part A: the answer to "How's your day?" for imaginative residents. Daydreams, colours and odd similes.
#! tags: personality=imaginative

## chat.personal  fn=reveal_character
Dreamy. The morning fog made the whole village look like it was still being drawn. I didn't want it to finish.
I spent the afternoon deciding what colour Tuesdays are. I've settled on a soft green, like moss after rain.
I'm writing a story about a teapot that wants to be a kettle. It's going badly. The teapot is very stubborn.
I watched the lanterns float on the river and imagined each was a little boat carrying a wish to sea.
Today the clouds looked like a fleet of dumplings. I got hungry looking at the sky. Is that strange?
I painted the Hokage Rock at sunset. It came out purple. My teacher said it's wrong. I think it's honest.
My son says the shadows under the bridge are trolls. I've been making up their names. The big one's called Pebble.
I'm sure the old willow by the river is listening when you walk under it. I always say hello. Just in case.
The rain this morning sounded like a hundred tiny drummers on my roof. I lay in bed and conducted them.
I made up a song for the crows on my roof. They didn't join in. They're not ready.
I keep a box of buttons. Every one is a person I've imagined. That green one's a retired pirate.
I dreamt the Academy was made of rice crackers and the teachers were trying to eat their way out. I woke up hungry.
I've been sketching people's shoes at the market. Shoes tell you everything. Those sandals there are very tired.
The smell of the bakery at dawn is the colour orange. I can't explain it. It just is.
I've been building a tiny village out of pebbles in my garden. It has a mayor. It's a snail.
I wrote a poem about my cat. Seven verses. The cat was asleep for all of them.
The wind today kept trying to tell me something. I think it was "close the window". I did.
I imagine the river is a long silver cat that never sleeps. It purrs under the bridges at night.
I sewed stars onto my curtains so the night comes in even at noon. My husband says it's like living in a lantern.
I was supposed to buy carrots, but a sunset happened. I came home with no carrots and a very good mood.
I tell my children the stars are the windows of a village in the sky. Now they want to visit. I need a better ending.
My tea went cold while I watched a spider build a web. It looked like a tiny map of the whole village.
I've decided the fishmonger is secretly a retired sea captain. I'm building the whole story. Don't ruin it.
Today I found a stone shaped like a heart, a leaf shaped like a hand, and a cloud shaped like my aunt.
I hum while I work. The bread rises better. That's my theory, and I'm keeping it.
I painted a door on my bedroom wall. Sometimes, before sleep, I imagine where it goes. Last night: a library of the sea.
I've been inventing festivals nobody holds. The Festival of Lost Socks is my favourite. Very solemn.
The first snow of last year looked like the sky was shaking out a pillow. I think about that every autumn.
I made my daughter a kite with a dragon's face. Every time it dives, she screams. It's the happiest sound I know.

## chat.personal  rank=civilian  fn=reveal_character
I paint shop signs. Today's was a fish wearing a hat for the fishmonger. Didn't ask for the hat. Loves the hat.
I make paper lanterns. Every one I fold, I imagine whose window it'll hang in. It makes the work go quick.

## chat.personal  rank=!civilian  fn=reveal_character
I've been designing a genjutsu that only makes you smell bread. Useless in a fight. Wonderful on a cold morning.
On missions I imagine the trees are old giants watching us pass. Keeps me alert. And company, too.
My summons would be a moth if I had one. Quiet, soft, drawn to lights. I've thought about this a great deal.

## chat.personal  fn=worldbuild
The Kurama lane has paintings on every wall. Some look like they move if you don't stare. I love that street.
On the night of the moon viewing, people float paper boats on the river. Each one has a wish written on the sail.
Children here say the Hokage faces turn their heads at night to watch over the village. I half think so myself.

## chat.personal  fn=hook
I lost my sketchbook somewhere on the market street. It's full of people's shoes. I'd die if anyone read it.
A painting of mine went missing from the tea house wall. A purple Hokage Rock. Someone must love it. Or hate it.
There's a door painted on the wall behind the dango shop that wasn't there last week. I'd love to know who's done it.

Like a cup of tea with a cloud in it. Soft. Warm. A little foggy.
Blue, with a bit of yellow coming in. That's how today feels.

I've been naming the steam clouds over the bathhouse. That one's a whale. That one's my landlord, as a whale.
I wove a bit of red thread into every basket I sold today. Like a secret all my baskets share.
I tried to paint the sound of the shrine bell. It came out as a gold spiral. Close enough.

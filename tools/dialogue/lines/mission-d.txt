# -- lost_pet: {target} is the pet's name, {place} where it was last seen --------------------------------

## mission.d.lost_pet.brief  job=mission_desk
D-rank, lost pet. {client} hasn't seen {target} since yesterday. Last sighting near {place}. Pays {pay}.
A pet search for {client}. The animal answers to {target}, apparently. Try {place} first. {pay} on return.
{client} is offering {pay} for {target}, safe and sound. Bring a treat and patience. Mostly patience.

## mission.d.lost_pet.brief
You're the one from the Mission Desk? Thank goodness. {target} slipped out the back gate and never came home.
{target} always goes to {place} when there's a storm coming. Please, I've looked everywhere else.
It's {target}. My {target}. Small, scruffy, bites when frightened. Don't take it personally.

## mission.d.lost_pet.accept
You'll really look? Here, take this. It's {target}'s favourite dried fish.
Thank you. Call the name softly, it hates shouting.
Bless you. I'll leave the gate open and the bowl full.

## mission.d.lost_pet.decline
Oh. Of course. You must be busy with real missions.
Never mind. I'll keep walking the streets myself.
I understand. If you see {target} anyway, just... tell me?

## mission.d.lost_pet.progress
Any sign? Even a paw print?
Someone saw something small and scruffy by {place}. Could be.
Don't grab at it. Crouch down and let it come to you.

## mission.d.lost_pet.success
{target}! You're home! Look at you, all burrs and mud!
You found {target}! I could cry. I might cry. Thank you.
There's my troublemaker. Thank you, truly. Go and claim your pay.
Look, it remembers me! Thank you for bringing {target} back.

## mission.d.lost_pet.fail
Still nothing? I suppose that's it, then.
Maybe {target} found a new home. I hope it's a kind one.
Thank you for trying. Really. It's not your fault.

## mission.d.lost_pet.report  job=mission_desk
Pet returned. {client} sent a note. Most of it is just the word "thank you".
Another lost animal home. Logged. Wash your hands, would you?
Done, then. You'd be amazed how many of these we get. Pay's at the counter.

# -- weeding: {place} the field or garden, {count} the beds or rows --------------------------------------

## mission.d.weeding.brief  job=mission_desk
Weeding at {place}. {count} rows, by hand. {client} is paying {pay}. Bring gloves.
D-rank for {client}: clear the weeds from {place} before they choke the beans. {pay}.
Garden work at {place}. Not glamorous, but every Genin does it. {pay}.

## mission.d.weeding.brief
The weeds have taken {place} over. {count} beds of them. My back won't let me bend any more.
Pull them up by the root, mind, or they're back in a week. All {count} rows.
Not the little purple ones! Those are herbs. Everything else goes.

## mission.d.weeding.accept
Wonderful. The basket's by the shed. Fill it as many times as you need.
Good. Start at the far end, by the fence. It's worst there.
Thank you. I'll bring you barley tea at noon.

## mission.d.weeding.decline
Fine. The weeds and I will keep arguing on our own.
No one wants the weeding. I know. I know.
Pity. My knees were counting on you.

## mission.d.weeding.progress
That's the way. Get the roots, not just the tops.
You're faster than the last Genin. Much less complaining, too.
Half done already? Have a drink. It's hot work.

## mission.d.weeding.success
Look at that! I can see the soil again!
Every row clear. My vegetables thank you, and so do I.
Lovely work. Not a dandelion left standing.

## mission.d.weeding.fail

Half the beds are still green. I'll finish myself, I suppose.
That's not weeding, that's just stepping on things.

## mission.d.weeding.report  job=mission_desk
Weeding done. {client} says you can come back any spring.
Logged. There's dirt on the report form. There's always dirt on these.
Good. Garden missions build patience. Your sensei will tell you the same.

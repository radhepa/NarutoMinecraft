## chat.event  fn=worldbuild
At the shrine you ring the bell, bow twice, clap twice, and make your wish. Don't tell anyone what it is.
The Hokage speaks to the village from the Residence balcony every New Year. Short, usually. Everyone's cold.
The shrine sells fortunes on paper slips. If you get a bad one, tie it to the tree and leave it there.
The first sunrise of the year is best from the Hokage Rock. The climb is worth it.
My family eats long noodles on the last night, for a long life. Then sweet rice cakes in the morning.
The bell at the shrine rings a hundred and eight times before midnight. One for every worry.
The Academy children write their resolutions on strips of paper. Mine said "stop losing kunai." It didn't work.
New Year is the one night the shops stay open late. Everyone wants hot sake and sweet soup.
The shrine steps are icy. Hold the rail, the priests don't salt them well enough.
Everyone cleans their whole house the week before. It sweeps last year out the door.
The year's first meal should be cooked by someone you love. That's what my mother said, anyway.

## chat.event  fn=teach
Tonight nobody sleeps, so the tiredness comes late. You'll be fine until two, then you'll feel it.
If you're staying up for the sunrise, eat something after midnight. A meal keeps your legs going.
Go home and sleep after the first sunrise. Tomorrow is a holiday, but your bed doesn't know that.

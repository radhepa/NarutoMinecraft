# Conversation topics, the lighter ones (LIFE_TOGETHER.md §6): small talk, food, training, a compliment,
# village news, their hobby, and their opinion of someone. A personality's line wins over the general one.
# Where a personality dislikes a topic (training: gentle; a compliment: reserved, pragmatic; village news:
# reserved; about someone: stern), its line is the awkward reaction.

## talk.small_talk  fn=filler
Not much to report. The well bucket squeaks again, and nobody's oiled it.
Busy morning, slow afternoon. The usual.
Did you see the sky at dawn? Pink all the way to the Hokage Rock.

## talk.not_yet  fn=react  kind=cycle
Let's leave that for when we know each other better.
That's a conversation for another day, I think.
Ask me again in a while, {player}. Not yet.

## talk.opinion.dear  fn=reveal_character
Them? One of the best people I know. I'd do anything for them.
I'd walk through the rain to bring them soup. That's how I feel about them.
Don't you say a word against them. They're dear to me.

## talk.opinion.likes  fn=reveal_character
I like them. Good company, always on time.
They're all right. More than all right, honestly.
We get on well. They make me laugh.

## talk.opinion.neutral  fn=reveal_character
I don't really know them. We nod in the street.
Them? Can't say I've an opinion. Seem decent enough.
We've spoken once or twice. Nothing to tell.

## talk.opinion.cool  fn=reveal_character
We're not close. Let's leave it at that.
They and I don't see eye to eye. It's nothing dramatic.
I keep my distance. It's better for both of us.

## talk.opinion.dislikes  fn=reveal_character
I'd rather not talk about them. Or to them.
Honestly? I can't stand them. Don't tell anyone I said it.
If they're coming, I'm leaving. That's all I'll say.

## talk.small_talk.again  fn=filler  kind=cycle
We've done the weather already, {player}. The sky hasn't changed its mind.
Still the same day, still the same squeaky bucket.
## talk.small_talk.too_soon  fn=react  kind=cycle
Let's start simpler, shall we?
We hardly know each other. Hello is plenty for now.

# -- food -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## talk.food  fn=reveal_character

I'd eat dango every day if my teeth would let me.
Have you tried the eel by the second bridge? They brush it with sauce three times.
## talk.food.again  fn=filler  kind=cycle

If we keep talking about food, I'll have to go and buy some.
## talk.food.too_soon  fn=react  kind=cycle

## talk.food.adventurous  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food.meticulous  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food.steadfast  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food.reserved  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food.imaginative  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food.pragmatic  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food.curious  fn=reveal_character
How do they get the broth at Ichiraku so clear? I've asked. They just wink.
## talk.food.protective  fn=reveal_character
You're eating properly, aren't you? Not just ramen? ...Mostly ramen. I knew it.
## talk.food.gentle  fn=reveal_character
I like warm sweet potato in winter. I share the skins with the neighbour's dog.
## talk.food.proud  fn=reveal_character
My family's pickling recipe goes back five generations. Nothing in the market compares.
## talk.food.fiery  fn=reveal_character
RAMEN! Extra pork! Extra egg! Extra everything! Is there any other answer?
## talk.food.shy  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food.stern  fn=reveal_character

# -- training ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## talk.training  fn=reveal_character
I'm working on my footwork. Slow, but it's coming.
Training Ground 3 has the best posts. The bark's worn to the shape of a fist.
Every morning, a lap of the outer wall. Some mornings I even enjoy it.
## talk.training.again  fn=filler  kind=cycle
We've already compared bruises today, {player}.
More training talk? Go and actually train, then come back.
## talk.training.too_soon  fn=react  kind=cycle
I don't talk technique with people I've just met.
Let's leave training for when we've sparred once or twice.

## talk.training.warmhearted  fn=reveal_character
I mostly train so I can carry the shopping up the hill without stopping. It counts!
## talk.training.thoughtful  fn=reveal_character
Most mistakes in a spar happen in the head first. I train my thinking as much as my arms.
## talk.training.playful  fn=reveal_character
I train by racing the pigeons off the roofs. They win. For now.
## talk.training.adventurous  fn=reveal_character
I climb the cliff behind the falls. No ropes. Don't tell anyone. Want to come?
## talk.training.meticulous  fn=reveal_character
Three hundred strikes, logged. Then I check where the misses went and adjust.
## talk.training.steadfast  fn=reveal_character
Same drills every day. They're dull. That's why they work.
## talk.training.reserved  fn=reveal_character
I train alone. Early. Before anyone's watching.
## talk.training.imaginative  fn=reveal_character
I pretend the training post is a mountain troll. It helps more than you'd think.
## talk.training.pragmatic  fn=reveal_character
Train what you'll use. Fancy jutsu won't help if you can't run for an hour.
## talk.training.curious  fn=reveal_character
Why does a spinning kunai fly straighter? I threw two hundred to find out. Still not sure.
## talk.training.protective  fn=reveal_character
I practise guarding more than striking. Someone has to stand in front.
## talk.training.gentle  fn=react
Oh... I don't really like fighting talk. Could we talk about something else?
## talk.training.proud  fn=reveal_character
I train harder than anyone my age. That's not a boast. Ask the posts.
## talk.training.fiery  fn=reveal_character
A thousand kicks before breakfast! Then a thousand more! Want to join? You want to!
## talk.training.shy  fn=reveal_character
I practise throwing at night, when nobody can see me miss. I'm getting better. A little.
## talk.training.stern  fn=reveal_character
Train until the movement is boring. Then train it until it's yours.

## talk.compliment.steadfast  fn=deepen_bond

## talk.compliment.proud  fn=deepen_bond

## talk.compliment.fiery  fn=deepen_bond

## talk.compliment.shy  fn=deepen_bond

## talk.compliment.stern  fn=deepen_bond

# Stale food from the cupboard (HOME.md §3): a stale meal feeds a little less, a very stale one not at all, and
# never makes anyone ill. The player's own inner voice, or a visitor's remark. Light and funny, never gross.
#! kind: cycle
#! fn: react

## home.pantry.stale.milk
This milk has started to have opinions. Still drinkable. Just.
The milk smells like it's thinking about becoming cheese.
Slightly sour milk. Good for the bones, maybe. Less good for the face you're making.
## home.pantry.very_stale.milk
The milk has turned into yoghurt without asking anyone's permission.
This milk is no longer milk. It's a new and independent thing. Leave it in peace.
Pouring the milk makes a sound milk shouldn't make. Down the sink it goes.

## home.pantry.stale.onigiri
The onigiri's gone a little hard. Crunchy rice is still rice.
Yesterday's onigiri. The seaweed has given up, but the rice soldiers on.
This rice ball could double as a training weight. Chew slowly.
## home.pantry.very_stale.onigiri
This onigiri has turned to stone. You could practise on the target board with it.
The onigiri clonks on the plate. Maybe the sparrows will want it.
That rice ball is a doorstop now. A small, sad doorstop.

## home.pantry.stale.dango
The dango's gone chewy. Very chewy. Your jaw is getting a workout.
Stale dango. The sauce has set like varnish, but it's still sweet.
Day-old dango tastes a little like patience.
## home.pantry.very_stale.dango
The dango could bounce now. Go on, try it. ...It does bounce.
These dango are so hard they clack together like beads on a string.
Old dango, stiff as the stick it's on. Your teeth would rather you didn't.

## home.pantry.stale.bread
The bread's gone a bit dry. Toast it and nobody will ever know.
Crusty bread. Crustier than anyone intended.
This bread crunches when you squeeze it. That's not usual.
## home.pantry.very_stale.bread
The bread is hard enough to knock on. Someone might answer.
This loaf could be a training weapon. Not a good one.
The bread snaps instead of tearing. Crumble it for the birds on the balcony.

## home.pantry.stale.fruit
The fruit's gone soft and wrinkly. Sweeter for it, maybe.
A bit of a bruise on this one. Eat around it.
The mikan's skin is loose and baggy, as if it's wearing someone else's coat.
## home.pantry.very_stale.fruit
This fruit has gone so wrinkly it looks a hundred years old.
The fruit is very, very soft. It's had a long life. Let it rest.
This apple squashes if you so much as look at it. Time for a new one.

## home.pantry.stale.vegetables
The cabbage is a bit floppy. Soup will hide it.
These carrots bend. Carrots shouldn't bend.
The greens have wilted. They look how you feel after laps round the training ground.
## home.pantry.very_stale.vegetables
The carrots bend all the way round into circles now. Impressive. Not supper.
The cabbage has gone so limp it's lying down. Let it sleep.
These vegetables have given up entirely. Off to the compost with them, with thanks.

## home.pantry.stale.home_dish
Leftovers from a few days back. Fine warmed up, though the flavour's gone a bit flat.
This stew tastes more like a memory of stew.
Day-old curry is better than fresh, people say. Third-day curry is a gamble.
## home.pantry.very_stale.home_dish
The leftovers have been here so long they've forgotten what they were.
That home cooking is past saving. Better make a fresh pot.
Whatever this was, it was delicious once. Farewell, supper.

## home.pantry.stale.bento
The bento's rice is going hard at the edges. The pickles are fine, at least.
Yesterday's bento. The egg's gone a little rubbery, but it'll do.
A tired bento. Everything's still in its little section, holding on bravely.
## home.pantry.very_stale.bento
That bento has sat in the cupboard so long it belongs in a museum.
Even the pickles in this bento look disappointed.
That bento box needs a wash and a fresh start, in that order.

## home.pantry.stale.any
A bit past its best, this. Still food, though.
That's gone a little stale. It shouldn't have sat in the cupboard so long.
Not fresh, not ruined. Somewhere in between, like a grey afternoon.
## home.pantry.very_stale.any
That's been in the cupboard so long it's practically a lodger.
Very stale. If it had a name, it would be "Too Late".
Time to clear out the cupboard. Some of this is older than your kunai pouch.

# Reaching a level (BONDS.md §3): what a resident says the moment the bond rises. Levels rise at once, so a
# level-up can land mid-conversation.

# -- reaching a level: said by the resident, the moment it happens --------------------------------------
## bond.level.0  fn=deepen_bond
Nice to meet you. I'm sure we'll see more of each other.
Well, now we've met. That's a start.

## bond.level.1  fn=deepen_bond
I'll remember you now, {player}. You've got one of those faces.
Next time you pass, I'll know who's waving.

## bond.level.2  fn=deepen_bond
You know what? I like having you around. Come by whenever.
I think we're past "hello" now, {player}. Ask me anything.

## bond.level.3  fn=deepen_bond
We're friendly now, you and me. I'll keep you up on the news.
I'd call this a proper friendship starting. Don't you think?

## bond.level.4  fn=deepen_bond
You're my friend, {player}. I hope you know that. And you'd better come on my birthday.
Friends, then. Real ones. I'm glad.

## bond.level.5  fn=deepen_bond
I could tell you things now I wouldn't tell most people. That's new for me.
You've become a good friend, {player}. The kind I can talk to properly.

## bond.level.6  fn=deepen_bond
I don't have many close friends. I have one more now.
Somewhere along the way you became one of my people, {player}.

## bond.level.7  fn=deepen_bond
I trust you, {player}. Completely. I've never said that so easily.
There's nothing I'd keep from you now. Not really.

## bond.level.8  fn=deepen_bond
You're my best friend. I've known it a while. Now I've said it.
Best friends, {player}. Me and you. I like the sound of that.

## bond.level.9  fn=deepen_bond
Some people just fit, like a key in a lock. You fit, {player}.
I think we're kindred spirits. I'll keep a little something for you every day.

## bond.level.10  fn=deepen_bond
Whatever happens, for the rest of our lives, you'll have me. That's a bond for life.
I can't imagine the years ahead without you in them, {player}.

# leave the village; fights never kill.

# -- escort: {client} travels to {place} -------------------------------------------------------------

## mission.c.escort.brief  job=mission_desk
C-rank, escort. {client} travels to {place} and wants a squad alongside. Two days' walk. {pay}.
Escort duty for {client}, as far as {place}. Bandits have been seen on that road. Stay sharp. {pay}.
Your squad's first escort? {client} to {place}, door to door. Keep the client comfortable and alive to complain. {pay}.

## mission.c.escort.brief
I'm bound for {place} with my wares. I've heard stories about that road. I'd like some ninja with me.
You're my escort? You look young. No offence. The last escort was all beard and no sense.
Just see me to {place} in one piece. And my cart. Mostly my cart.

## mission.c.escort.accept
Good. We leave at dawn from the main gate. Don't be late.
Excellent. I walk slowly and complain often. You've been warned.
Right then. I'll pack an extra rice ball for each of you.

## mission.c.escort.decline
No? I'll wait for another squad. I'm not walking that road alone.
Pity. I'll hire someone from the next village, if I live that long.
Hm. Fine. The Mission Desk will find me someone braver.

## mission.c.escort.progress
How much further? My feet are writing letters of complaint.
Did you hear that? In the trees? No? Just a bird? Good.
We've made good time. At this pace, {place} by tomorrow evening.

## mission.c.escort.success
{place}! At last! I owe you a fine meal, all of you.
Not a scratch on me or the cart. You're welcome to escort me any time.
We're here. I didn't think we'd make it so smoothly. Tell the Leaf I said so.

## mission.c.escort.fail
I'll walk the rest myself. You've been more trouble than the road.
My cart's in a ditch and my wares are in a river. Some escort.
We turned back? After all that? I'll want words with your Mission Desk.

## mission.c.escort.report  job=mission_desk
Escort to {place}, complete. {client} wrote a letter. A long letter. Mostly good.
Logged. Your first road out of the village and back. How was it?
Good work. Escorts are the backbone of C-rank. Quiet, long, and worth every ryo.

# -- bandit_clearing: {count} bandits near {place}, for {client} ----------------------------------------

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.brief  job=mission_desk
C-rank. {count} bandits are holding the road near {place}. {client} wants them gone. Drive them off or bring them in. {pay}.
Bandit trouble at {place}. Thieves, not shinobi, as far as we know. {count} of them. Careful anyway. {pay}.
{client} has lost three carts this month near {place}. Clear the bandits, bring back what you can. {pay}.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.brief
They come out of the trees at {place}. {count}, maybe more. They took my whole harvest.
We can't take our goods to market. Nobody can. Not with those bandits on the road.
I'm not asking you to hurt them. Just make them leave. And maybe bring back my rice.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.accept
Thank you. The bend by the twin pines is where they hide.
Bring back what you can. Even half the rice would carry us through winter.
Good. Be careful. They're cowards, but cowards in a crowd are bold.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.decline
Then the road stays closed, and the market goes hungry.
I see. I'll ask the Desk to post it again.
No one wants to face them. I don't blame you, but I hoped.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.progress
Did you find their camp? Are they still there?
They say one of them has a club the size of a log. Mind that one.
Fewer on the road this week. Something's working.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.success
The road's open! We'll be at market by the weekend!
You brought back my rice! And my cart! And... my neighbour's goat?
No more bandits. I'll sleep tonight for the first time in a month.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.fail
They're still out there. Worse, they're angry now.
You let them go? All of them? With my rice?
The road's no safer than it was. I'll wait for a stronger squad.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.report  job=mission_desk
Bandits cleared from {place}. Those you brought in are with the police. Well done.
Logged. {client} sent a sack of rice to the Desk. We're keeping it.
Good. Remember this one: you won without hurting more than you had to. That's the job.

# -- far_delivery: {item} to {target} at {place}, a town past the border -----------------------------------

## mission.c.far_delivery.brief  job=mission_desk
Long-distance delivery: {item} to {target}, all the way to {place}. Four days there and back. {pay}.
C-rank courier work. {client} needs {item} in {target}'s hands at {place}. Sealed. Don't open it. {pay}.
A far run for {client}: {place}, past the river crossing. The parcel is {item}. Pack for rain. {pay}.

## mission.c.far_delivery.brief
This {item} has to reach {target} in {place}. It can't go with the merchants. Too many hands.
I'd go myself, but my knees and that road don't get along. Please, {place}, and quickly.
Give it to {target} and no one else. If {target} isn't there, bring it home.

## mission.c.far_delivery.accept
Good. Here. Keep it dry and keep it close.
Thank you. {target} will know it's from me by the knot.
Safe travels. And bring back news of {place}, if you can.

## mission.c.far_delivery.decline
It's a long way, I know. I'll find someone.
Then it waits. Some things are worth waiting for.
No? Hm. The Desk will have another squad soon.

## mission.c.far_delivery.progress
Is it still sealed? Good. Good.
How far to {place} now? Two more days, maybe less.
Rest at the inn by the ford. They know the Leaf there.

## mission.c.far_delivery.success
All the way from the Leaf? For me? Thank {client} for me. Thank you, too.
It's here, and it's sealed. You've no idea what this means.
{target} has it. I can stop worrying now. Thank you.

## mission.c.far_delivery.fail
It's opened. The seal's broken. This is no use to anyone now.
You lost it? On the road? Where on the road?
Too late. {target} left {place} two days ago.

## mission.c.far_delivery.report  job=mission_desk
Delivery to {place}, confirmed. A thank-you letter came back by hawk this morning.
Logged. Long road, that one. You've earned a bath and a hot meal.
Good. Courier work across the border is how the Leaf keeps its friends. Remember that.

# -- bandit_clearing: a camp robbing the farms. Slots: client (farmer), camp, lookout, hostages ----

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.title
Clear the Bandit Camp

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.brief  job=mission_desk  fn=quest,guide
C-rank. A bandit camp at {camp} has been robbing the outer farms. {client} brought the complaint. Clear it out. {pay}.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.brief  fn=quest,guide
They come down from {camp} every few nights. Took my seed rice, my neighbour's ox, half the village's tools.
There's a lookout at {lookout}. Get past that and the rest are in the hollow.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.accept
Thank you. I'll tell the other farms help is coming. They'll sleep tonight.
Good. Be careful. They're rough, and there are more of them than you'd think.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.decline
Then we keep sitting up with pitchforks every night. Until something happens.
No? We'll bar the doors and hope. It hasn't worked yet.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.progress
Did you see the lookout? Are they still up there?
Smoke from the hollow. They're cooking. That means they're all in.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.success
The camp's empty and our tools are back. You've given the farms their nights back.
Is that my ox? It is! You found my ox! Thank you, all of you.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.fail
They're still up there. We'll keep the lamps lit and the dogs loose.
It didn't work. They'll be angrier now. I'm not blaming you. I'm just afraid.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.report  job=mission_desk
Bandit camp cleared. The farms outside the wall have sent a thank-you in the form of a very large radish. Logged.
C-rank done, everyone back on their own feet. That's the result I like best. Paid.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.twist.hostages  fn=quest,react
There are people tied up by the fire. Farmhands. Get them clear before the fighting reaches them.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.twist.leader_flees  fn=quest,react
The leader's running! Straight for the hills, leaving the rest behind. After them!

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.twist.desperate_farmers  fn=quest,react
These aren't bandits. Look at their hands, their boots. Farmers, from a village that burned. Starving.
They're thin as rakes and fighting with hoes. Whatever they've done, these are farmers, not raiders.

## mission.c.bandit_clearing.choice.farmers
Starving farmers, not hardened thieves. Talk them down and show mercy, or arrest them all?

# -- bear: on the outer training grounds. Slots: client (groundskeeper), ground, honey, beast -----

## mission.c.bear.title
Bear on the Outer Training Grounds

## mission.c.bear.brief  job=mission_desk  fn=quest,guide
C-rank. A bear has moved into {ground} and won't leave. {client} wants it gone before the squads come back. {pay}.

## mission.c.bear.brief  fn=quest,guide
Big, brown, and sitting in the middle of {ground} like it pays rent. Nobody can train with that out there.
Move it on, back to the hills. Hurt it only if you must. It's just a bear doing bear things.

## mission.c.bear.accept
Good. Don't run if it charges. Or do. I'm not a bear expert.
Thank you. I'll keep everyone off the grounds till you're back.

## mission.c.bear.decline
Then the squads train in the village yards for another week. Crowded and grumpy.
No? Fine. The bear doesn't mind waiting. It's very patient.

## mission.c.bear.progress
Is it still by the target posts? It likes the target posts.
Claw marks on the fence. Fresh ones. It's close.

## mission.c.bear.success
Gone, back to the hills, and nobody hurt. Including the bear. Good.
The grounds are clear. The squads can come back tomorrow. Thank you.

## mission.c.bear.fail
Still there. Still eating the straw dummies. We'll try again.
It won't budge. Come back when you've rested.

## mission.c.bear.report  job=mission_desk
Bear moved on from the outer grounds. The training squads are delighted. The bear, less so. Logged.
C-rank, done. No one mauled. I like a report with no hospital forms attached.

## mission.c.bear.twist.honey_trail  fn=quest,react
{client}'s beehives at {honey}. A bear will follow honey anywhere. Even away from here.

## mission.c.bear.twist.cubs  fn=quest,react
Cubs in the bushes. Two of them. And their mother knows exactly how close you are.

## mission.c.courier_relay.report  job=mission_desk

## mission.c.courier_relay.twist.interceptors  fn=quest,react

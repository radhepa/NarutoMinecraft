# == food ==========================================================================================
## talk.food  fn=reveal_character
Rice balls with salted plum inside. The sour makes my whole face scrunch up, and I love it.
Hot roasted sweet potato from the cart by the gate. The paper bag's half the fun.
Anything grilled on a stick. If it's on a stick, I'll try it.
I could live on the miso ramen at Ichiraku. I might already be.
Tamagoyaki, sweet, not salty. People fight about this. I'm right.
Chestnuts in autumn. Burnt fingers every year and I never learn.
Shaved ice with strawberry syrup. Summer isn't summer without it.
Cold noodles on a hot day, with a lot of spring onion.
Fresh tofu from the tofu maker by the canal. It's still warm in the morning.
Taiyaki, filled right to the tail. The cheap stall skimps on the tail.
Mochi at New Year. Toasted until it puffs up and cracks.
Grilled corn with soy sauce at the festival. I always get it on my chin.
## talk.food  stage=teen|adult  fn=reveal_character
A good bowl of miso soup after a long shift. Simple as that.
Pickles. My own. Three years in the cellar and they're finally right.
Grilled eel with a cup of warm tea, by the river, when the work's done.
Whatever my neighbour brings over in the evening. I never ask, I just eat.
Salted salmon on rice. It's what I ate before every mission, and it's never failed me.

## talk.food.warmhearted  stage=teen|adult  fn=reveal_character
Hot pot. One big pot, everyone's chopsticks in it, nobody leaves hungry. That's food.
## talk.food.thoughtful  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food.playful  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food.adventurous  fn=reveal_character
I want to taste the sea. Not fish. The actual sea. People say it's salty. I want to check.
## talk.food.meticulous  fn=reveal_character
Rice should be washed three times, soaked for half an hour, and never, ever stirred.
## talk.food.steadfast  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food.reserved  fn=reveal_character
Plain rice. Quietly.
## talk.food.imaginative  fn=reveal_character
If I could eat a colour, I'd eat the pink of a sunset. It'd taste like peaches, I think.
## talk.food.pragmatic  fn=reveal_character
Rice keeps, beans keep, dried fish keeps. Anything that keeps is good food.
## talk.food.curious  fn=reveal_character
Why does bread rise? Why doesn't rice? I asked the baker and got flour on my nose.
## talk.food.protective  fn=reveal_character
Never eat the mushrooms in the forest unless someone who knows checks them. Promise me.
## talk.food.gentle  fn=reveal_character
I share my lunch with the birds by the canal. They like the crusts best.
## talk.food.proud  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food.fiery  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food.shy  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food.stern  fn=reveal_character

## talk.food  stage=child  fn=reveal_character

# == training ======================================================================================
## talk.training  fn=reveal_character
I'm learning to throw with my weaker hand. It's humbling. Very humbling.
I run the stairs up the Hokage Rock. All of them. Then I lie at the top for a while.
Tree climbing without hands is harder than they make it look.
I've been working on my balance on the fence posts by the river. I fall in a lot.
Chakra control's my weak point. I can't keep a leaf on my forehead for more than a minute.
My hand signs are getting faster. Still not fast enough to show off.
I practise my throws at the old stump behind the Academy. It's full of holes now.
Footwork, footwork, footwork. My teacher won't let me learn anything else till it's right.
I did a hundred sit-ups this morning. Ninety of them were good ones.
I've started training at dawn. The training grounds are empty and the air's cold.
Water walking. I'm wet up to my knees most days.
## talk.training  stage=teen|adult  fn=reveal_character
I train before my shift. If I leave it till after, I never do it.
My knees aren't what they were. I train smarter now, not harder.
I've been drilling the new Genin on the posts. They remind me how bad I used to be.
I keep up the old drills from my Academy days. Muscle remembers what the head forgets.

## talk.training.warmhearted  fn=reveal_character
I train with my little cousin. I let them win sometimes. They know, but they like it anyway.
## talk.training.thoughtful  fn=reveal_character
I read about a technique before I try it. Twice. Then I try it and it goes wrong anyway.
## talk.training.playful  fn=reveal_character
I turned kunai practice into a game. Hit the target, get a sweet. I'm getting very good. And full.
## talk.training.adventurous  fn=reveal_character

## talk.training.meticulous  fn=reveal_character

## talk.training.steadfast  fn=reveal_character
Every day. Rain or snow. Missing one day makes missing the next one easy.
## talk.training.reserved  fn=reveal_character

## talk.training.imaginative  fn=reveal_character
I train to music in my head. Kicks on the drumbeat. My teacher says it shows, in a good way.
## talk.training.pragmatic  fn=reveal_character
Train what keeps you alive first. Running, hiding, climbing. Then the flashy things.
## talk.training.curious  fn=reveal_character
Did you know your feet stick better to bark than to stone when you mould chakra? I tested it.
## talk.training.protective  fn=reveal_character
I practise catching people when they fall. Someone always falls.
## talk.training.gentle  fn=react

## talk.training.proud  fn=reveal_character
I set my targets further back than anyone else. If I'm going to miss, I'll miss something hard.
## talk.training.fiery  fn=reveal_character
I ran ten laps of the village yesterday! I'm doing eleven today! Then twelve! Forever!
## talk.training.shy  fn=reveal_character
I found a corner of Training Ground 3 behind the trees. Nobody goes there. It's my spot.
## talk.training.stern  fn=reveal_character
Basics. Every single day. Those who skip them are the ones who get hurt.

## talk.training  stage=child  fn=reveal_character

# == village news ==================================================================================
## talk.village_news  fn=worldbuild

Somebody's cat climbed the bell tower and won't come down. It's been two days.
The lantern maker's preparing for the festival already. The workshop's full of paper.
There's a new stall selling candied fruit on market street. The queue goes round the corner.
The river's high after the rain. The stepping stones by the mill are under water.
A flock of cranes landed in the rice fields by the wall. Everyone went out to look.
The bridge by the bathhouse is getting new planks. Mind the loose one in the meantime.
Someone left a basket of persimmons at the shrine gate. Nobody knows who.
The kennel's training a new litter. You can hear them barking from the market.
The Hokage Rock's being cleaned. Somebody's hanging off a rope, scrubbing a nose.
The fireworks for the festival came in on a cart this morning. Very carefully.
There's a puppet show coming to the square on the weekend. With real music.
## talk.village_news  stage=teen|adult  fn=worldbuild

They're building a new house by the canal. Nobody knows whose it is yet. Everyone's guessing.

# == their hobby ===================================================================================
## talk.their_hobby  fn=reveal_character
I collect feathers. I've got one from a hawk. Well, I think it's a hawk.
I fold paper into animals. My frogs can jump. My cranes can't fly.
I keep a little notebook of every bird I've seen in the village. Forty-two so far.
I've been learning the flute. Badly. The dogs on my street howl along.
I press flowers between the pages of old books. The library doesn't know.
I build little boats from bark and race them in the canal.
I've started drawing maps of the village from the rooftops. The streets look different from up there.
I juggle. Three balls, sometimes four. Never eggs. Not since the last time.
I spin tops. I've got one that hums when it spins. It's my favourite.
I watch the clouds and name them. That one's called Lumpy.
I sketch the people at the tea house when they're not looking. Don't tell them.

I keep bees on the roof. My neighbours are nervous. My honey is excellent.

# Small talk, part A: the answer to "How's your day?" for reserved residents. Few words, dry, private.
#! tags: personality=reserved

## chat.personal  fn=reveal_character
Quiet. I fixed a shelf. It's level now.
Read a book. Drank tea. Nobody knocked. Ideal.
I went to the festival meeting. Said nothing. Volunteered for lanterns. Lanterns don't talk.
My sister visited. Talked for four hours. I said "mm" eleven times. She thinks it went well.
Fine. I found a bench by the river nobody else uses. I'm not telling you which.
I grow bonsai. One tree, nine years. It's nearly the shape I want.
I don't like crowds. Market Day is a test of character. I passed. Barely.
I fed the koi at the shrine pond. They don't ask questions. Good company.
I painted my door. Same colour as before. Nobody noticed. That was the point.
My neighbour waves every morning. I've started waving back. Took me two years.
I play the flute at night, quietly. If you've heard it, you haven't. Understood?
I went to the tea house and sat at the corner table. Someone else was in it. I went home.
I keep a diary. Three lines a day. Today's: rain, rice, shelf.
My wife does the talking for both of us. It's a good arrangement. I do the dishes.
I like the hour before dawn. The village is mine then. Briefly.
Somebody asked me to a party. I said I had plans. My plans were a book.
I repaired a clock today. It ticks now. Satisfying, in a small way.
I carve small animals from offcuts. A fox today. Gave it to a child who was crying. Don't spread that around.
I walked the long way home to avoid a conversation. It started raining. Worth it.
I sat on the roof and watched the clouds. The Nara do it too. They have the right idea.
My brother's always telling me to "open up". I opened a window. It's a start.
Not much. I like it that way.
I learned to make one dish properly. Grilled mackerel. I make it every night. I'm not bored.
I bought a new teacup. Plain grey. I like it more than I should.
I sweep the shrine steps at dawn. Nobody's ever seen me do it. Keep it that way.
I've been asked to give a speech at my cousin's wedding. I'm considering moving villages.
A stray cat sleeps on my windowsill. We have an understanding. Neither of us mentions it.
I tried the new tea house. Too loud. Good tea, though. I'll go when it rains.
I keep pressed flowers in my books. Each one's a day I liked. There aren't many. They're good ones.

## chat.personal  rank=civilian  fn=reveal_character
I bind books for the library. Silent work. Paste, thread, cloth. Best job in the Leaf.
I run the night stall at the bathhouse. People come in tired and leave quiet. Suits me.

## chat.personal  rank=!civilian  fn=reveal_character
Back from a mission. It went as planned. That's all you'll get from me.
My squad talks the whole walk out. I take rear guard. Further from the talking.
I trained alone at Ground 3 at dawn. Just me and the stumps. They don't chatter either.

## chat.personal  fn=worldbuild
The library has a reading room on the top floor. No talking allowed. It's the best room in the village.
The Aburame don't make small talk. I've always respected that.
There's a stone bench at the back of the shrine where people go to be left alone. Everyone knows. Nobody says.

My flute's missing a cap. Small brass thing. Probably fell by the river. I won't ask twice.
A lantern on my lane keeps going out. I'd fix it, but the lantern-keeper is chatty. You go.

# Small talk about work: the trade from the inside, its customers, tools and prices in ryo.
# One block per job; the heading's fn says what the lines do.

# -- ramen cook ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=ramen_cook  fn=worldbuild
The broth goes on at four in the morning: pork bones, kelp and a lot of patience. By the lunch rush it has had eight hours, and you can taste every one.
Noodles get cut fresh twice a day. Come at three and you get the second batch, which between us is the better one.
Running a noodle counter is nine parts washing up and one part cooking. Nobody mentions that when you pick up the ladle.
Everyone thinks the secret is the broth. It's the soy base underneath, and that pot never leaves my sight.
You can spot a Jonin at the counter: they eat fast, say nothing and always take the stool facing the door.
Rain is the best thing that can happen to a ramen shop. Nobody wants cold rice when the street turns into a river.

## chat.job  job=ramen_cook  fn=reveal_character
I burned my first hundred pots of broth. The hundred-and-first I served, and the customer came back the next day. That was enough.
The third stool wobbles and I won't fix it. The regular who sits there says it keeps a person humble.
I've fed three generations of Genin at this counter. They come back as Jonin and still order the same bowl.

## chat.job  job=ramen_cook  fn=commerce
A plain bowl is six ryo, miso seven, and the special is whatever I was brave enough to try this morning.
Ramen puts a little chakra back in you. Ask any Genin who comes in straight off the training ground.
I take part-time help at the counter: scallions, bowls, the mop. Seventy ryo a shift, if nothing gets broken.
Extra pork is two ryo, and I've seen customers argue over it like it was a border treaty.

## chat.job  job=ramen_cook  fn=hook
The boat that brings the pink swirl fish cake has been late three days running. If it misses tomorrow, the bowls go out bare.
Somebody leaves exact change on the end stool before we open, every morning, with the shutter still locked. I would love to know how.

# -- cook ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=cook  fn=worldbuild
A good kitchen in the Leaf runs on rice. Two pots on the go from dawn, and if the second one burns, the whole lunch rush burns with it.
Shinobi eat at odd hours. I keep a pot of miso warm until midnight for whoever comes back late from the gate.
The tea houses buy my pickles by the jar. Plum, radish and ginger, salted for a month in the cellar.
Onigiri are a cook's handshake. Square ones say the cook was in a hurry; round ones say they cared.
Mackerel on Market Day, tofu the rest of the week, and eel only when the river is generous.

## chat.job  job=cook  fn=reveal_character
I learned to cook in my grandmother's kitchen by being told I was wrong every day for six years. She was right every time.
I wanted to be a shinobi once. Now I feed them, which I think is the better half of the deal.
I taste everything twice. Once for salt and once to be sure I wasn't fooling myself the first time.

## chat.job  job=cook  fn=commerce
A bento is eight ryo: rice, an egg roll, pickles, and fish if the boats came in.
A full meal steadies the legs. Eat before a long night and you won't stagger home half as badly.
If you have a cooking pot at home, buy the rice dry from the grocer. It works out at about a third of the price of mine.

## chat.job  job=cook  fn=hook
My big iron pan went missing from the back step. Iron that heavy doesn't wander off on its own.
Someone keeps ordering a bento for a name nobody here knows, and paying in advance. The box always gets collected.

# -- grocer --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=grocer  fn=worldbuild
The cabbages come in through the main gate before sunrise. If the cart's late, the whole market eats less salad.
Rice sells by the scoop, miso by the cup, and soy by the bottle you bring yourself. Forget the bottle and you pay for one.
Shinobi buy the strangest lists: twenty eggs, a sack of flour and eighty sheets of plain paper. I've stopped asking.
Spring is bamboo shoots, summer is cucumbers, autumn is chestnuts and winter is whatever kept in the cellar.
Half my shop is on shelves and half is in the storeroom behind, sorted by the date it came in. Oldest sells first.

## chat.job  job=grocer  fn=reveal_character
I can tell a good melon by knocking on it. My father could tell by looking. I'm still working on that.
Ten years behind this counter and I've never once taken a day off on Market Day. It feels like skipping my own birthday.
I keep a slate of who owes me what. Most of the names on it are Academy children who'll pay me back as Genin.

## chat.job  job=grocer  fn=commerce
An onigiri is three ryo and a bag of rice is twelve. If you cook for yourself, the rice goes further.
I need someone to stock shelves on busy mornings. Forty ryo a shift, fifty if you can carry a sack of rice up the stairs.
Dried fish keeps for a mission. Fresh fish keeps until noon. Buy accordingly.

## chat.job  job=grocer  fn=hook
Every third night a sack of chestnuts goes missing from the storeroom, and the window is far too small for a thief.
The farmer who grows my radishes hasn't come in for a week. The gate guards say the cart never passed.

# -- fishmonger ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=fishmonger  fn=worldbuild
River fish in the morning, sea fish twice a week on the coastal cart, and the eel are only for those who order a day ahead.
Ice is the dearest thing on this stall. A water-style Genin earns good pocket money keeping my boxes cold in summer.
You learn to read a fish by its eye. Clear and it swam this morning; cloudy and it's been travelling.
The stall smells of the river no matter how often I scrub it. My family says I smell of it too.
Mackerel goes salted for the Akimichi, filleted for the tea houses and whole for anyone who knows what they're doing.

## chat.job  job=fishmonger  fn=reveal_character
My hands haven't been warm since I was twelve. Twenty years of cold water and colder fish.
I was the slowest gutter in my family. Now I'm the fastest on the street, because I was too stubborn to stay slow.
The old neighbour upstairs buys one sardine a day for a cat, and pays in exact coins. I always slip in a second.

## chat.job  job=fishmonger  fn=commerce
Sardines are a ryo apiece, trout four, and eel I won't quote until I see what the river gives me.
Fish grilled with salt is about the best thing a tired shinobi can eat. Cheaper than ramen, too.
I'll trade you a fillet for an hour of carrying boxes. Fish now, or forty ryo at closing.

## chat.job  job=fishmonger  fn=hook
The drying racks behind the stall were picked clean two nights running. Whatever took the fish left the nets tied neatly.
The coastal cart is two days late, and the driver has never once been late in eleven years.

# -- farmer --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=farmer  fn=worldbuild
The fields outside the wall feed half the village. Every cabbage you eat came past the gate guards first.
We plant rice in spring with the whole family in the paddy, knee-deep, singing badly to keep time.
Shinobi trample the edges of the east field cutting across to the training grounds. We put up a fence; they jump it.
A farmer's day starts before the shrine bell and ends when the light goes. The village sleeps on our schedule, not the other way round.
The best fertilizer is the bath house's old straw. The bath keeper gives it free if we cart it ourselves.

## chat.job  job=farmer  fn=reveal_character
I've never been past the far hills and never wanted to. Everything I need grows on four fields.
I name the scarecrows after Hokage. Out of respect, mind you. The crows have never shown them any.
My grandfather planted the persimmon tree by the gate road. I eat the first one every autumn standing under it.

## chat.job  job=farmer  fn=commerce
Pulling weeds pays forty ryo a morning, and you get to take home whatever's bruised.
I sell straight from the cart on Market Day. A cabbage is two ryo; the grocer sells the same one for three.

## chat.job  job=farmer  fn=hook
Something has been digging up the sweet potatoes on the far field, neat rows of holes, and not a footprint anywhere.
The gate wants new paperwork for every cart now. My cabbages wait in the sun while the stamp goes round.
The irrigation ditch by the old mill keeps silting up overnight. Someone's damming it upstream, or something is.

# -- carpenter -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=carpenter  fn=worldbuild
A good Leaf house is joined, not nailed. Cut the joints true and the beams hold each other up for a hundred years.
Cedar for the frames, cypress for the baths, and pine for anything a shinobi is likely to break.
Half our work is repair. Training accidents, a stray paper bomb, a Genin who missed the roof they were jumping to.
Tatami frames are cut to one size across the whole village. Your room is measured in mats, not in feet.
The carpenters' lane wakes up to the sound of planes on cedar. You can find the yard with your eyes shut.

## chat.job  job=carpenter  fn=reveal_character
I keep my first chisel on a nail above the bench. It's blunt, chipped and worth more to me than the rest of the rack.
I count the rings of every board before I cut it. Feels rude not to know how old something is before you saw it in half.
I can't sit on a chair without checking the joints. My family has learned to stop asking why I'm on the floor.

## chat.job  job=carpenter  fn=commerce
A low table runs about sixty ryo. A full set for a flat is a week's work for a Genin, but it lasts a lifetime.
Furniture sells back at half what you paid, so buy the piece you actually want the first time.
Bring your own planks and cloth to a workbench and you can build simple pieces yourself. Just don't ask me to fix them after.

## chat.job  job=carpenter  fn=hook
A whole stack of seasoned cedar vanished from the yard overnight. Too heavy for one person to carry, and nobody heard a cart.
The landlady on the corner wants the roof fixed before the rains, and pays in pickles. I'll need a pair of hands.

# -- tailor --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=tailor  fn=worldbuild
Mission clothes come back to me in three states: torn, scorched or something I'd rather not ask about.
Every Genin squad orders new outfits within a month of graduating. Then they ruin them within a month of that.
Shinobi cloth is double-stitched at the knees and elbows. Civilians ask why; shinobi never do.
Festival week is my busiest. Everyone wants a yukata, and nobody remembers to order it until three days before.
The dye vats out the back run indigo, rust and dark green. Somehow every clan wants a shade that's just slightly different.

## chat.job  job=tailor  fn=reveal_character
I can guess a person's measurements across a crowded street. It makes me terrible company at festivals.
My mother sewed for the old Hokage's household. She said a hem tells you whether the wearer runs or walks.
I've stuck myself with a needle so many times my thumb has a callus shaped like a thimble.

## chat.job  job=tailor  fn=commerce
Rank outfits are issued, but a variant of your own runs a hundred ryo for the simple cut and up to six hundred for the fine one.
A patch is five ryo, a new sleeve twenty, and a whole new jacket if you've been setting yourself on fire again.

## chat.job  job=tailor  fn=hook
Someone ordered a full mourning set, paid in advance, and never came to collect it. The name on the slip isn't anyone in the village.
My bolt of good festival silk is gone. The shutter was locked, and the only thing out of place is a single loose thread on the step.

# -- smith ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=smith  fn=worldbuild
A kunai is three things: the point, the balance and the ring. Get the ring wrong and you can't hang it on a finger to throw.
The forge never goes fully cold. I bank the coals at night and wake them at dawn with the bellows.
Shuriken are punched from sheet steel, then filed by hand. The filing is where a cheap star and a good one part ways.
Most of my steel comes from the quarry road and the rest from what Genin drag back from bandit camps.
A tempered blade is the colour of straw at the edge and blue at the spine. Too far either way and it's a paperweight.

## chat.job  job=smith  fn=reveal_character
I lost the tip of a finger to my first anvil. The anvil's still here and so am I, so I call it even.
I don't like making swords. A kunai builds a fence or saves a life; a sword only has the one use.
My apprentice years were spent pumping bellows until my arms shook. I'd do it all again.

## chat.job  job=smith  fn=commerce
Kunai run five ryo for practice grade and up to forty for proper steel. Shuriken are cheaper, but you lose more of them.
Work a few shifts at the forge, eighty ryo each, and I'll start teaching you what the anvil is for.
Steel is ten ryo a bar from the rack, if you've got a forge at home and the nerve to use it.
A blade you forge yourself can come out better than my shop stock, if your hammer is honest.

## chat.job  job=smith  fn=hook
Someone is buying up all the senbon wire in the market. A whole spool at a time, from every stall.

# -- florist -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=florist  fn=worldbuild
Every flower in the shop means something. Hand someone yellow chrysanthemums by mistake and you'll spend the evening explaining.
The cut flowers come in before dawn and go into cold water within the hour, or they sulk all day.
Hospital visitors buy small bunches, nothing with a strong smell. The nurses have rules about that.
Funerals take white, graduations take something bright, and apologies take whatever the shop has most of.
Camellias drop their heads whole. Some families won't have them in the house, and I never argue.

## chat.job  job=florist  fn=reveal_character
I talk to the plants in the greenhouse. They don't answer, which makes them better listeners than most customers.
My favourite is the plain white bellflower. It never sells, so I keep one on the counter for myself.
I could never be a medic. I cried for an hour when the hydrangeas froze.

## chat.job  job=florist  fn=commerce
A single stem is three ryo, a small bunch twelve, and a proper arrangement starts at thirty.
I take on help for watering and wrapping. Fifty ryo a shift and you go home smelling far better than a ramen cook.
A potted plant brightens a flat for years. It counts as furniture, and it's the only furniture that grows.

## chat.job  job=florist  fn=hook
A bouquet of red spider lilies turns up on the step every week, unsigned. We don't even sell them.

# -- teacher -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=teacher  fn=worldbuild
Lessons run nine till noon on weekdays. The afternoons are for marking, and the evenings are for wondering where the afternoon went.
The first term is hand signs until the children dream of them. Rat, Ox, Tiger, over and over, until their fingers stop arguing.
Every class has one child who can't sit still, one who won't speak and one who will be Hokage. Usually they're the same child.
The target posts in the yard have been replaced six times this year. The children are getting better, which is the problem.
History is the lesson they groan at, and it's the one that matters most once they're out past the wall.

## chat.job  job=teacher  fn=reveal_character
I keep every graduation photograph on the staff room wall. I can name every face, even the ones who didn't make it to Chunin.
I failed the Academy exam my first year. I tell every class that, and it's the only lesson they never forget.
Some nights I correct papers by lantern light until the ink runs out. My spouse says I'm married to the red brush.

## chat.job  job=teacher  fn=hook
Someone keeps leaving perfect answers to tomorrow's test on the blackboard before dawn. In chalk, in my own handwriting.
One of my pupils has stopped coming to afternoon practice. The family says the child leaves every day at noon.

## chat.job  job=teacher  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character
A Clone that falls over is still a Clone. I mark the effort first and the jutsu second.
The swing by the old tree out front has outlasted four instructors. We'd riot if anyone took it down.
Parents ask me whether their child will make Genin. I tell them honestly: the child decides that, not me.

# -- medic ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=medic  fn=worldbuild
A medic's chakra has to be thinner than thread. Push too hard and you heal the wound and bruise everything around it.
Mission season fills the third floor. Sprains, burns, a kunai in the shoulder, and one Genin a week who tried to catch a paper bomb.
Every medic learns anatomy from scrolls first, then fish, then each other. In that order, thankfully.
We revive anyone brought in knocked out, no questions asked and no bill sent. The village pays for that, and it's worth it.
A field kit holds bandages, a needle, a splint and a soldier pill. Most of the job is the bandages.

## chat.job  job=medic  fn=reveal_character
I fainted at the sight of blood in my first week. My instructor made me hold the bowl for a month. I don't faint anymore.
I count the patients I've sent home, not the ones I couldn't. It's the only way to sleep after a night shift.
My hands shake when I'm not working. As soon as there's a wound in front of me, they go perfectly still.

## chat.job  job=medic  fn=commerce
Soldier pills are sixty ryo at the hospital stall. They refill chakra in a hurry, and taste like a punishment.
Fall in the field and you wake up here in the morning, patched and rested. No bill, but your thrown kit stays wherever you dropped it.

## chat.job  job=medic  fn=hook
The herb room's stock of numbing salve keeps running out a week early. Somebody is either very clumsy or very careful.
A patient on the third floor won't say what happened to their hands. I'd like to know before they leave.

# -- nurse ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=nurse  fn=worldbuild
The medics get the glory; the nurses get the sheets. Forty beds, changed every morning before the medics have had their tea.
Visiting hours are ten to six. Shinobi come in through the window at midnight anyway, and I've stopped shouting about it.
Every bed on the second floor has a bell. The one by the window has been rung more than all the others together.
Genin are the worst patients. They try to leave the moment they can stand, and half of them can't.
The hospital kitchen makes rice porridge for everyone, no matter what's wrong with them. It works more often than you'd think.

## chat.job  job=nurse  fn=reveal_character
I'm not a shinobi and never will be. But I've held the hand of more Jonin than most Jonin have met.
I knit between rounds. The children's ward has a scarf on every bed, and none of them match.
My worst habit is checking everyone's pulse when I shake their hand. My friends have learned to wave instead.

## chat.job  job=nurse  fn=hook
The night shift is short two nurses this week. Someone who can stay awake until dawn would be a real help, and the tea's free.
An old patient keeps asking for the same visitor by a name none of us recognise. I'd like to find them before the week is out.
The children's ward has lost its picture book, the one with the frog Hokage. It's the only thing that gets them to sleep.

## chat.job  job=nurse  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character
Flowers go on the sill, not the bedside table. Too many patients have knocked a vase onto a fresh bandage.
The worst noise in this building is a quiet ward. I'd take a room full of complaining Genin over silence any day.

# -- guard ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=guard  fn=worldbuild
A patrol walks the same streets in a different order every night. Predictable is the one thing a guard must never be.
Most of my shifts are lost children, lost purses and lost tempers outside the bath house at closing.
The guard house keeps a lantern burning on every corner it watches. Count them from the Rock and you can see our route.
Fights on Market Day are always over the same three things: the last eel, the best stall and whose dog it was.
We relieve the watch at six and at six. Twelve hours on the cobbles and you learn which stones wobble.

## chat.job  job=guard  fn=reveal_character
I joined the guard because I wasn't fast enough for the field. Turns out walking slowly and watching closely is its own skill.
I know every cat on my route by sight. They're better informants than the people.
I've never drawn my blade in the street. I mean to keep it that way until I retire.

## chat.job  job=guard  fn=hook
Lanterns on the east lane keep going out at the same hour every night. Not blown out: someone turns the wick right down.
A shopkeeper on the market street swears the shutters were locked from the inside after a theft. I'm still turning that one over.
Someone's been chalking little arrows on the walls along my route. They don't point anywhere I can see.

# -- gate guard ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=gate_guard  fn=worldbuild
Every traveller signs the book: name, village, purpose and how long. We fill one book a month and the archive keeps them all.
Carts come in at dawn, squads go out at eight, merchants arrive at noon. You could set a clock by the gate.
The booth has two chairs, one cushion and a kettle that's older than both of us.
The painted characters on the doors get touched up every spring. Climbing up there is the most exciting thing that happens all season.
A squad leaving on a mission salutes the booth. A squad coming back usually just waves, if they can lift an arm.

## chat.job  job=gate_guard  fn=reveal_character
Nine years at this gate. I've seen everyone leave, and nearly everyone come back. The nearly is the part I think about.
People think gate duty is boring. It is. That's how you know it's working.
We play shogi between carts. The board's been on the counter so long the pieces have worn smooth.

## chat.job  job=gate_guard  fn=guide
Past the gate is the outer road and the training fields. Beyond the far hills you need a mission scroll and a stamped pass.
Squads leave from here, but the paperwork starts at the Mission Desk. Get it stamped there first or you'll be turned round.

## chat.job  job=gate_guard  fn=hook
A farmer's cart has been stuck at the booth all morning over a missing stamp. The cabbages are not getting any fresher.
Three travellers this month signed the book in the same handwriting under different names. Probably nothing. Probably.

# -- merchant ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=merchant  fn=worldbuild
I buy cloth from the coastal towns, sell it here, buy tea here and sell it there. The road between is where the money goes.
The Leaf is the safest market on the road, which means it's also the one where everyone haggles hardest.
Market Day draws carts from three valleys. I've done more business by noon on a Market Day than in the rest of the week.
A merchant's caravan hires Genin to guard it. Cheap, eager and they mostly know which end of a kunai goes forward.

## chat.job  job=merchant  fn=reveal_character
I've crossed the far hills forty times. The only thing I've never got used to is coming home and finding the village bigger.
My ledger is older than I am. My grandmother started it, and I still write in her columns.
I can't sleep in a bed that doesn't move. Too many nights on a cart.

## chat.job  job=merchant  fn=commerce
Everything has a price, and in the Leaf it's usually in ryo notes. Coins are for the road, where nobody trusts paper.
The pawn shop gives half what a thing's worth. I give sixty percent, if you can wait until my cart's back.
Prices go up three days before a festival and fall the morning after. Buy your lanterns in the quiet weeks.
Hiring a squad of Genin for a D-rank costs less than you'd think. Getting them to stop chasing the client's cat is extra.

## chat.job  job=merchant  fn=hook
A crate of mine came in with the seals intact and half the tea missing. Nobody on my side opened it, and I'd swear to that.
My usual supplier in the river town hasn't answered three letters. Either they're ill, or they've found a better buyer.

# -- librarian -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=librarian  fn=worldbuild
The Academy library has four thousand scrolls and one rule: nothing leaves the room still rolled the wrong way.
Children borrow the jutsu scrolls and bring them back with fingerprints on the hand sign pages. That's how I know they read them.
The history shelf is the dustiest and the most important. I give it a wipe every Fifth Day and nobody notices.
Overdue books get a reminder slip, then a visit, then a note to the instructor. The note usually works.
A library is the quietest place in a loud village. Even the Inuzuka children whisper in here.

## chat.job  job=librarian  fn=reveal_character
I read every scroll before it goes on the shelf. I've read some of them four times. The cookery ones mostly.
I wanted to be a scholar in the capital. Then I found out the Leaf's library needed someone, and I never left.
I can't stand a bent corner. It's not personal. It's just wrong.

## chat.job  job=librarian  fn=guide
The beginners' scrolls on chakra control are on the lowest shelf by the window. Start there, not with the fire jutsu.
The workshop is through the door at the end of the library. Mind the shavings; nobody sweeps them up.

## chat.job  job=librarian  fn=hook
Eleven scrolls are overdue, scattered across the whole village. If someone could fetch them back, I'd stop dreaming about them.
Somebody returns a scroll to the wrong shelf every night. Always the same one, always one shelf to the left.

# -- tea master ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=tea_master  fn=worldbuild
Green tea for the morning, roasted tea after dinner and barley tea in summer. Serve them in the wrong order and the regulars notice.
The water comes from the shrine spring, carried up in the morning. Tap water makes good tea; spring water makes better.
A tea house is the village's parlour. Half of the Leaf's marriages, quarrels and business deals started on these cushions.
Whisk the matcha until the foam is like a calm pond. If you can see the bottom of the bowl, start again.
The ceremony room upstairs has one rule: shinobi leave their pouches at the door. Even the Jonin.

## chat.job  job=tea_master  fn=reveal_character
I've poured tea for two Hokage in this room. Both of them asked for a second cup, and both of them forgot to pay.
I spent three years learning to fold a cloth. People laugh, until they see what a badly folded cloth does to a ceremony.
My mother ran this house and her mother before. I'll be the first to run it without a cat on the counter. I miss the cat.

## chat.job  job=tea_master  fn=commerce
A pot of green tea is four ryo. Drink it in the evening and you'll stay sharp an hour longer than you should.
Dango with tea is the classic pairing. My dango aren't the best on the street, but they're the best with tea.
I need someone to sweep the garden path and change the hanging scroll. Fifty ryo a shift, and all the tea you want.

## chat.job  job=tea_master  fn=hook
The tea house across the corner dropped its prices again. One more week of this and one of us closes.

# -- bath keeper ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=bath_keeper  fn=worldbuild
The boilers go on at three in the afternoon and the first bathers arrive at four. By six the steam's so thick you can't see the mural.
Wash first, then soak. I've watched Jonin shrink like children when I catch them skipping the stools.
The mural on the big wall is the Hokage Rock at sunrise. It's been repainted twice, and the second time someone gave the third face a moustache.
Training ground regulars come in caked in mud at sundown. I keep a separate tub for them, and I'm not ashamed.
Everyone in the village passes through here sooner or later. A bath house hears more news than the Residence.

## chat.job  job=bath_keeper  fn=reveal_character
I've never once had a bath in my own bath house. By closing, the last thing I want is more hot water.
I keep a list of who sings in the tubs. It's longer than you'd expect, and it includes a council member.
My grandfather built the boiler. I talk to it every morning, and it starts more willingly when I do.

## chat.job  job=bath_keeper  fn=commerce
A soak is three ryo, a towel one more, and the milk in the cold box is two. It's the best seven ryo in the Leaf.
A long soak after a hard day eases the legs. You won't wake up stiff, and you'll sleep like a stone.

## chat.job  job=bath_keeper  fn=hook
The lost and found basket is full again: a comb, a single sandal and a wooden charm. Somebody must miss them.
The boiler's been coughing all week. If it gives out before the festival, the whole village goes unwashed.

# -- pawnbroker ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=pawnbroker  fn=worldbuild
Everything on these shelves has a story. Most of them start with somebody needing ryo in a hurry.
Bandit camp loot comes through my door every week. I don't ask where it came from, because I already know.
The back room is for things people promised to come back for. Some of them have waited longer than I have.
I'm the only shop in the Leaf where a rusty sword and a silver hairpin sit side by side, and I like it that way.

## chat.job  job=pawnbroker  fn=reveal_character
I keep a ledger of every pledge, every name and every date. Some days I read it the way other people read novels.
I hate selling a pledge that wasn't collected. I give it a month longer than the law says, every time.
A child once pawned a marble to buy a parent's birthday present. I still have the marble. The child is a Chunin now.

## chat.job  job=pawnbroker  fn=commerce
I pay half the value, in notes, on the spot. If you want the full price, go and haggle in the market.
Salvage from a bandit camp is the only loot worth bringing in. Leaves and rocks I can find on my own.
Buy back a pledged piece within the month and you pay what I paid plus a tenth. After that, it's on the shelf.

## chat.job  job=pawnbroker  fn=hook
Somebody pawned a forehead protector with a village mark I've never seen. They left before I could ask a single question.
A family heirloom came in that I'm almost sure was reported stolen. I'd rather return it than sell it.

# -- vet -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=vet  fn=worldbuild
Ninken come in with thorns in their pads, ticks in their ears and, once a month, a kunai in somewhere awkward.
The clinic sees more dogs than anything else, but I've stitched a cat, two hawks and a very offended tortoise.
A ninken's nose is a weapon. I treat it like one: no smoke, no perfume and no spicy food within reach.
Every pup in the clan gets checked at six weeks, weighed at twelve and paired with a partner at the clan day.
Village cats are the hardest patients. A ninken will hold still for a stitch. A cat considers it a challenge.

## chat.job  job=vet  fn=reveal_character
I was bitten twenty-three times in my first year. I stopped counting after that, which is a kind of progress.
I wanted to be a medic, but people complain too much. Dogs just look at you, and you know.
My own dog is fourteen and grey round the muzzle. It still meets me at the gate every evening.

## chat.job  job=vet  fn=commerce
Adopting a pup is free; feeding it isn't. Budget a few ryo a day for meat, more once it starts growing.
A check-up is five ryo, a stitched paw fifteen. Pets are knocked out, never killed, but they still need mending afterwards.

## chat.job  job=vet  fn=hook
Three ninken came in this week with the same scratch on the same leg. Something near the wall has thorns we don't know.
A stray cat keeps bringing kittens to the clinic step, one a day, as if it's lending them to us.

# -- priest --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=priest  fn=worldbuild
The shrine bell rings at dawn and dusk, and once more at midnight on New Year. The rest of the year, the crows do the ringing.
Wish plaques go on the wall by the gate. I read them all, though I'm only supposed to burn them at year's end.
Shinobi come here the night before a long mission. They don't pray, mostly. They just stand under the tree for a while.
The rope over the main hall is renewed every autumn. It takes eight people and an argument to tie the knots.
Weddings in spring, the dead in autumn and children's blessings on the first warm day. The shrine keeps the village's calendar.

## chat.job  job=priest  fn=reveal_character
I sweep the steps every morning. All two hundred of them. It's the only part of the job I'm sure I'm doing right.
I was a Genin once, for a year. I found I preferred keeping lanterns lit to blowing things up.
The fox statues by the gate wear the knitted caps I make them each winter. Somebody steals one every year, and I knit another.

## chat.job  job=priest  fn=commerce
A wish plaque is five ryo and a charm for safe travel ten. The money goes on lamp oil and rope.
Fortunes are two ryo. Bad ones get tied to the branch outside and left behind for the wind to take.

## chat.job  job=priest  fn=hook
Somebody has been granting the small wishes on the plaques before I can read them. A lost doll found, a fence mended. I'd like to thank them.
The old stone lantern on the lower steps has a crack running through it, and it grows a little every week.

# -- painter -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=painter  fn=worldbuild
Half my work is shop signs. The other half is fixing shop signs after Genin practise their throws on them.
The best light in the village is on the old artists' lane at four in the afternoon. Every painter in the Leaf knows it.
I grind my own colours: red from cinnabar, blue from the river stones and black from the soot of the tea house chimney.
The Hokage Rock is the hardest thing to paint. The faces change every hour with the light, and they never sit still.
Lanterns for the festival are painted in the last week before it. The lane smells of glue and paper for days.

## chat.job  job=painter  fn=reveal_character
I paint the same view of the river every morning. Two hundred paintings so far, and none of them are right.
My first commission was a ramen shop's menu. I misspelled miso, and they kept it up for four years out of spite.
I can't stand a crooked frame in anyone's house. I've straightened pictures at funerals.

## chat.job  job=painter  fn=commerce
A small scroll painting is forty ryo and a poster for your wall twenty. Furniture's fine, but a wall wants something to look at.
I'll paint a nameplate for your door for ten ryo. Be sure of the spelling first.

## chat.job  job=painter  fn=hook
My best brush went missing from the studio, and a painting of a heron turned up on the bath house wall the same night. Not bad, either.

# -- courier -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=courier  fn=worldbuild
A courier knows every shortcut in the village: the alley behind the tea house, the steps under the bridge, and which roofs hold.
Letters inside the village go by foot, letters outside by hawk, and the urgent ones by a Genin with very fast legs.
The Residence sends a sealed bundle to the clan seats every morning. A sealed letter is never opened by its carrier.
Rain is bad, snow is worse and Market Day is a nightmare. You can't run a parcel through that crowd.
I've delivered to every door in the Leaf. Some I've knocked on so often the dogs stopped barking at me.

## chat.job  job=courier  fn=reveal_character
I can't walk anywhere slowly anymore. My family says I go to the bath house like there's a fire.
My first delivery was a cake. I ran so fast the cake didn't arrive as a cake.
I keep a map of the village in my head that's better than the one at the Residence. Nobody believes me until they're lost.

## chat.job  job=courier  fn=commerce
Inside the wall, a letter is a ryo and a parcel three. Past the gate, the hawk charges more than I do.
I need runners on festival days. Sixty ryo a shift, and you get to see every corner of the village before lunch.

## chat.job  job=courier  fn=hook
A parcel came in addressed to a house that's been empty for ten years. Somebody has to know who sent it.
A letter on my round has gone missing twice, the same letter, sent again. I'd like to know where it keeps ending up.

# -- dango maker ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=dango_maker  fn=worldbuild
Three colours on a stick: pink for spring, white for winter and green for summer. Autumn is the stick, I suppose.
The rice flour gets kneaded by hand until it's as soft as an earlobe. That's the old test and nobody has found a better one.
Grilled dango with sweet soy go on the brazier out front. The smell brings in more customers than any sign.
Cherry blossom season sells more dango in a week than the whole of winter. We work through the night for it.
A dango stick has to be bamboo, split thin. Pine splinters and cedar tastes, and both are bad for business.

## chat.job  job=dango_maker  fn=reveal_character
I ate dango for breakfast every day of my childhood. My teeth have opinions about that now.
I tried to invent a fourth colour once. Purple. The customers called it bruised dango and I gave up within a week.
I never sell the last stick of the day. That one is mine, and it's always the best one.

## chat.job  job=dango_maker  fn=commerce
Three ryo a stick, eight for three. A stick or two speeds your chakra back up, so the training ground crowd buys them by the dozen.
Sweet soy or bean paste, take your pick. The soy ones keep better in a pouch, if you're taking them on a mission.

## chat.job  job=dango_maker  fn=hook
Someone has been buying exactly forty sticks every morning for a week and carrying them toward the Hokage Rock.

# -- barbecue cook -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=barbecue_cook  fn=worldbuild
The grills here burn charcoal, never wood. Wood smoke tastes of the forest; charcoal tastes of nothing, so the meat can talk.
A squad celebrating a mission orders three plates of beef, two of pork and whatever the loudest member can't finish.
Every table has its own grill, and every table has one person who insists on doing all the turning.
The marinade is soy, pear, garlic and something the clan won't let me write down.
After the Chunin Exams, the whole house is booked for a week. Winners and losers both eat here, just at different tables.

## chat.job  job=barbecue_cook  fn=reveal_character
I stopped smelling smoke years ago. My clothes haven't, and my family hangs them on the porch at night.
I can tell when beef is done by the sound. A good sizzle says turn me. A loud one says too late.
Some cooks get burns on their hands. I get them on my forearms, from leaning over to steal a bite.

## chat.job  job=barbecue_cook  fn=commerce
A plate of beef is twelve ryo, pork ten, and the vegetables are free for anyone who actually eats them.
Barbecue before a spar puts power in your fists. Not much, but enough for the taijutsu crowd to swear by it.

## chat.job  job=barbecue_cook  fn=hook
The charcoal merchant hasn't come in two weeks. If the stock runs out, we're cooking on the shrine's candles.

# -- ferryman ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=ferryman  fn=worldbuild
The ferry crosses where the river is too wide for stepping stones and too slow for anything else. Fifteen crossings on a good day.
Shinobi can walk across the water, so they think the ferry is for old people. Then their chakra runs out halfway.
The river is three different rivers: brown in spring, green in summer and grey all winter.
I pole the boat, I don't row it. A pole knows the bottom; an oar only knows the surface.
The river people mend nets on the far bank in the mornings. If you want to know anything, ask there, not in the market.

## chat.job  job=ferryman  fn=reveal_character
I've poled this ferry for thirty-one years. My father did it for forty. I don't think I'll beat him.
I talk to the herons. They don't trust me yet, but they're closer than they used to be.
I've never learned to swim. Nobody believes it, and I'm too old to start now.

## chat.job  job=ferryman  fn=commerce
A crossing is one ryo. Carts are five, and dogs are free if they sit still.

## chat.job  job=ferryman  fn=hook
They're building a new bridge downstream. When it opens, nobody will need the ferry. I'd like one good season first.
Somebody keeps untying the boat at night and bringing it back before dawn, tied to the wrong post.

# -- herbalist -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=herbalist  fn=worldbuild
The herb gardens by the hospital grow eighty plants, and seven of them could kill you. We label those twice.
Drying racks need shade and wind, never sun. Sun cooks the leaves and leaves you with expensive hay.
Medics buy salves, cooks buy spices, and the Academy buys everything that smells strong enough to wake a class.
Half of a good herbalist's work is knowing which plant looks almost exactly like the one you want.
Pick yarrow at dawn, mugwort at noon and moonflower only after dark. Plants have schedules too.

## chat.job  job=herbalist  fn=reveal_character
I taste a little of every new batch. My tongue can name a bitter root from across the room.
I can't walk past a weed without checking what it is. It makes my walks very slow.
My grandmother said a garden tells you about the gardener. Mine says I never have enough time.

## chat.job  job=herbalist  fn=commerce
A burn salve is ten ryo and a sleep tea four. I only sell the strong mixes to medics.
Weeding the gardens pays forty ryo a morning, and you'll learn three plants that save lives.

## chat.job  job=herbalist  fn=hook
The hospital is short on fever root, and the patch by the river that usually supplies it has been dug up.
A whole tray of drying moonflower vanished overnight. It's useless to anyone who doesn't know how to brew it.

# -- kennel keeper -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.job  job=kennel_keeper  fn=worldbuild
Forty dogs, forty bowls, forty names. Feeding time is the loudest ten minutes in the whole village.
A ninken pup sleeps in a pile with its littermates until it chooses a partner. After that, it sleeps at its partner's feet.
The run behind the kennels is the length of three training grounds. The dogs still find a way to dig under the fence.
Every kennel has a slate with the dog's name, age, partner and favourite food. The food column causes the most arguments.
The old ninken get the sunny kennels. They've earned the warm floor more than any of us.

## chat.job  job=kennel_keeper  fn=reveal_character
I've been bitten, chased, licked and sat on, sometimes all by the same dog before breakfast.
I keep a jar of dried fish in my pocket. My family complains, but every dog in the village knows my name.
When a dog retires, it comes home with me. I've got six now, and a very small flat.

## chat.job  job=kennel_keeper  fn=commerce
A pup is yours to adopt at the clinic. Just know it'll want feeding, walking and the best spot on your bed.
I pay forty ryo a shift for kennel help: brushing, bowls and the bit we don't talk about.

## chat.job  job=kennel_keeper  fn=hook
One of the young ninken slips its kennel every night and comes back at dawn smelling of the river.

The tongs are the cook's, the grill is the guest's. Reach for my tongs and you'll be eating your beef raw.
Lunch is quiet, the dinner rush is a battle, and the last table always wants one more plate just as the coals die.
A squad's first meal after graduating is on the house. It's the oldest custom on this street, and the best advertising.

## chat.job  job=dango_maker  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character
The brazier takes an hour to come up to heat, so I light it before I've even had my tea.

## chat.job  job=ferryman  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character

The boat's had three new hulls and four new poles, but the bench is the one my father carved. That makes it the same boat.
Market Day mornings, the ferry carries more vegetables than people. The cabbages are better company.
Anglers on the far bank wave me over just to ask what's biting. I always tell them trout, and I'm usually right.

## chat.job  job=guard  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character
The night shift's worst enemy isn't thieves. It's the bakery on the corner at four, when the first loaves come out.
A guard learns the village by its sounds. The tea house shutters at ten, the bath house boiler at eleven, the last dog at midnight.
My boots get resoled every season. I wear through the left one first, and nobody can tell me why.

## chat.job  job=kennel_keeper  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character

A ninken that won't eat is the first sign of trouble. I check every bowl at sundown, all forty of them.
The pups learn their names in a week, their partner's scent in a day and the sound of the treat jar in an hour.

## chat.job  job=painter  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character
Festival masks are the best-paid work of the year. Fox, cat and crane sell out; nobody ever wants the frog.

A painter's apprentice spends a year mixing colours before touching a brush. I spent two, because I kept eating the persimmon black.
Ink for a scroll has to dry in the shade. In the sun it cracks, and the crack runs right through the Hokage's nose.

## chat.job  job=bath_keeper  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character

The pails are cypress, the stools are cypress, and the whole place smells like a forest after rain. I'll fight anyone who brings in plastic buckets.
Closing is midnight. I scrub the tiles till one, and I've never once been too tired to enjoy the quiet.

## chat.job  job=courier  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character

I've worn out more sandals than any shinobi in the Leaf. The cobbler on the main street gives me a regular's price.
Every courier has a route they hate. Mine is the steps up to the shrine, all two hundred of them, with a parcel of lamp oil.

## chat.job  job=florist  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character

The greenhouse has to be warm in winter, cool in summer and damp all year. It's more demanding than any customer.
Market Day I set out buckets on the street. By noon the lilies are gone and only the stubborn carnations are left.

## chat.job  job=gate_guard  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character

Travellers always ask how far to the Hokage Rock. I point up. They look up. That's usually the best part of my day.

## chat.job  job=herbalist  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character

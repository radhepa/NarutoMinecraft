## followup.exam
So? The exam! How did it go? Don't make me guess, {player}.
You had that exam, didn't you? I've been wondering all week. Tell me.
I kept my fingers crossed through the whole exam morning. Did it work?

## followup.mission
You're back! How was the mission? All in one piece, I see. Mostly.
I heard your squad came in through the gate. How did the mission go?
You said you were heading out on a mission. I watched the road until I heard you were home. How was it?

## followup.festival
How was the festival? Did you win anything at the stalls? Eat anything ridiculous?
You went to the festival, didn't you? Tell me about the lanterns. I missed half of it.
I looked for you at the festival but the crowds were too thick. Did you have fun?

## followup.training
How's the training coming along? Did you get that move you were stuck on?
You said you'd be training hard this week. Show me your hands. Ha, blisters. Good.
So, did all that training pay off, or are you just more bruised?

## followup.date  stage=teen|adult  player_stage=teen|adult

You had that evening out planned. I want every detail. The good ones, anyway.

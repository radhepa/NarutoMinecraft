# Mission banter on the road: mission.banter.<moment>.<speaker>, speakers sensei (the squad's Jonin),
# teammate (a squadmate, a child in the beta) and client (whoever hired the squad). Generic voices

#! kind: cycle

# -- travel ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## mission.banter.travel.sensei  fn=quest,teach
Keep a steady pace. A squad that sprints the first hour walks the rest.
Eyes on the treeline, not your feet. The road will still be there.
Count the milestones. If we're lost, I want to know how lost.
Talk if you like, but quietly. Voices carry further out here.
We'll rest at the next stream. Fill your canteens there, not before.

## mission.banter.travel.teammate  fn=quest,deepen_bond  stage=child|teen
Are we nearly there? Don't laugh, I'm only asking once.
I've never been this far from the village. The trees even smell different.
Race you to that big rock? No? Fine. Later.
I packed six rice balls. Six. Want one?
Does the road look like the map to you? Because it doesn't to me.

## mission.banter.travel.client  fn=quest,worldbuild
That hill there? My grandfather farmed it before the road was paved.
You ninja walk so quietly. It's unnerving, honestly.
I make this trip every season. Never with company before.
There's a tea house at the crossroads. Best roasted chestnuts this side of the river.
Do they teach you to carry that much without complaining? I'm impressed.

# -- camp --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## mission.banter.camp.sensei  fn=quest,teach
Watches in pairs, two hours each. Wake the next pair, don't just nudge them.
Small fire, low smoke. We're guests in these woods.
Eat, then sleep. Tomorrow's a long road.
Good work today. I mean that. Now get some rest.
Sit a while. Tell me something you learned today that the Academy didn't teach you.

## mission.banter.camp.teammate  fn=quest,deepen_bond  stage=child|teen
Can you see all those stars? There's never this many over the village.
I can't sleep. Want to talk for a bit?
Do you think we're doing well? On this mission, I mean.
If you snore, I'm putting a leaf in your mouth.
This is the first time I've slept outside. It's not so bad, with the squad here.

## mission.banter.camp.client  fn=quest,reveal_character
Thank you for the fire. And the company. I don't usually have either on the road.
I brought dried persimmons. Here, take a handful. Go on.
My family thinks I'm mad, travelling with goods at my age. Maybe I am.
You lot sleep in shifts? Then I'll sleep soundly. First time in years.
Tell me about the Leaf. I only ever see the market.

# -- arrival -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Good. On time and in one piece. That's a mission done right.
Look around. Remember this place. You'll be back one day.
Rest tonight. We report in the morning.

# Word gets round (BONDS.md §8): the opening line when someone saw, or heard about, something you did and has
# not brought it up yet. Said once, after VF's own deed reactions. Seen is first-hand; heard is gossip,
# and it may have bent a little on the way.
#! fn: react

## bond.seen.abandoned_mission

## bond.heard.abandoned_mission

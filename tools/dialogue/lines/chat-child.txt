# -- lessons and the Academy ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character
We did hand signs all morning. My fingers are so tired they keep making the Tiger by themselves.
I can do the Clone now! Well, half a Clone. It came out lying down, but it's still a Clone.
Our instructor can tell who's talking even facing the board. I think there's a secret eye on the back of the head.
History is the worst. Who cares what happened a hundred years ago? It already happened!
I got a gold star for my shuriken throws. Three out of five on the post! The other two hit the fence.
Transformation is so hard. I tried to turn into a cat and I came out as me with whiskers.
We had a test on the twelve signs today. I mixed up the Hare and the Dog again. They look the same, I swear.
The library lady says if you read one scroll a week you'll be Chunin before anyone else. I've read two. Ever.
I sit by the window in class so I can watch the Genin squads walk past. That's learning too.
Practice kunai are blunt, but they still hurt if you drop one on your foot. Ask me how I know.
The janitor caught me drawing on the desk and made me scrub every desk in the room. I'm never drawing again. Until next week.
Our instructor makes us run laps if anyone's late. I'm never late. Somebody else always is.
I wrote my name on the inside of my practice headband so nobody steals it. Everybody's is the same, it's a problem.
My homework got rained on and now it looks like a frog sat on it. A frog did not sit on it. I'm pretty sure.
In the workshop today we made smoke bombs. Mine just made a sort of sad cough.
Substitution is the coolest. You get hit and then poof, it's a log! I can't do it yet, but I've got the poof part.

## chat.child  stage=child  personality=meticulous|thoughtful  fn=reveal_character
I made flashcards for all twelve signs. Rat, Ox, Tiger, Hare... do you want to borrow them? You have to give them back in order.
I counted. There are fourteen target posts in the yard, and one of them is shorter than the others. Nobody else noticed.

## chat.child  stage=child  personality=fiery|proud  fn=reveal_character
I'm going to be the best in the class by the end of the year. Maybe by the end of the week. I'm deciding.
Somebody said I throw like a duck. I'll show them. I'll throw like an angry duck.

## chat.child  stage=child  personality=shy|gentle  fn=reveal_character

I practise my hand signs under the desk where no one can see. I'm getting good at it. Don't tell.

## chat.child  stage=child  personality=playful|curious  fn=reveal_character

Did you know the target posts are made of cedar? I asked the carpenter. I ask the carpenter a lot of things.

# -- recess and games ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=worldbuild,filler
At recess we play ninja and bandits. Nobody ever wants to be the bandit, so we just have a lot of ninja.
I'm the champion of the swing. I can go so high you can see over the Academy fence.
We played hide-and-seek and nobody found me for an hour. Then I realised nobody was looking.
Kick-the-can is the best game. The can is the Hokage and you have to protect it. Except we kick it.
I can climb the big tree out front to the third branch. The fourth branch is for Genin. I'll get there.
Tag is better with shinobi rules. If you do a hand sign before they tag you, it doesn't count.
We drew a map of the whole village in the dirt at recess. We put the ramen shop in the middle because it's the most important.
Shadow tag only works when it's sunny. On rainy days we just tag, which is worse.
The yard has a puddle that never dries. We call it the Lake of Doom. You have to jump over it or you lose.
We made up a jutsu called Thousand Pebbles. It's just throwing lots of pebbles, but it has a cool name.
Somebody brought a ball and we played until the bell. My team won. Probably. Nobody was counting right.
We had a race to the end of the yard and back. I came second, but the first one cheated with a running start.

## chat.child  stage=child  player_stage=child  fn=worldbuild,deepen_bond

Do you want to see my secret hiding spot? It's behind the woodshed. Well, it was secret.
Come and swing with me! I'll push you first, then you push me, and you have to push hard.
If you're on my team for kick-the-can, we'll win for sure. You look like a fast kicker.

My little brother keeps copying everything I do. I did a hand sign and he did it too, and then he fell over.
My dad works at the fish stall, so my lunch always smells like fish. I don't mind. The cats follow me everywhere.
My mum says I can't be a ninja and a baker at the same time. I'm going to be both and prove her wrong.

My aunt runs the tea house on the corner. If I sweep the garden path, I get a free cup and a sweet bean cake.

My parents both work at the market, so I walk home on my own. I'm very good at it. I only got lost twice.

My parents run a shop, so after the Academy I help count the stock. I can count to a thousand. In ryo, even.

## chat.child  stage=child  group=clanless  fn=reveal_character

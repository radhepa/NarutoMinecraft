# Children's small talk: Academy chatter, recess, snacks, siblings, pets, dares, and wanting to be ninja.
# Every heading here is stage=child.

# -- lessons and the Academy ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=worldbuild,reveal_character
We did hand signs all morning. My fingers are so tired they keep making the Tiger by themselves.
I can do the Clone now! Well, half a Clone. It came out lying down, but it's still a Clone.
Our instructor can tell who's talking even facing the board. I think there's a secret eye on the back of the head.
History is the worst. Who cares what happened a hundred years ago? It already happened!
I got a gold star for my shuriken throws. Three out of five on the post! The other two hit the fence.
Transformation is so hard. I tried to turn into a cat and I came out as me with whiskers.
We had a test on the twelve signs today. I mixed up the Hare and the Dog again. They look the same, I swear.
The library lady says if you read one scroll a week you'll be Chunin before anyone else. I've read two. Ever.
I sit by the window in class so I can watch the Genin squads walk past. That's learning too.
Practice kunai are blunt, but they still hurt if you drop one on your foot. Ask me how I know.
The janitor caught me drawing on the desk and made me scrub every desk in the room. I'm never drawing again. Until next week.
Our instructor makes us run laps if anyone's late. I'm never late. Somebody else always is.
I wrote my name on the inside of my practice headband so nobody steals it. Everybody's is the same, it's a problem.
My homework got rained on and now it looks like a frog sat on it. A frog did not sit on it. I'm pretty sure.
In the workshop today we made smoke bombs. Mine just made a sort of sad cough.
Substitution is the coolest. You get hit and then poof, it's a log! I can't do it yet, but I've got the poof part.

## chat.child  stage=child  personality=meticulous|thoughtful  fn=reveal_character
I made flashcards for all twelve signs. Rat, Ox, Tiger, Hare... do you want to borrow them? You have to give them back in order.
I counted. There are fourteen target posts in the yard, and one of them is shorter than the others. Nobody else noticed.

## chat.child  stage=child  personality=fiery|proud  fn=reveal_character
I'm going to be the best in the class by the end of the year. Maybe by the end of the week. I'm deciding.
Somebody said I throw like a duck. I'll show them. I'll throw like an angry duck.

## chat.child  stage=child  personality=shy|gentle  fn=reveal_character
I don't like it when the instructor calls on me. I know the answer, it just... hides when everyone looks.
I practise my hand signs under the desk where no one can see. I'm getting good at it. Don't tell.

## chat.child  stage=child  personality=playful|curious  fn=reveal_character
I asked the instructor if you can do jutsu with your feet. I got sent to stand in the hall. It was a real question!
Did you know the target posts are made of cedar? I asked the carpenter. I ask the carpenter a lot of things.

# -- recess and games ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=worldbuild,filler
At recess we play ninja and bandits. Nobody ever wants to be the bandit, so we just have a lot of ninja.
I'm the champion of the swing. I can go so high you can see over the Academy fence.
We played hide-and-seek and nobody found me for an hour. Then I realised nobody was looking.
Kick-the-can is the best game. The can is the Hokage and you have to protect it. Except we kick it.
I can climb the big tree out front to the third branch. The fourth branch is for Genin. I'll get there.
Tag is better with shinobi rules. If you do a hand sign before they tag you, it doesn't count.
We drew a map of the whole village in the dirt at recess. We put the ramen shop in the middle because it's the most important.
Shadow tag only works when it's sunny. On rainy days we just tag, which is worse.
The yard has a puddle that never dries. We call it the Lake of Doom. You have to jump over it or you lose.
We made up a jutsu called Thousand Pebbles. It's just throwing lots of pebbles, but it has a cool name.
Somebody brought a ball and we played until the bell. My team won. Probably. Nobody was counting right.
We had a race to the end of the yard and back. I came second, but the first one cheated with a running start.

## chat.child  stage=child  player_stage=child  fn=worldbuild,deepen_bond
You can play ninja and bandits with us at recess. You can even be a ninja. We have plenty of ninja.
Do you want to see my secret hiding spot? It's behind the woodshed. Well, it was secret.
Come and swing with me! I'll push you first, then you push me, and you have to push hard.
If you're on my team for kick-the-can, we'll win for sure. You look like a fast kicker.

# -- snacks --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=reveal_character,filler
The dango stall gives you an extra stick if you say please really nicely. You have to mean it, though.
My favourite snack is the sweet potato from the roasting cart. It burns your fingers but it's worth it.
I saved my lunch money all week to buy a whole bowl of ramen by myself. I'm going to eat every noodle.
Rice crackers are the best because they crunch so loud the instructor knows you're having a snack.
I traded my egg roll for a pickled plum. It was a bad trade. My whole face went inside out.
My grandma makes onigiri in the shape of animals. Today mine was a frog. I ate the eyes first.
The Akimichi kids always have the best lunches. They share sometimes, if you say nice things about the food.
Melon bread is my favourite. The bakery on the corner sells it warm if you're there before school.
I had three dango for breakfast. Don't tell my mum.
I like the pink dango best. The green one tastes like grass. Good grass, but still grass.
Somebody brought shaved ice with plum syrup to school and everybody crowded round. The ice melted before anybody got any.
I'm saving up for barbecue. It costs a lot more than ramen, but my cousin says it makes you punch harder.

# -- siblings and family -------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=reveal_character
My big sister is a Genin. She doesn't let me touch her kunai, but I touched it once when she was asleep.
My little brother keeps copying everything I do. I did a hand sign and he did it too, and then he fell over.
My dad works at the fish stall, so my lunch always smells like fish. I don't mind. The cats follow me everywhere.
My mum says I can't be a ninja and a baker at the same time. I'm going to be both and prove her wrong.
I've got four big brothers and every one of them was in the Academy. The instructors look at me like they're tired already.
My grandpa used to be a Chunin. He says when he was little the Academy was just a field and a stick.
My baby sister can't even talk yet, but I've been teaching her the Rat sign. She mostly grabs my nose.
My big brother says he'll teach me the Fireball when I'm older. He says that every time I ask, so I ask a lot.
My aunt runs the tea house on the corner. If I sweep the garden path, I get a free cup and a sweet bean cake.
My cousin is in the police. I asked if I could ride along and they said no, but they let me wear the hat once.
My parents both work at the market, so I walk home on my own. I'm very good at it. I only got lost twice.
My big sister snores so loud the neighbours knock on the wall. My mum says it's a family talent.

## chat.child  stage=child  group=civilian  fn=reveal_character
Nobody in my family is a ninja. We make tofu. But I'm going to be the first, and then the tofu will be famous.
My parents run a shop, so after the Academy I help count the stock. I can count to a thousand. In ryo, even.

## chat.child  stage=child  group=clanless  fn=reveal_character
I don't have a clan, but my mum says that just means I get to make up my own family technique. I'm calling it the Big Kick.
The clan kids get special jutsu. That's fine. I'm going to learn every normal jutsu twice as good.

# -- pets and animals ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=reveal_character,worldbuild
I want a ninken so badly. The Inuzuka clinic has pups, and one of them licked my hand, so I think it picked me.
Our cat sleeps on my feet every night. I can't move my legs until morning, but I don't want to wake it.
There's a frog in the Academy pond that's bigger than my fist. We named it Hokage. It doesn't do anything, it just sits.
I fed the deer by the Nara forest. One of them ate my whole sleeve. My mum was not happy.
The ninken at the gate know my name. Well, they know the sound of my lunch box.
I found a beetle as big as a coin and showed it to an Aburame kid. They said it was a very good beetle. I was so proud.
Our neighbour's dog can do a trick where it rolls over and plays dead. I tried to teach it Substitution. It didn't work.
I want a cat from the shelter, but my dad says we already have enough with my brother.
There's a crow that sits on the Academy roof every day at lunch. I've been giving it rice. I think we're friends now.
The koi at the Hyuga estate are huge. I saw one through the gate. It looked at me like I was in trouble.
If I get a ninken, I'm going to name it Kunai. Or Ramen. Or Kunai Ramen.
A puppy from the kennels followed me all the way to the Academy. I had to walk it back. It was the best morning ever.

# -- dares ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=reveal_character,filler
I touched the shrine bell at night on a dare. Nothing happened, but my heart is still going.
They dared me to run past the Uchiha gate shouting. I made it halfway and then a grown-up looked at me.
The biggest dare is climbing to the second Hokage's eyebrow. Nobody has ever done it. Nobody has even tried really.
I ate a whole raw onion on a dare. I won, but now my mouth is a battlefield.
Somebody dared me to put my feet in the river in winter. I did it for a count of ten. I'm still cold.
The best dare is sneaking into the training grounds after dark. I got as far as the first stump and a frog jumped on me.
I bet the new kid can't do a cartwheel off the Academy steps. I did one. I kind of landed on my face.
They dared me to knock on the Shimura door and run. I knocked, but I was too scared to run, so I just stood there.
I climbed the water tank on our roof on a dare. I could see all the way to the wall! Then I couldn't get down.
Daring someone to sit alone in the hospital waiting room after dark is the scariest dare. I don't do that one.

## chat.child  stage=child  personality=playful|adventurous  fn=react
I dare you to jump across the Lake of Doom in the yard! Run-up allowed! [EXCLAIM]
Bet you can't stand on one leg longer than me. Ready? Go! Hey, that's cheating, you can't use chakra!

# -- admiring Genin and shinobi -----------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=reveal_character,worldbuild
I saw a Genin walk across the river today. Across it! On top! I'm going to practise in the bath tonight.
There's a Chunin on my street who can jump from one roof to the next without even looking. I want to do that.
When a squad comes back through the gate, I wave at them. Sometimes they wave back. Once a Jonin did.
Genin get real missions! I would do any mission. Even the cat ones. Especially the cat ones.
The Genin at the training grounds threw shuriken so fast I couldn't even see them. Then they hit a stump, thunk-thunk-thunk.
My hero is the Jonin who lives above the tofu shop. They bought me a dango once and didn't even ask for anything.
The Hokage's guard stands so still I thought they were statues. Then one sneezed.
I want to get my headband so badly. I practise tying it on with my old scarf every night.
A Genin squad was eating at Ichiraku and I sat next to them. They talked about a mission with a goat. A goat!
When I graduate I'm going to the Mission Desk first thing. I'll take the hardest D-rank they have.
The flak vests the Chunin wear have so many pockets. I bet there's a snack in at least one of them.
I followed a Genin home to see where they live. They noticed and gave me a lecture. It was so cool.

## chat.child  stage=child  player_rank=genin|chunin  fn=react
You're a real Genin! Can I touch your headband? Just the edge. I won't smudge it.
Is it true Genin get to go outside the wall? What's out there? Are there bears? Are there big bears?
Show me a jutsu, please! Even a little one! I won't tell anyone, I promise. Well, I'll tell a few people.

# -- the Hokage ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=reveal_character,worldbuild
I want to see the Hokage up close. I stood outside the Residence all morning and only saw a hawk.
I'm going to be Hokage one day, and my face will be on the Rock, and it's going to be smiling.
My grandpa says he shook a Hokage's hand once. He hasn't washed it since. That was forty years ago. Gross.
If you climb to the top of the Rock you can sit on the Hokage's heads. I haven't done it. I'm working up to it.
The Hokage works in the round building. My friend says there's a slide inside. I don't think there's a slide.
I drew all the Hokage faces in my notebook. The third one came out looking like my dad.
Do you think the Hokage ever eats ramen? I hope so. Everybody should.
When I'm Hokage I'm going to make it a rule that every day ends with dango.

# -- the hospital and being scared --------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=reveal_character
I don't like the hospital. It smells like soap and medicine and everyone talks in whispers.
I had to get a splinter out at the hospital. The medic was really nice, but the tweezers were the size of a kunai.
My big brother stayed in the hospital for a week after a mission. I brought him a drawing of a frog. He kept it on his bed.
The hospital has a room with painted frogs on the walls. That's the room for kids. It's still scary.
I heard if you get hurt in training they take you to the hospital and give you the worst porridge in the world. I'm never getting hurt.
I'm scared of the dark lanes by the wall at night. My mum says the Tobari are watching, so it's safe. That doesn't help.
Thunder used to scare me. Now I just count. If I get to ten before it bangs, it's far away.
The instructor says even Jonin get scared. I don't believe that. Maybe a little. Maybe Jonin are scared of the hospital too.

## chat.child  stage=child  personality=shy|gentle  fn=reveal_character
I get scared before tests. My stomach goes all wobbly. Then I eat a rice ball and it's a bit better.
I don't like loud noises. The forge on the market street makes my ears ring, so I walk past it really fast.

# -- ninja play and dreams -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=reveal_character,filler
I've been training my secret jutsu. It's called Silent Stomp. You stomp, but quietly.
When I grow up I'm going to have a summon. It'll be a giant cat. My mum says we don't have room.
I made a headband out of my mum's old belt. It has a leaf drawn on it in ink. It's almost real.
I've been sneaking up on my brother all week. I'm getting so good he didn't even know until I sneezed.
I practise tree walking on the tree in our garden. I get two steps up and then I'm on my back again.
I tied a rope between two chairs and practised walking on it like a ninja. Then the chairs fell over.
I'm going to invent a jutsu that makes vegetables taste like dango. Everyone will want me on their squad.
I hid in a cupboard for an hour pretending it was a mission. I learned the cupboard is very boring and very dusty.
Sometimes I pretend the main street is the Forest of Death and the shoppers are enemy ninja. I've never been caught.
I want to go outside the wall and see the whole world. And then come back for dinner.
My friends and I have a squad. We even have a name. We're not telling anyone the name.
I made my own sealing tag with a brush and some rice paper. It doesn't seal anything, but it looks great on my door.

## chat.child  stage=child  player_stage=child  fn=deepen_bond,filler
Do you want to be in my squad? We need someone who can throw. Or run. Or just be good at hiding.
We should practise hand signs together after the Academy. You do one, I copy it, then I do one and you copy it.
When we both make Genin, let's eat at Ichiraku together. I'm going to order the biggest bowl.
You're pretty good at the Academy stuff. Can you show me how you do the Ram? Mine looks like a sock puppet.

# -- weather, seasons and the village through a child's eyes -----------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  weather=rain  fn=react
I jumped in every puddle from my house to the Academy. My sandals are full of water, but I won.
When it rains the frogs in the Academy pond sing so loud you can't hear the instructor. Best days ever.

## chat.child  stage=child  weather=snow  fn=react
We're building a snow Hokage outside the Academy! It's got a big hat and stick arms and a scary face.
I tried to catch snowflakes on my tongue and caught a leaf instead. It tasted like a leaf.

## chat.child  stage=child  weather=thunder  fn=react
Did you hear that thunder? I wasn't scared. I just jumped because my foot slipped.

## chat.child  stage=child  season=summer  fn=react
Summer means the river! We go to the shallows after lessons and try to catch fish with our hands. Nobody's caught one yet.

## chat.child  stage=child  season=autumn  fn=react
I'm collecting the reddest maple leaves in the village. I've got thirty. I'm pressing them in my history scroll.

## chat.child  stage=child  season=spring  fn=react
The cherry blossoms are out! I caught a petal before it hit the ground, so I get a wish. I wished for a ninken.

## chat.child  stage=child  season=winter  fn=react
My mum knitted me a scarf so long I can wrap it round three times. I'm going to use it as a rope for ninja training.

# -- odd jobs, neighbours and small mysteries ---------------------------------------------------------

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=hook,reveal_character
There's a cat on our street that keeps stealing socks. It's got a whole pile of them under the tea house porch.
Somebody keeps leaving a rice ball at the little shrine on the corner every morning. I've been trying to catch who, but I always fall asleep.
I lost my lucky marble somewhere by the river. It's blue with a white swirl. If you find it, it's mine.
My friend says there's a secret tunnel under the Academy. I think it's just a cellar, but I still want to see it.
The old grandma at the end of our lane gives a sweet to anyone who carries her shopping. I've carried it four times this week.

## chat.child  stage=child  fn=worldbuild,filler
The bath house has a mural of the Hokage Rock on the wall. Somebody painted a moustache on one face and they never got it all off.
I know every shortcut from my house to the Academy. There are eleven. Twelve if you can climb a fence.
There's a dango stall by the shrine steps that only opens on festival days. That's my favourite stall in the whole world.
The fish stall man gives me the fish heads for our cat. The cat loves them. I don't look at them.
On Market Day I help my parents carry baskets. The best part is the free sample from the pickle stall. The worst part is the pickle.
The ramen shop has a stool that wobbles. I always pick that one. It's like a ride.
The lanterns on the main street go on all at once at dusk. I try to blink at the exact second. I never get it.
The training grounds are way bigger than they look from the Rock. I walked to the third stump and my legs fell off. Not really.
At the festival there's a game where you catch goldfish with a paper scoop. I've never caught one. The paper always breaks.
My street has a cobbler who can fix any sandal. I brought in my practice headband once, and that got fixed too.

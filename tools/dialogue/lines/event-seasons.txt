# The year's quiet events: the first snow, cherry blossoms, moon viewing.

## greet.event  event=first_snow  fn=react
First snow! Look at the Hokage Rock, the faces have got white eyebrows.
It's snowing, {player}! The first of the year. I always feel about six years old.
The first snow came down overnight. The whole village sounds muffled.
Snow! Mind the bridge, it ices before anything else does.
Did you see the rooftops this morning? White from the gate to the Rock.

## greet.event  event=first_snow  stage=child  fn=react
It snowed! It snowed! Snowball fight at the Academy yard, everyone's going!
First snow! I caught a flake on my tongue. It tasted like nothing. It was perfect.

## chat.event  event=first_snow  fn=worldbuild
On the first snow, people leave a cup of warm sake on the doorstep for the winter spirits. Old habit.
The training grounds are slippery after the first snow. Half the Genin spend the day on their backs.
The tea house brings out roasted tea on the first snow. Nobody announces it, everyone knows.
The children always build a snow Hokage by the Academy gate. It's never a good likeness.
The bath house is packed on first-snow evenings. Nothing beats hot water and cold air.
First snow means the farm carts stop coming so often. Stock up on rice and pickles now.

## chat.event  event=first_snow  personality=imaginative|thoughtful  fn=reveal_character
I like the first snow because it covers every footprint. The village looks like nobody's been anywhere.

## chat.event  event=first_snow  personality=protective|warmhearted  fn=reveal_character
Have you got a proper coat? First snow is the coldest one, because nobody's ready for it.

## chat.event  event=first_snow  stage=child  fn=worldbuild
I built a snow dog. It looks more like a snow potato, but I know it's a dog.
My hands are frozen, but I'm not going inside. Not ever.

## greet.event  event=cherry_blossoms  fn=react
The blossoms are out! The riverbank's gone pink overnight.
Cherry blossom season, {player}. Have you been down to the river yet?
Look up! There are petals in your hair already.
The trees by the Academy are in full flower. The whole street smells sweet.
Blossom week! Half the village is going to have a picnic under the trees today.

## greet.event  event=cherry_blossoms  stage=child  fn=react
The blossoms are out! We're having a picnic! My mother made the pink rice balls!

## chat.event  event=cherry_blossoms  fn=worldbuild
People spread mats under the trees along the river from dawn to claim the best spot.
The dango stall sells the three-colour skewers only in blossom season. They sell out by noon.
The petals fall within a week. That's what makes the viewing matter.
The Academy holds lessons outside during blossom week. Not much gets learnt.
At night they hang paper lanterns under the trees by the river. It's the prettiest walk in the Leaf.
Somebody always falls asleep under a tree and wakes up covered in petals.
The shrine has an old tree that blooms first every year. People bet on which day it opens.

## chat.event  event=cherry_blossoms  personality=thoughtful|reserved  fn=reveal_character
Blossoms remind me that good things don't stay. You have to stop and look while they're here.

## chat.event  event=cherry_blossoms  personality=playful|fiery  fn=reveal_character
I tried to catch a falling petal for an hour. I've decided it's a training exercise now.

## chat.event  event=cherry_blossoms  stage=child  fn=worldbuild
If you catch a falling petal before it touches the ground, you get a wish. I've had seven wishes today.
We're making petal crowns. Mine keeps falling apart, so it's a petal pile.

## greet.event  event=moon_viewing  fn=react
Full moon tonight, and a clear sky! Perfect for moon viewing.
Good evening, {player}. Are you going up the hill to see the moon?
Moon-viewing night. Everyone's making moon dumplings, the whole street smells of rice.
Look at that moon! Big as a lantern over the Hokage Rock.

## greet.event  event=moon_viewing  stage=child  fn=react
Moon night! We stacked the dumplings in a pyramid. I ate the top one. Don't tell.

## chat.event  event=moon_viewing  fn=worldbuild
People set pampas grass and white dumplings on their windowsills for the moon. It's an autumn custom.
The best place to see the moon is the hill behind the shrine. Bring a blanket and something warm.
Some say there's a rabbit on the moon, pounding rice. Look closely and you'll see its ears.
On moon-viewing night the tea house serves a special roasted tea, very mellow.
They say if you see the moon reflected in the river on viewing night, you'll have a good harvest.
The old ones write short poems about the moon and pin them on the shrine gate.

## chat.event  event=moon_viewing  personality=imaginative|gentle  fn=reveal_character
I always think the moon looks a little lonely. That's why we all come out to keep it company.

## chat.event  event=moon_viewing  personality=pragmatic|steadfast  fn=reveal_character
A full moon means a bright night. Good for travellers, bad for anyone trying to sneak about.

## chat.event  event=moon_viewing  stage=child  fn=worldbuild
I'm looking for the moon rabbit. I see its ears! I think. Maybe they're clouds.
We get to stay up late for the moon. I'm going to stare at it until I fall asleep.

# Part-time jobs (ROADMAP §7, Ryo: 40 to 80 ryo a shift): what the employer says when a shift starts, when the
# player is doing well, and when it is done. Shifts repeat, so every pool cycles.
#! kind: cycle

## job.ichiraku.start  fn=teach,commerce
Apron on, hair back, hands washed. You're on bowls and broth today.
Right! Noodles in for ninety breaths, no more. I'll shout when the orders come.
Wipe the counter between every customer. Nobody wants the last one's spilled soup.
Busy night ahead. Keep the bowls coming from the back and we'll be fine.

## job.ichiraku.good  fn=commerce,react
Look at you, ladling like you were born behind this counter!
Not a drop spilled. You've got the hands for this.
The customers are smiling. That's the broth, but it's also you.
Faster than my last helper, and tidier. Keep it up!

## job.ichiraku.done  fn=commerce
That's the shift. {pay}, and a bowl on the house. You earned both.
Done! Here's your {pay}. Same time next week?
Stools up, pot scrubbed. {pay} for you. Good work tonight.
There's your {pay}. Go home and put your feet up.

## job.grocer.start  fn=teach,commerce
Carry the crates in from the cart first, then stack the greens, heavy at the bottom.
You'll weigh beans today. A full scoop is a full scoop, don't skimp and don't spill.
Sweep out the front, then mind the counter while I go to the farms.
Sort the bruised fruit from the good. Bruised goes in the cheap basket, not the bin.

## job.grocer.good  fn=commerce,react
Nicely stacked! Customers buy more when it looks tidy.
You're quick with the scale. My old helper counted on fingers.
The onion crates are done already? I'm impressed.
That's how you talk to a customer. Friendly, quick, honest.

## job.grocer.done  fn=commerce
That'll do for today. Here's {pay}, and take a few onigiri home.
All done. {pay} for you. My back thanks you.
Shift's over. {pay}. Come again on Market Day, I'll need you then.
There's your {pay}. Take a radish too. Go on.

## job.flower_shop.start  fn=teach,commerce
Water the pots on the steps first, then change every bucket inside.
Cut the stems on a slant, under water, so they drink. Like this.
Wrap the orders on the counter. Paper first, then the ribbon, then the card.
Mind the roses. The thorns don't care that you're helping.

## job.flower_shop.good  fn=commerce,react
That bouquet's lovely! You've an eye for colour.
Every bucket fresh and every stem trimmed. Wonderful.
A customer asked who made the arrangement in the window. I said you did.
You're gentle with them. Flowers can tell, you know.

## job.flower_shop.done  fn=commerce
That's the day done. {pay}, and take a stem home for your room.
Thank you! Here's {pay}. Come and help again soon.
All finished. {pay} for you. The shop's never looked so good.
There's your {pay}. You smell like lilies now, sorry.

## job.tea_house.start  fn=teach,commerce
Two hands on the tray, and bow before you set the cups down.
Water just off the boil for green tea, never boiling. Boiling makes it bitter.
Clear the tables as soon as guests leave. A dirty table makes a slow afternoon.
Speak softly in here. Guests come for the quiet.

## job.tea_house.good  fn=commerce,react
Not a single cup rattled. You'd make a fine tea server.
The guests at the window asked for you by name. Well done.
Your matcha's foaming nicely. You've been practising.
Quick and quiet. That's exactly how it's done.

## job.tea_house.done  fn=commerce
That's the afternoon finished. {pay}, and a cup for yourself before you go.
Thank you. Here is {pay}. Please come again.
All tidy. {pay} for you. The kettle will miss you.
There's your {pay}. Rest your feet, you've earned it.

## job.haruno_store.start  fn=teach,commerce
Count the stock on the shelves and write down what's low. Neat writing, please.
Mind the counter while I'm in the back. Prices are on the little tags.
Wrap every gift in cloth, corners tucked. My mother checks.
Dust the glass case. Gently, those fans are older than both of us.

Those wrapping corners are perfect. My mother would approve.
Look how fast that went. Thank you.

## job.furniture_shop.start  fn=teach,commerce
Dust every piece on the floor, then oil the tables. Thin coat, rub it in.
Help me carry the new delivery in. Lift with your legs, not your back.
Mind the shop while I'm out. If anyone sells back, it's half what they paid. Check the book.
Straighten the cushions and screens. People buy what looks like a home.

## job.furniture_shop.good  fn=commerce,react
That table shines! You could see your face in it.
Not a scratch on the delivery. Careful hands.
A customer bought the screen you set up by the window. Good eye.
You're quick, and you don't bang into things. Rare.

## job.furniture_shop.done  fn=commerce
That's the shift. {pay}. Come back when the next delivery's in.
All done. {pay} for you. My shoulders thank you.
Finished. Here's {pay}. The floor looks better than when I opened.
There's your {pay}. Off you go.

## job.forge.start  fn=teach,commerce
On the bellows. Slow and steady. Too fast and you'll burn the steel.
Fetch charcoal from the shed, then sort the salvage. Iron in one pile, junk in the other.
Hold the tongs low and firm. If the piece slips, step back, don't catch it.
Quench in the tank when I say, not before. Then stand clear of the steam.

## job.forge.good  fn=commerce,react
Good heat! That's the colour we want, cherry red.
Your pumping's even. The fire likes you.
Clean salvage pile. You can tell good iron from rust now.
Steady with the tongs. Another month of this and I'll show you the hammer.

## job.forge.done  fn=commerce
Fire's banked. {pay}. Wash the soot off before you go home.
That's a shift. Here's {pay}. You'll sleep well tonight.
Good work. {pay}, and you can take an offcut of steel.
There's your {pay}. Same time, same apron, next week.

## job.bath_house.start  fn=teach,commerce
Scrub the washing area first, then fold the towels. Corners square.
Keep the boiler fed, a shovel of wood every so often. Watch the gauge.
Check the cubbies for things people left behind. They always leave something.
Restock the fruit milk in the cooler. Strawberry at the front, it sells fastest.

## job.bath_house.good  fn=commerce,react
The tiles are spotless. I could eat off them, if I were mad.
Perfect towels! The guests noticed.
The water's just right. You've got a feel for the boiler.
Quick work. The evening rush won't catch us out today.

## job.bath_house.done  fn=commerce
That's the shift. {pay}, and you can have a free soak before you go.
All done. {pay}. Take a fruit milk, too.
Finished. Here's {pay}. You've earned that bath.
There's your {pay}. Off home, clean as anything.

## job.kinoshita_yard.start  fn=teach,commerce
Stack the planks crossways so the air gets between them. Flat, or they'll warp.
Sweep the shavings into the sacks. The bath house buys them to light the boiler.
Sand this panel along the grain. Never across, you'll leave scratches.
Hold the board steady while I saw. Don't let go until I say.

## job.kinoshita_yard.good  fn=commerce,react
Straight stack! The old carpenters would nod at that.
Smooth as a river stone. Good sanding.
You held that board like a rock. Clean cut.
The yard's never been so tidy. Don't let it go to your head.

## job.kinoshita_yard.done  fn=commerce

## job.pharmacy.start  fn=teach,commerce
Turn the herbs on the drying racks. Gently, the leaves break.
Grind these roots fine, then measure them into the paper twists. Exactly one spoon each.
Label every jar. If it's not labelled, it's poison until proven otherwise.
Watch the kettle of fever tea. When it goes dark, take it off the fire.

## job.pharmacy.good  fn=commerce,react
Lovely and fine, that powder. Just right.
Every twist the same weight. You have patient hands.
Clear labels! Thank you, nobody ever writes clearly.
You're careful. In this work, careful is everything.

## job.pharmacy.done  fn=commerce
That's enough for today. {pay}, and a little bag of tea for your trouble.
All done. Here's {pay}. Wash your hands well before you eat.
Finished. {pay} for you. The racks are in better order than ever.
There's your {pay}. Thank you, truly.

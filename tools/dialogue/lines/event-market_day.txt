# Market Day: once a week the farm carts and travelling stalls fill the market street.
#! tags: event=market_day

## greet.event  fn=react
Market Day! The farm carts have been rolling through the gate since dawn.
Morning, {player}! Have you seen the market street? You can't move for baskets.
It's Market Day, so mind your pockets and your toes.
Market Day again. My favourite day of the week, and my purse's least favourite.
Good morning! I'm off to get the eggs before the tea house buys them all.

## greet.event  stage=child  fn=react
Market Day! The candy stall's here! It only comes on Market Day!
Are you going to the market? There's a stall with spinning tops. Come on!

## chat.event  fn=worldbuild
The travelling merchants set up at the far end of the street. They bring things you can't get in the Leaf.
The coast cart only comes on Market Day. Squid, clams, seaweed. Everyone fights over it.
Farmers from the outer fields sell straight from their carts. Cheapest vegetables of the week.
There's a knife grinder who comes on Market Day. Half the shinobi in the village queue at that grindstone.
The grocer shouts louder on Market Day. I think it's a competition with the fishmonger.
I always find something I didn't need. Last week it was a ceramic frog.
The guards at the gate check every cart on Market Day. It takes them all morning.
If you want a bargain, come after noon. The farmers don't like carting things home.
The noticeboards are covered in requests on Market Day. Everyone needs a hand carrying something.
A storyteller sets up by the well on Market Day. The children sit there for hours.

## chat.event  fn=teach,hook
On Market Day half the village needs help: carrying sacks, minding stalls. Easy ryo if you ask around.

## chat.event  personality=pragmatic|meticulous  fn=reveal_character
I make a list every Market Day. I stick to it. Mostly. The frog was not on the list.
Prices drop after noon by a fifth. I've timed it. Every week.

## chat.event  personality=adventurous|curious  fn=reveal_character
I ask every travelling merchant where they came from. One had walked from the sea.
There's a spice merchant today with a powder that turns rice yellow. I need to know how.

## chat.event  stage=child  fn=worldbuild
I've got three ryo for Market Day. I'm going to spend them very, very carefully.
The candy stall made me a sugar dragon. I can't eat it, it's too good.
My mother lets me carry the basket. It's heavy, but I'm strong.

## chat.event  job=grocer|fishmonger|merchant|farmer  fn=reveal_character
Market Day is my whole week in one morning. I'll be asleep by sundown.

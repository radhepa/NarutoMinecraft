# Small talk, part A: the answer to "How's your day?" for playful residents. Their own life, in their voice.
#! tags: personality=playful

## chat.personal  fn=reveal_character
Great! I swapped all the sandals at the bathhouse door. Not mine, everyone else's. I'm hiding from about nine people.
I bet my brother I could eat ten dango in one breath. I lost. I'm still a winner in my heart.
I've been practising a face that makes cats sit down. It works on two cats. And one very confused gate guard.
Today I taught a parrot at the market to say "ramen". Now it won't stop. The owner is not pleased.
I spent the morning racing a duck along the river. The duck cheated. It used wings.
I've invented a game called "don't touch the street". Roofs, fences, carts only. I've fallen in a flowerbed twice.
My sister dared me to wear my clothes inside out to the market. Nobody noticed. I'm a little hurt, honestly.
I found the perfect hiding spot for hide-and-seek. So perfect nobody found me. For three hours. I won, though!
I drew a moustache on my reflection in the pond. Didn't work. I'll try a smaller pond.
I balanced a teacup on my head all the way from the tea house. Paid for the cup, kept the glory.
I hid a toy frog in my husband's lunchbox. He hid it back in my sandal. This war has gone on for a year.
The old cook at the barbecue house and I have a riddle trade. Every visit, one riddle. I'm losing badly.
I keep a jar of excuses by my door. "A cat sat on my sandals" got me out of three chores this month.
I learned to whistle with two fingers! Very loud. My whole street knows I learned to whistle with two fingers.
I lost a game of stone-paper-scissors to a five-year-old, then lost a rematch. The child is unbeatable.
Every morning I say good morning to the Hokage faces. One of them winks back. Probably the sun. Probably.
I tried to juggle eggs. Two went fine. The third has a new home on the ceiling.
I made a paper crown for the fishmonger. Wore it all day, didn't notice. Wonderful fishmonger.
Today's plan was to nap. I napped. A complete success. I'm thinking of making it a tradition.
I hid my own birthday present so well I can't find it. Now I'm excited and annoyed at the same time.
I've been teaching the neighbour's dog to bow. It bows, then sneezes. We're calling it a style.
I've got a bet going that I can make the stern shopkeeper on the corner laugh before the festival. Two weeks left.
I counted how many steps it takes to cross the village while hopping. Seven hundred and forty, and one bruise.
I wrote a song about my landlord. The landlord heard it and asked for a second verse. I wasn't ready for that.
I challenged a crow to a staring contest. It won and then took my rice ball. Rude bird. Great bird.
My kids think I can talk to frogs. I've been practising the croak. I'm very convincing now.
I keep losing at cards to my grandfather. He says it's skill. I say he's hiding a whole deck in his sleeves.
I slid down the shrine banister. The priest saw. I'm sweeping the shrine steps for a week. Worth it.

I run the ring-toss stall at festivals. The secret? The rings are fine. People just throw like they're angry.
Somebody bought a fish from me and I sold it with a song. They came back for another fish just for the song.

My sensei banned pranks during missions. Didn't say anything about the walk home, though.

## chat.personal  fn=worldbuild
Every summer the kids hold a secret frog race under the second bridge. The adults all know. We all bet.
There's a game here called kunai-and-crane: you toss a paper crane, and someone hits it with a stick. Old Leaf fun.
The fishmongers hold a shouting contest on Market Day. Loudest call wins a free lunch. I'm training my lungs.
The tea house keeps a slate behind the counter of everyone who's ever beaten the owner at shogi. It's very short.

I dared someone to climb the water tower, and now their hat's up there and they're blaming me. Fair, actually.
Somebody keeps stealing my wind chimes. I think it's a monkey. Or a very tall neighbour.

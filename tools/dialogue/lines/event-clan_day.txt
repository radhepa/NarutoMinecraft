# Clan days: one per clan per year. Generic lines use {clan}; clan-tagged lines are said by that clan's members
# on their own day (ROADMAP §11). A clan line shows life inside the clan, never a secret.
#! tags: event=clan_day

## greet.event  fn=react
Good day to you! It's the {clan} clan's day. The whole quarter's dressed up.
Have you heard? The {clan} are holding their clan day, and the gates are open.
A clan day today. The {clan} put on a good one, I hear.
Morning, {player}. Banners are up for the {clan}. Lovely colours.

## chat.event  fn=worldbuild
Every clan has a day of its own, spread through the year so no two fall in the same week.
Clan days are when the clans open their gates. Even civilians get a peek inside.
The minor clans share a day of their own, out in the neighbourhoods. Tables in the street, everyone's invited.
On a clan day, the clan's elders sit in the gateway and greet everyone who passes.
The {clan} clan's day is a good one to visit. They feed anyone who turns up polite.
I keep a list of the clan days on my wall. My calendar is nothing but feasts and ceremonies.

## chat.event  group=civilian  fn=reveal_character
I'm not from any clan, but I go to every clan day. It's like visiting a different village for an afternoon.

# -- Akimichi: the harvest feast (autumn) --

## greet.event  clan=akimichi  fn=react,worldbuild
Harvest feast today! Come, come, there's a seat at the long table for you. There always is.

## chat.event  clan=akimichi  fn=worldbuild
The harvest feast runs from noon until the last pot is scraped. Some years that's midnight.
On feast day the Akimichi kitchen cooks for the whole village. We set out more bowls than we have family.
Every family brings one dish to the harvest feast. My grandmother's pickled radish is the first thing gone.
We say a thank you to the fields before we eat. Then we eat a lot.

# -- Inuzuka: the ninken pairing --

## greet.event  clan=inuzuka  fn=react,worldbuild
Pairing day! The pups are washed, the children are nervous, and the whole kennel is howling.

## chat.event  clan=inuzuka  fn=worldbuild
On pairing day, our children meet their first pups. The pup chooses, never the child.
The kennels are scrubbed for a week before pairing day. You could eat off the floor. Don't.
My partner chose me on pairing day when we were both tiny. We've not been apart a day since.
Pairing day ends with a run round the outer fields, every child and every pup together.

# -- Nara: the deer count --

## greet.event  clan=nara  fn=react,worldbuild
Deer count today. We walk the forest from dawn, so forgive me if I yawn at you.

## chat.event  clan=nara  fn=worldbuild
Once a year we count the deer in the clan forest. Every fawn gets a name in the book.
The deer count is slow. You sit, you wait, they come to you. Rushing only scares them off.
We bring the deer a little rice cake on count day. They know the date better than we do.
The antlers the stags shed are gathered on count day, for the clan's medicine work.

# -- Yamanaka: the flower festival (spring) --

## greet.event  clan=yamanaka  fn=react,worldbuild
The flower festival! The shop's front is one great wall of blossom. Come and see.

## chat.event  clan=yamanaka  fn=worldbuild
For the flower festival we fill the main street with pots from the greenhouse. It takes all night.
Every guest at the flower festival leaves with one stem. We choose it for them by how they look.
The greenhouse opens to the public only once a year, on our festival day.
My first arrangement was in the flower festival when I was six. Lopsided. My father kept it anyway.

# -- Uchiha: the police parade --

## greet.event  clan=uchiha  fn=react,worldbuild
The police parade's today. Uniforms pressed, crests polished, and my boots are pinching.

## chat.event  clan=uchiha  fn=worldbuild
The Military Police parade from the compound to the Residence once a year. The children line the street.
The parade is mostly marching, then the captains bow to the Hokage. It's very solemn, then we eat.
Every officer polishes the fan crest on their jacket the night before. We inspect each other.
The parade goes past the market, and the stallkeepers throw paper flowers. It's a nice custom.

# -- Aburame: insect night with fireflies (summer) --

## greet.event  clan=aburame  fn=react,worldbuild
Insect night. Come to the gardens after dark. Stay quiet, and you will see.

## chat.event  clan=aburame  fn=worldbuild
On insect night the fireflies rise over our gardens. Thousands. We have tended them for a year.
Guests are asked not to carry lanterns on insect night. The fireflies give all the light anyone needs.
Children always try to catch the fireflies. We let them, gently, then they let them go.
Insect night is very quiet. Perhaps that is why so few come. Those who come, come back.

# -- Yuhi: the grove viewing (spring) --

## greet.event  clan=yuhi  fn=react,worldbuild
Grove viewing today. The old trees are in flower, and the grove's open to everyone.

## chat.event  clan=yuhi  fn=worldbuild
Our grove is the oldest stand of trees in the village. On viewing day, people sit under them and say nothing.
We sweep the grove paths at dawn and lay mats under the blossoms. Bring tea, if you come.
The grove looks different every hour of viewing day. At dusk, everyone stays to watch the light change.

# -- Gekko: the moon-viewing duels (autumn full moon) --

## greet.event  clan=gekko  fn=react,worldbuild
Moon-viewing duels tonight. The platform's swept and the blades are polished.

## chat.event  clan=gekko  fn=worldbuild
The duels are fought on the moon-viewing platform, by moonlight only. No lanterns.
A moon duel ends at the first touch. It's about grace, not winning. Though winning is nice.
Guests sit on the slope below the dojo and watch. The swords flash silver in the moonlight.
We drink one cup of tea to the moon before the first duel. Then the dojo goes silent.

# -- Sarutobi: the lantern walk (New Year eve) --

## greet.event  clan=sarutobi  fn=react,worldbuild
The lantern walk is tonight. Find a lantern and join us at the Residence.

## chat.event  clan=sarutobi  fn=worldbuild
We walk from our house to the Hokage Rock with lit lanterns. Anyone can join behind us.
The lantern walk is about passing the flame along. Each lantern is lit from the one before it.
By the time the walk reaches the Rock, there are hundreds of lanterns. You can see them from the wall.

# -- Senju and Uzumaki: the founders' memorial (spring) --

## greet.event  clan=senju|uzumaki  fn=react,worldbuild
The founders' memorial is today, at the shrine. It's a quiet day for us.

## chat.event  clan=senju  fn=worldbuild
At the founders' memorial we lay a branch from the living tree on the shrine altar.
The memorial is short. A few words, a bow, and the whole village remembers who built it.

## chat.event  clan=uzumaki  fn=worldbuild
At the memorial we read the names aloud, every one. It takes a long time. Nobody minds.
We stand beside the Senju at the memorial. Old allies, old friends. That has never changed.

# -- Kurama: the painting exhibition --

## chat.event  clan=kurama  fn=worldbuild
Our studio opens for the exhibition today. Every painting on the walls was finished this year.
Visitors always ask how long a painting takes. The honest answer is: longer than you think.

# -- The minor clans' shared day --

## chat.event  group=minor  fn=worldbuild
On our shared day the minor clans set tables down the neighbourhood streets. Anyone can sit anywhere.
Each family brings out its trade on the shared day. Herbs, carpentry, metalwork. It's half fair, half feast.

## chat.event  clan=hagane  fn=worldbuild
On the shared day we open the forge doors and let children try a hammer on cold iron. Gently.

## chat.event  clan=kinoshita  fn=worldbuild
For the shared day our yard builds one new bench for the street. We've done it so long the street is all benches.

## chat.event  clan=suzuran  fn=worldbuild
On the shared day we give away little bags of dried herbs. For sleep, for colds, for tea.

## chat.event  clan=umino  fn=worldbuild
On the shared day the teachers' houses on the Academy streets open their doors for anyone with questions.

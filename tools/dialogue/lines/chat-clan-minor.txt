# Small talk from the ten minor clans: families without walls, living among civilians and clanless shinobi.

# -- Shiranui ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=shiranui  fn=worldbuild
Our house is the one by the gate barracks with the target wall out front. The wall has more holes than wall by now.
Every Shiranui learns senbon before chopsticks. My little brother eats noodles by spearing them one at a time.
We serve in the Hokage's guard, mostly. It's quiet work: a lot of standing and a little of being very fast.
Clan day is a throwing contest by the barracks. The guard comes to watch, and the Hagane bet on the outcome.
Yes, we keep a senbon in our mouths. No, it isn't sharp at that end. Everybody asks.
The Namiashi and we share the guard rotation, and we'll never agree on who's better at it. Keeps both houses sharp.

## chat.clan  clan=shiranui  fn=reveal_character
I smile easy and worry hard. Somebody in the guard has to look calm, and it may as well be me.
I can hit a falling leaf with a senbon at twenty paces. Parties, you'd think I'd be popular. Mostly people back away.
I nap standing up. Fourteen years of guard duty will do that.
My father taught me one thing about guarding: the dull hour is the dangerous one.

## chat.clan  clan=shiranui  fn=hook
Somebody keeps moving the targets on our wall a hand's width to the left every night. My aim's fine; my patience isn't.

# -- Morino --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=morino  fn=worldbuild
Our house is near the police station, with bars on the windows. The bars are for the neighbours' peace of mind, not ours.
Morino work as interrogators. People cross the street when we pass in our greatcoats. Then they come to us for advice on their landlords.
At home we're gentle. My grandmother grows violets on the windowsill behind the bars and talks to them every morning.
The police and we disagree over who holds a prisoner and for how long. We argue over it at tea. The tea is very polite.
Clan day is a long dinner with no questions at the table. That's the rule: one night a year, nobody asks anybody anything.
Every Morino coat has the same deep inner pocket. Children fill it with sweets before they're old enough to fill it with notes.

## chat.clan  clan=morino  fn=reveal_character
I can tell when someone's lying by how they hold a cup. It ruins dinner parties.
The village thinks we're frightening. I think honesty is frightening, and we just don't look away from it.
I keep canaries. Three of them. They sing whenever I come home, and it's the best part of my day.
I've never raised my voice in an interview. Quiet is far more effective. It works on my children too.

## chat.clan  clan=morino  fn=hook
A woman from the river district keeps coming to our gate asking for news of her brother. We've no word, but she should hear that from someone kind.

# -- Umino ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=umino  fn=worldbuild
Ask any shopkeeper on the Academy streets who taught them to write, and nine times in ten the answer is an Umino.
Our houses are on the Academy streets, so close you can hear the morning roll call from our kitchens.
Clan day is teachers' day. Former pupils leave notes on our doorsteps, and some of them are very rude about our handwriting.
We tie our hair back with a brush cord, every one of us. It started as a joke and became a family mark.
There's always a pile of marking on an Umino table. We eat around it.
Umino don't take holidays in exam week. We don't take holidays much at all, if I'm honest.

## chat.clan  clan=umino  fn=reveal_character
I can hear a pencil stop moving from across a classroom. It means the child is either stuck or about to throw it.
I keep a box of every note a pupil has ever given me. The box is three boxes now.
I'm stretched thin. Everyone's children are my children for four hours a day, and then they're mine again in my dreams.
Patience isn't a gift. It's a muscle, and mine is very tired on Fifth Days.

## chat.clan  clan=umino  fn=hook
One of the pupils has stopped coming to class. The family hasn't said why, and the child's desk is very tidy now.

# -- Haruno --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=haruno  fn=worldbuild
Our shop is on the market street, the one with the pink awning. You can't miss it, and believe me, plenty of people have tried.
The Haruno sold dry goods for four generations, and then the children started becoming ninja. The shop survives anyway.
Every Haruno works the counter at least one Market Day a month, ninja or not. Even the Chunin.
Our garden is small and very neat. The cherry tree in it was planted the year the shop first opened.
We buy shelves and crates from the Kinoshita yard every season. They complain about our prices; we complain about their splinters.
The anniversary sale is our clan day, more or less. The whole family wears pink aprons and the street eats free sweets.

## chat.clan  clan=haruno  fn=reveal_character
I can count change faster than most shinobi can throw a kunai. Different skill, same reflexes.
I love the shop and I love the field, and I've never been able to choose. So I do both, badly, and happily.
I keep the books in a pink ledger. It's not a family rule; I just think numbers deserve a nice home.
Bargaining is a conversation. The best customers are the ones who haggle back and leave smiling.

## chat.clan  clan=haruno  fn=commerce
Haruno prices are fair and we'll tell you so twice. Rice crackers two ryo, tea tins ten, and the cushions are on sale this week.

# -- Namiashi ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=namiashi  fn=worldbuild
Our dojo near the training grounds is small, but the floor is polished like water. That's on purpose.
They call us wave-foot. Every Namiashi learns to cross the river stepping stones without a splash before they can hold a sword.
We serve in the Hokage's guard beside the Shiranui and the Gekko. Three families, three styles, and one long argument.
The Gekko say they move like moonlight. We say moonlight doesn't move at all, it just sits there looking pale.
Clan day is a footwork contest on the river stones. Whoever splashes first serves dinner.
Barefoot in the dojo, sandals on the street, and never shoes. Shoes make you forget where the ground is.

## chat.clan  clan=namiashi  fn=reveal_character
I can't walk across a room without counting my steps. It's a family habit, and it makes me very punctual.
I fell in the river eleven times before I crossed it dry. My grandfather made me count every one out loud.
I'm quiet, I know. Swordsmen of my family speak with their feet.
I envy the Gekko their moon platform. I'd never tell them. Please don't.

## chat.clan  clan=namiashi  fn=hook
Somebody has shifted one of the river stones our children train on. Just a little, just enough to make the youngest slip.

# -- Hagane --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=hagane  fn=worldbuild
Hagane guard the gate and run the messages. If anything happens in the Leaf, we've heard about it by lunch.
Every Hagane name means some kind of metal. My cousins are Iron, Copper and a very unlucky one named after tin.
Our family shop is the metalworker's beside the forge on the market street. Hinges, locks, gate fittings, the solid stuff.
The gate's boring for weeks and then it isn't for an hour. A Hagane learns to be ready for that hour.
The Tobari take the wall at night and we take it by day. We leave each other notes in the gate booth, mostly about the kettle.
A bandaged nose is practically our family crest. Doors, maces, the odd cart. We bump into things at speed.

## chat.clan  clan=hagane  fn=reveal_character
I can't keep a secret, so nobody tells me any. It's very freeing.
I carry a mace because my grandmother did. It's heavy, ugly and I wouldn't trade it for any blade in the Leaf.
My favourite sound is the gate bell at dawn. Means everyone made it through the night.
I know every carter by the sound of their wheels. That one squeaks, that one wobbles, that one owes me a peach.

## chat.clan  clan=hagane  fn=hook
A message on the relay came back with the seal broken. Nobody on the route admits to dropping it, and I've asked everybody twice.

# -- Kinoshita -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=kinoshita  fn=worldbuild
Our yard on the carpenters' lane raised half the houses in the Leaf and rebuilt the other half after storms.
Every piece of furniture in the furniture shop comes from our benches. If it wobbles, it isn't ours.
The yard opens with the sound of six planes on cedar at dawn. The neighbours set their clocks by it.
Clan day is a roof-raising. We pick a house that needs a new roof and the whole family puts one on in a day.
The Haruno buy our crates and shelves every season. We grumble about their bargaining; they grumble about splinters.
A Kinoshita child's first piece is a stool. Mine still stands in the yard, and nobody's allowed to sit on it.

## chat.clan  clan=kinoshita  fn=reveal_character
I can tell what wood a beam is by tapping it with a knuckle. My knuckles are mostly scar.
I hate nails. A joint that needs a nail is a joint that wasn't cut right.
I build furniture for other people's homes and my own flat is three crates and a cushion. Isn't that always the way?
I like the smell of sawdust better than flowers. My spouse buys me cedar shavings for birthdays.

## chat.clan  clan=kinoshita  fn=hook
A cheap crew from outside the lane is underbidding us on every roof. Their joints won't last a winter, and someone will get hurt.

# -- Tsuchigumo ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=tsuchigumo  fn=worldbuild
Our stone houses are at the quarry edge of the village. Stone stays cool in summer and stubborn in winter, like us.
The shrine up the slope is ours. The shimenawa ropes over its gate are renewed every year, and the knots are older than the Leaf.
The spider on our cloth is a guardian, not a threat. Visitors flinch at it; children of the family pat it for luck.
We live in the Leaf and we keep our own counsel. That isn't coldness. A mountain doesn't explain itself either.
Offerings at our shrine are simple: rice, salt and a stone from the quarry. Choose the stone carefully.
Clan day is the rope renewal. Every member twists one strand, so the rope has all of us in it.

## chat.clan  clan=tsuchigumo  fn=reveal_character
I can split a slab along its grain with one tap. It's the only party trick I have, and I'm proud of it.
I came down from the shrine to live in the village when I was twelve. It still feels too loud, and I still like it.
I don't talk about the shrine. That isn't secrecy; it's just that some things lose something when you describe them.
The quarry dust gets into everything. My tea tastes of stone and I've grown to like it.

## chat.clan  clan=tsuchigumo  fn=hook
Somebody left the wrong offering at the shrine gate: a coin and a scrap of cloth. The elders are uneasy, and they don't say why lightly.

# -- Suzuran -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=suzuran  fn=worldbuild
Our houses are beside the hospital herb gardens. The drying racks run along every fence, and the whole street smells like tea.
Lily of the valley is our flower. It's beautiful and every part of it is poison. People find that a little too fitting.
Suzuran can drink what would make you very ill. We were raised on bitter brews, which explains our faces.
The hospital buys our salves and the Yamanaka buy our cuttings. Then they undercut us at the market. We sigh and carry on.
Clan day, we brew the year's first tonic and everyone in the family takes one cup. Nobody enjoys it.
A Suzuran apprentice spends a year naming plants before touching a pestle. The poisonous ones are named twice.

## chat.clan  clan=suzuran  fn=reveal_character
I've tasted every plant in the garden, including the ones on the second shelf. My grandmother says that's why I'm so calm.
I'm a healer who knows ten ways to stop a heart. The two go together more than people like to think.
I love the drying room at night. Everything smells of summer, even in the snow.
I've never been ill a day in my life. I take no credit; I've just been mildly poisoned for thirty years.

## chat.clan  clan=suzuran  fn=hook
The hospital's run short on fever root, and the clan's patch by the river was dug up. Not picked. Dug, roots and all.

# -- Tobari --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=tobari  fn=worldbuild
Our houses are on the dark lanes behind the wall, shuttered and padded. You can shout in our parlour and the street won't hear.
Tobari keep the night watch and the lanterns. If a lamp's lit on the wall after dark, one of us lit it.
We walk softly. Every Tobari child learns to cross a floor of dry leaves without a sound before they learn to run.
The Hagane take the day and we take the night. Their kettle is never where we left it.
Clan day is the lantern-keepers' round. Every lantern in the village is cleaned, trimmed and relit by one of us in a single night.
We wear mufflers all year round. Partly for the cold, mostly for the quiet.

## chat.clan  clan=tobari  fn=reveal_character
I sleep in the afternoons with a pillow over my head. The village is very loud when you're used to the night.
Nobody thanks the night watch. That's fine. The thanks is waking up and finding the village still there.
I can hear a moth land. It's not a technique, just years of listening.
I've seen more sunrises than anyone I know, and I've never once been awake for a breakfast.

## chat.clan  clan=tobari  fn=hook
Three lanterns along the north wall keep going out before midnight. The wicks are trimmed, the oil's full, and still.

# -- more from each family -------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## chat.clan  clan=shiranui  fn=worldbuild
Our family keeps its senbon in bamboo cases, one per member, hung by the door. Count the cases and you know who's home.
The gate barracks cook feeds us for free on festival nights. We guard the door; it's a fair trade.

## chat.clan  clan=morino  fn=worldbuild
The bars on our windows were put up by a great-grandfather nobody remembers liking. We've kept them; the violets climb them nicely.
A Morino greatcoat is passed down, not bought. Mine has three generations of mending in the lining.

## chat.clan  clan=umino  fn=worldbuild
Every Umino house has a slate by the door. Visitors write their name; we correct the spelling. We can't help it.
The family meets for dinner on the last day of term. Twelve teachers at one table, and nobody is allowed to mention a pupil.

## chat.clan  clan=haruno  fn=worldbuild
The shop bell is the one my great-grandmother hung on the first day. It's cracked, and we'd never replace it.
Haruno children grow up stacking crates. That's why so many of us made Genin; our arms were ready before our jutsu.

## chat.clan  clan=namiashi  fn=worldbuild
The dojo floor is oiled every new moon. The whole family slides about in socks for a day afterwards, pretending it's training.
Our swords are short and light. A Namiashi blade follows the feet, never the other way round.

## chat.clan  clan=hagane  fn=worldbuild
Clan day, every Hagane hammers one nail into the beam over the shop door. The beam's more iron than wood by now.
We swap gate gossip at dinner like other families swap recipes. My grandfather keeps score of who heard it first.

## chat.clan  clan=kinoshita  fn=worldbuild
The yard keeps a plank from every house we've built, labelled and stacked in the loft. The loft is groaning.
Kinoshita apprentices sweep the shavings for a year before they touch a saw. The yard is very clean and the apprentices are very bored.

## chat.clan  clan=tsuchigumo  fn=worldbuild
Our houses have no wood in the walls at all. A fire can't touch us, and a draught can't find a way in.
The quarry bell rings at noon so the cutters stop for rice. The family eats on the same flat stone it always has.

## chat.clan  clan=suzuran  fn=worldbuild
Our kitchen and our workroom share a wall, and the jars are labelled very clearly. Visitors still check before they take a biscuit.
Suzuran gardens are planted by the moon. Roots at new moon, leaves at full, and seeds whenever the old folks stop arguing.

## chat.clan  clan=tobari  fn=worldbuild
Our breakfast is at sunset. Guests who come at dawn find the family yawning over bowls of soup.
The lantern-keepers' oil is stored in our cellar. The whole lane smells faintly of it, and nobody here notices anymore.

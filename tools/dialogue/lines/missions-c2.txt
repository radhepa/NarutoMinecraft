# -- escort_traveller: a scholar through the hills. Slots: client (scholar), pass, camp, village, healer

## mission.c.escort_traveller.title
Escort a Scholar to the Border

## mission.c.escort_traveller.brief  job=mission_desk  fn=quest,guide
C-rank, two days. Escort {client}, a scholar, through the hills to {village}. The road goes over {pass}. {pay}.

## mission.c.escort_traveller.brief  fn=quest,guide
I have business in the border village. Books to see, people to ask. I've never walked the hills before.
Two days, they tell me. Over {pass}, a night at {camp}, then down to {village}. How hard can it be?

## mission.c.escort_traveller.accept
Splendid. I've packed three books and one spare sock. Is that enough?
Thank you. I promise to complain only about the important things.

## mission.c.escort_traveller.decline
Then I'll wait for the next squad. My research has waited this long.
No? I'll read about the hills instead. Much less walking.

## mission.c.escort_traveller.progress
Is it always this steep? Don't answer that.
I've filled four pages with notes already. The wildflowers here are extraordinary.

## mission.c.escort_traveller.success
We're here. I can't feel my feet, but we're here. Thank you, truly.
Safe in the border village, and with my books dry. You're a credit to the Leaf.

## mission.c.escort_traveller.fail
I'll go home. The hills won this time. Thank you for getting me back.
It didn't work out. I'm not cut out for mountains, it seems.

## mission.c.escort_traveller.report  job=mission_desk
Scholar delivered safely. {client} wrote a whole page about how brave you were. It's quite flowery. Logged.
C-rank, done. A client who arrived healthier than they left. That's good escort work.

## mission.c.escort_traveller.twist.sick_on_road  fn=quest,react
{client} is pale and sweating at the pass, sitting down every ten steps. The hills have got to them.

## mission.c.escort_traveller.twist.client_secret  fn=quest,react
By the fire, {client} goes quiet, then admits the real reason for the trip. Harmless, and private. "Please don't tell anyone."

## mission.c.escort_traveller.choice.choice
{client} asked you to keep the secret. Keep it, or put it in the mission report?

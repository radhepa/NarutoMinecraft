# Mission banter: mission.banter.<moment>.<speaker_kind>, for any board mission. Speakers are
# sensei (the squad's Jonin), teammate (a squadmate, a child in the beta) and client (whoever hired
# the squad). Generic voices until the cast is written. Squad-cohesion variants are in
# missions-banter-bands.txt.
#! kind: cycle

# -- brief: hearing the job ------------------------------------------------------------------------

## mission.banter.brief.sensei  fn=react,teach
Listen to the whole brief before you ask anything. Half your questions answer themselves.
Read the job, then read it again for what it doesn't say. That's where the trouble lives.
Every mission has a client. The client is the mission. Remember that when you get bored.
Small jobs teach the habits that keep you alive on big ones. Take this seriously.
Questions now, not halfway there. Out on the road, I'd rather you were listening.

## mission.banter.brief.teammate  fn=react,deepen_bond  stage=child|teen
That's it? That's the whole mission? I was hoping for something with a scroll in it.
I'm writing it down. Don't look at me like that, I forget things.
Did they say "outside the wall"? Did they actually say that? Yes!
Who gets to carry the map? Not you. You held it upside down last time.
Fine. It's not glamorous. But we're doing it properly. Deal?

## mission.banter.brief.client  fn=react
You're younger than I expected. No, no, I'm sure you're very good.
I've written it all down for you. Twice, in case you lose one.
Is it always this formal? I thought I'd just ask and you'd come.
Please don't think it's silly. It isn't silly to me.
I can pay. It's all here, counted. I just want it done right.

# -- depart: leaving -------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## mission.banter.depart.sensei  fn=react,teach
Last check. Water, wire, rations, bandages. If it isn't on you now, it isn't coming.
Gate in five minutes. Anyone who needs the latrine, this is your moment.
We leave together and we come back together. That's not a rule. It's a promise.
Packs tight, straps short. A rattle is a noise, and noise is a mistake.
Wave goodbye to the gate guards. They count us out and they like to count us in.

## mission.banter.depart.teammate  fn=react,deepen_bond  stage=child|teen
I forgot something. I don't know what. I just have the feeling.
My stomach's doing a flip. The good kind. Mostly.
Look, the gate guard's waving at us. We're a real squad now.
Let's make a promise: nobody does anything stupid. I'll start breaking it in an hour.
Bet I can spot the first milestone before you.

## mission.banter.depart.client  fn=react
Is everyone ready? I've been ready since dawn.
I said goodbye to my family like I was going to war. Silly, isn't it?
Walk on my left, if you would. I'm deaf in the right ear.
I've packed snacks. For all of you. Don't argue.
Right. Off we go. I'll try not to slow you down.

# -- travel: on the road ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

## mission.banter.travel.sensei  fn=react,teach
Walk where the ground is hard. Soft ground keeps your footprints and your secrets.
Breathe through your nose. You'll go twice as far before your legs complain.
If you're thirsty, you waited too long. Drink a little, often.
Notice the birds. When they go quiet, something is moving.
We'll stop when I say. Not when your feet say. Your feet are liars.

## mission.banter.travel.teammate  fn=react,deepen_bond  stage=child|teen
Want to play a game? First one to see a fox gets the last rice ball.
My sandal strap's rubbing. No, keep going. It's fine. It's not fine.
That cloud looks like the Hokage Rock. Sort of. If you squint.
Tell me something about you I don't know. We've got a long way to go.
I'm not tired. I'm walking slower to save energy. There's a difference.

## mission.banter.travel.client  fn=react
I never knew the country was this green so close to home.
You don't talk much on the road, do you? I'll talk enough for everyone.
My knees are telling me about every hill. In detail.
Do ninja ever just walk? Without watching every tree?
There used to be a tea stall around this bend. Gone now. Shame.

# -- arrive: reaching the job ----------------------------------------------------------------------

## mission.banter.arrive.sensei  fn=react,teach
Stop. Look before you walk in. Exits, people, anything out of place.
We're here. Now the real work starts. The walk was just the walk.
Find the client, confirm the job, then set out your plan. In that order.
First impressions count. Stand up straight, you represent the Leaf.
Note the time. It goes in the report, and it tells us how we paced.

## mission.banter.arrive.teammate  fn=react,deepen_bond  stage=child|teen
We're actually here! I thought that road would never end.
It looks smaller than I pictured. Everything does, close up.
Okay. Okay. What do we do first? Someone tell me what we do first.
I'm so hungry I could eat this fence. Not now. After. Right.
We made it, all of us. That has to count for something.

## mission.banter.arrive.client  fn=react
Here at last! You can't know how much I've worried about this.
Let me show you around. It won't take long. It's not a big place.
Thank you for coming all this way. Sit, rest, there's tea.
It looks worse than it did yesterday. I'm sure of it.
You came. I half thought nobody would.

# -- ambush: attacked ------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## mission.banter.ambush.sensei  fn=react,teach
Down! Guard the client. Count them before you count on anything.
Backs together. Don't chase anyone into the trees.
Calm. They chose the ground, so we choose the moment.
Watch the flanks. The loud one in front is meant to be watched.
Hold. Hold. Let them come to us.

## mission.banter.ambush.teammate  fn=react,deepen_bond  stage=child|teen

I knew that bush looked wrong. I knew it!
Stay close. I've got the left. Don't argue, I've got the left.

My hands are shaking. That's fine. They still throw.

## mission.banter.ambush.client  fn=react
What's happening? Who are they? What do I do?
I'll stay down! I'm very good at staying down!
Don't let them take the cart. Or me. Mostly me.
I knew this road was cursed. I said so. Didn't I say so?
Behind the wheel. I'm behind the wheel. Is that right?

## mission.banter.twist.sensei  fn=react,teach

Plans change. People who can't change with them get hurt. Think.
Take a breath. Then tell me what you see. Not what you expected. What you see.
A job is only simple until it isn't. Stay calm and adjust.
Nobody panics. We've just been given more information. Use it.

# -- fight -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

## mission.banter.fight.sensei  fn=react,teach
Feet first, hands second. A good stance wins more fights than a fast kunai.
Don't stare at the weapon. Watch their shoulders.
Keep moving. Standing still makes you a target.
Breathe out when you strike. Hold your breath and you'll tire twice as fast.
Finish what you start, then look for the next one.

## mission.banter.fight.teammate  fn=react,deepen_bond  stage=child|teen

## mission.banter.fight.client  fn=react

# -- choice: a decision point ----------------------------------------------------------------------

## mission.banter.choice.sensei  fn=react,teach
This one's yours to decide. I'll tell you what I think after, not before.
There's no rule for this. That's why it's hard.
Think about who it helps, who it hurts, and whether you can live with it.
Whatever you pick, own it in the report. Honestly.
Shinobi make calls like this every day. You'll make better ones for having made this one.

## mission.banter.choice.teammate  fn=react,deepen_bond  stage=child|teen

Take your time. No, actually, don't take too much time.

## mission.banter.choice.client  fn=react

# -- camp: a night out -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

## mission.banter.camp.sensei  fn=react,teach
Fire small, backs to the rock, kit where you can find it in the dark.
First watch is mine. Get your sleep while it's offered.
Dry your socks. Wet feet will end a mission faster than any bandit.
Tell me one thing you'd do differently today. Just one.
Bank the fire before you sleep. Embers by dawn, not ashes.

## mission.banter.camp.teammate  fn=react,deepen_bond  stage=child|teen

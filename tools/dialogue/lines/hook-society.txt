# Society hooks (BONDS.md §19): the village raises tensions, needs and chances, and a resident mentions one
# (.offer); after the player resolves it, someone involved reacts (.<outcome>). Romantic hooks and family
# estrangements wait for a teen or older player.

# -- a clan feud ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## hook.clan_feud.offer  fn=hook
There was a shoving match at the well between two clans' youngsters. The elders are making it worse. Someone should step in.
Two clans are glaring at each other across the market again. One more insult and it'll come to blows.
The {clan} and their neighbours are at it again. Over a boundary stone, if you can believe that. Someone needs to talk sense into them.
## hook.clan_feud.mediated  fn=react
You got both clans to sit at one table. I didn't think I'd live to see that.
The feud's cooled. Both sides are saying you were fair. That's rare, {player}.
## hook.clan_feud.sided_a  fn=react
You stood with one side. They won't forget it, and neither will the other.
Well, you picked a side. Half the quarter's cheering you, and half isn't.
## hook.clan_feud.sided_b  fn=react
You backed the other clan. It might have been right. It's made enemies, though.
You chose your side. The ones you didn't choose are already talking.

# -- a favour needed --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## hook.favor_needed.offer  fn=hook
{client} could use a hand, if you've a spare hour. They'd never ask for it themselves.
I heard {client} is struggling with something. Nothing dangerous. Just more than one pair of hands.
If you're looking for something useful to do, {client} needs help and won't say so.
## hook.favor_needed.done  fn=react
{client} hasn't stopped talking about how you helped. You've made a friend there.
That was good of you, helping {client}. It's the little things that hold a street together.
## hook.favor_needed.refused  fn=react
{client} managed in the end. Took them all week, but they managed.
No help, then. That's your choice. {client} noticed, though.

# -- a quarrel to mediate ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
## hook.quarrel_to_mediate.offer  fn=hook
{friend} and an old friend aren't speaking. Over something silly, I think. Maybe you could help.
Have you noticed {friend} sitting alone at lunch? There's been a quarrel. They're both too stubborn to fix it.
Two friends of mine have fallen out and it's driving me mad. One of them is {friend}. Could you try?
## hook.quarrel_to_mediate.mediated  fn=react
{friend} and their friend are laughing together again. Whatever you said, it worked.
You patched it up! I don't know how. Thank you, {player}.
## hook.quarrel_to_mediate.failed  fn=react
It didn't take, the peacemaking. They're still not speaking. You tried, at least.
Some quarrels won't be talked down. That one's still burning.

# -- a secret crush ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## hook.secret_crush.offer  fn=hook  stage=teen|adult  player_stage=teen|adult
Can you keep a secret? {friend} has a crush on someone and is too scared to say a word.
{friend} goes red every time a certain someone walks past the stall. It's painful to watch. Help them out?
I think {friend} has fallen for someone. Hard. They need a nudge, and I'm hopeless at nudging.
## hook.secret_crush.helped  fn=react  stage=teen|adult  player_stage=teen|adult
{friend} and their sweetheart walked past this morning holding hands. Your doing, I hear!
You helped {friend} speak up. They're beside themselves. Happily.
## hook.secret_crush.kept_quiet  fn=react  stage=teen|adult  player_stage=teen|adult
You kept {friend}'s secret. That's loyalty. Maybe they'll find the nerve themselves.
You didn't meddle. Probably wise. {friend}'s crush is still a secret, for now.
## hook.secret_crush.told  fn=react  stage=teen|adult  player_stage=teen|adult
You told everyone about {friend}'s crush? That was theirs to tell, {player}.
The whole street knows about {friend}'s crush now. They're mortified, and they know it was you.

# -- a lost item ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## hook.lost_item.offer  fn=hook
{neighbor} lost something precious on the way home. They've been retracing their steps all morning.
Keep your eyes open, would you? {neighbor} dropped a keepsake somewhere between the market and the river.
{neighbor} is beside themselves. Lost an heirloom, a little carved charm. If you find it...
## hook.lost_item.returned  fn=react
You found it and brought it back! {neighbor} cried, and then gave everyone on the street a sweet.
{neighbor} has the keepsake back, thanks to you. That's the kind of thing people remember.
## hook.lost_item.kept  fn=react
{neighbor} never got it back. And there's talk about where it ended up, {player}.
Someone saw you with {neighbor}'s keepsake. You kept it? That's not like the person I thought you were.

# -- a newcomer -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## hook.newcomer.offer  fn=hook
There's a new family on the street. They don't know anyone yet. You remember what that's like.
Someone new moved in by the well. Keeps to themselves. A friendly face would do wonders.
Have you met the newcomer? They sit alone at the tea house every afternoon. Go and say hello.
## hook.newcomer.befriended  fn=react
The newcomer told me you were the first to make them feel at home. Good on you.
You shared a meal with the new arrivals! They've been glowing about it all week.
## hook.newcomer.ignored  fn=react
The newcomer's still sitting alone. Nobody went over. Not even you.
Shame nobody welcomed the new family. They're thinking of moving on.

# -- an elder's request -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
## hook.elder_request.offer  fn=hook
One of the {clan} elders has a request for you. That's an honour. Don't keep them waiting.
An elder of the {clan} asked after you by name. They need something done the old way.
The {clan} elders want a task done, and they've decided you're the one to do it.
## hook.elder_request.done  fn=react
The {clan} elders are speaking well of you. That doesn't happen often.
You did what the elder asked, and did it properly. The whole {clan} quarter's heard.
## hook.elder_request.refused  fn=react
You turned down the {clan} elder? They'll remember that, {player}. Elders always do.
The elder found someone else. They weren't pleased you said no.

# -- a rivalry heating up ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
## hook.rivalry_heating.offer  fn=hook
{friend} and their rival are at each other's throats on the training ground. It's past friendly now.
Every spar between {friend} and that rival gets rougher. Someone's going to get hurt.
{friend} has a rival, and it's turning sour. Maybe you could cool them both down.
## hook.rivalry_heating.cooled  fn=react
{friend} and their rival shared dango after training! Friends again, it seems. Your doing?
The rivalry's back to the good kind. They push each other, not hurt each other.
## hook.rivalry_heating.fanned  fn=react
You egged them on, and now {friend} and their rival aren't speaking at all.
That rivalry's a full quarrel now. I hope it was worth the show, {player}.

# -- a forbidden match ------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## hook.forbidden_match.offer  fn=hook  stage=teen|adult  player_stage=teen|adult
Two people I know want to marry, but their clans won't hear of it. It's breaking their hearts.
{friend} wants to wed someone from a clan that won't allow it. They need someone on their side.
Their families have forbidden the match. You know them both. Could you speak up?
## hook.forbidden_match.supported  fn=react  stage=teen|adult  player_stage=teen|adult
They're to be married! The clans are grumbling, but they're married all the same. Thanks to you.
You stood by them, and now there's a wedding at the shrine. Some elders won't forgive it. They don't care.
## hook.forbidden_match.discouraged  fn=react  stage=teen|adult  player_stage=teen|adult
You told them to give each other up. Maybe it was sensible. It was very sad.
They've parted. The clans are satisfied. I'm not sure anyone else is.

# -- an estrangement --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
## hook.estrangement.offer  fn=hook  player_stage=teen|adult
Two relatives haven't spoken in a month. Family quarrels go deep. Someone outside could help.
{friend} hasn't spoken to their own family in weeks. They pretend it doesn't matter. It does.
There's a family on our street eating in two separate rooms. It can't go on like this.
## hook.estrangement.reconciled  fn=react  player_stage=teen|adult
They're speaking again! Both of them came to the tea house together. You did that.
The family's mended. It'll take time, but they're at the same table again.
## hook.estrangement.failed  fn=react  player_stage=teen|adult
It didn't mend. Some family wounds just won't close. Thank you for trying.
They're still apart. Maybe in time.
